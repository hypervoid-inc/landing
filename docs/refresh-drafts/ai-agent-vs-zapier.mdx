Construct is the Zapier Agents alternative for small teams. It gives you several agents that plan their own steps across your apps, a live browser, a sandbox terminal and shared workspace files, from $9 a month with 3 scheduled jobs on Lite. Zapier Agents count every action, web search and knowledge lookup as an activity: the free tier stops at 400 a month and Agents Pro at 1,500, and a Zapier Team account shares one pool. Classic Zaps go further back, running a fixed trigger-action recipe wired in advance.

For founders and small teams whose recurring work involves reading, judgment or research, Construct is the better choice. You describe the outcome once, the agents handle the cases nobody wired for, and every finished file stays in a workspace your team can open next week. Already on Zapier? Keep the simple Zaps and give Construct the jobs that keep falling through.

## Why choose Construct over Zapier Agents?

- Your work leaves files behind. Reports and spreadsheets are saved to workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29, and next week's run starts from this week's output.
- Agents reach sites with no integration. When a step needs a website or a script, Construct uses a live browser you can watch and take over, or a sandbox terminal, alongside its integration catalog and your own MCP tools.
- You pick the model. Construct runs on bundled models, and on Pro you bring your own keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Amazon Bedrock or xAI, as a fallback when bundled usage runs out or for every run.
- Memory you can correct. Construct remembers your qualification rules and report format between runs, and you can open, fix or forget any stored fact.
- Your team reaches it where it already talks: the web, Slack, Telegram, Discord and an agent email address.
- It costs $9 a month to start. Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task and 3 scheduled tasks. Starter at $59 raises that to 5 agents, 150 steps and 10 scheduled tasks.

## What are Zapier Agents?

Zapier Agents are Zapier's AI agent product. You create specialized agents, with help from Zapier Copilot, that connect to your business data and perform tasks across Zapier's 9,000+ apps, browse the web, and chat with you through a Chrome extension. ([Zapier Agents](https://zapier.com/agents))

Every agent action is metered as an activity. Zapier says activities "include actions your agent takes in behaviors or in chat, browsing the web, or looking up information from attached knowledge," and they are a separate meter from Zap tasks. ([Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing))

## Is Zapier Agents free? Pricing compared

Zapier Agents has a free tier with 400 activities a month. Agents Pro is $400 billed annually, about $33 a month, for 1,500 activities. Enterprise is custom. Because each action, web search and knowledge lookup counts, one instruction can use several activities, so a busy month can reach the cap well before 1,500 jobs are done.

## How a Zap works, and where agents come in

A Zap starts with a trigger, such as a new row, a new email or a new deal. Filter steps decide whether the run continues, and action steps carry out each task. Every step is wired in advance and runs in the order it was built. Zaps cap out at 100 steps including every step inside Paths, and they run in one direction. ([Zapier, Zap limits](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496181445261-Zap-limits))

A workflow that has to read a reply and decide among a few responses has to be pre-built as a tree of Paths and filters for every case its builder thought of, and anything outside that tree falls through. Zapier Agents and Construct both start from the other end: you give a goal and the agent works out the path. The difference is what the agent has to work with and what it leaves behind.

## Where agent-based automation is headed

The gap this closes shows up in how much unstructured coordination work still sits on people's calendars. Knowledge workers spend about 60% of their time on "work about work," communicating about tasks, hunting for information, and switching between apps, rather than on the skilled work itself, according to Asana's Anatomy of Work Index, based on a global survey of more than 10,000 knowledge workers ([Asana, Anatomy of Work Index](https://asana.com/resources/why-work-about-work-is-bad)). Much of that time goes to exactly the kind of judgment calls and cross-app handoffs a fixed trigger-action recipe cannot absorb.

Analyst research points the same direction. Gartner predicts that 40% of enterprise applications will feature task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, up from less than 5% in 2025. "AI agents are evolving rapidly, progressing from basic assistants embedded in enterprise applications today to task-specific agents by 2026 and ultimately multiagent ecosystems by 2029," said Anushree Verma, Sr Director Analyst at Gartner ([Gartner, press release](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-08-26-gartner-predicts-40-percent-of-enterprise-apps-will-feature-task-specific-ai-agents-by-2026-up-from-less-than-5-percent-in-2025)). Zapier's own move toward Agents and Human in the Loop steps fits the same pattern: even a platform built on fixed automations is adding room for a system to plan.

## Already using Zapier? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to turn off a single Zap or agent. Start with the one workflow that keeps needing a person to finish it.

- Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial if the job needs longer runs or your own model keys.
- Describe the outcome in a Construct chat. Paste in your agent's instructions or the steps from the Zap, and add the cases it currently drops.
- Connect the apps the job needs from the integration catalog, such as Gmail and your CRM.
- Run it once and check the result in Files and Activity.
- Save it as a workflow and put it on the Calendar so it runs server side with your laptop closed.

There is no import from Zapier, so the job description replaces the Zap or the agent setup. Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Zapier still fit?

- A high-volume, fully deterministic mapping with the same trigger and action every time. Zapier connects to more than 9,000 apps.
- A workflow that must fire within seconds of an event.
- A flow that needs built-in branching, delays or approval steps today.

For recurring work where the inputs vary, a step needs reading or research, or the job spans several apps and a website with no integration, Construct is the better fit.

## Which should you choose?

Choose Construct. It starts at $9 a month, gives your team several agents that plan their own steps, saves every result to files your team can open, and does not stop at a monthly activity cap when you bring your own keys. If you already run Zaps, keep them for simple mappings and let Construct take the jobs that need judgment.

Pick the workflow that most often ends with someone finishing it by hand, such as qualifying inbound leads or summarizing a weekly inbox, and give it to Construct with the rules you already use. Run it once, check the saved result, then put it on the Calendar. Start on Lite at $9, or try Pro free for seven days.
