Construct gives a small team an AI employee with its own cloud workspace, files, Calendar schedules, and email address from $9 a month, with no per-seat fee. Most paid alternatives on this page start at about $20 to $50 a month, several charge per person, and many meter usage in credits that vary by task.

For most founders and small teams, Construct is the better choice. Switching away means giving up one shared workspace with no member limit, finished files that the next run builds on, memory you can read and correct, and on Pro, the freedom to run on your own model keys. If you are weighing an alternative because of one missing piece, read "Before you switch, try this" below first: several common reasons have a setup fix inside Construct.

## Why start with, or stay on, Construct?

- **$9 a month for the whole workspace, not per person.** Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Lindy is $29.99 per user, and Microsoft Copilot Cowork needs a $25.20 per-user license before usage.
- **One workspace for the team, with no member limit.** Invite teammates as Owner, Admin, or Member. Everyone's work draws on the owner's plan.
- **Work that outlives the run.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still keeps steps 1 to 29, and next week's run starts from this week's file.
- **Memory you can inspect, correct, and forget.** When a client changes its format, you fix the stored fact once and every later job uses it.
- **Your model, or your own keys.** Pro supports your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, and xAI, so your files and procedures do not depend on one AI lab.
- **It answers where your team already talks.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and its own email inbox, with Calendar schedules that run server-side while your laptop is closed.

## What is Construct, briefly?

Construct is an AI employee for solo founders and small teams. Each agent works from a persistent cloud workspace: files, a browser, a sandbox terminal, its own email address, a Calendar of scheduled jobs, and connected apps through a live integration catalog that includes Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable, plus custom MCP tools. It can also build private workspace apps, such as a review queue or a tracker, for work that several people check.

Plans, from the [pricing page](/pricing/):

- **Lite, $9 a month:** up to 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address.
- **Starter, $59 a month:** 6x Lite usage, up to 5 agents, 150 steps per task, and 10 scheduled tasks.
- **Pro, $299 a month:** 32x Lite usage, up to 15 agents, up to 1,000 steps per task, 50 scheduled tasks, and bring-your-own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, and xAI. Pro has a 7-day free trial.

## What each alternative costs you by comparison

### Lindy

Lindy is an AI employee that "lives in your Slack, connects to your tools, and comes back with the work done" ([Lindy docs](https://docs.lindy.ai/)). Anything with outside impact waits for approval, and it works in Slack, over iMessage, and in up to 5 email inboxes ([Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/)).

**Price:** Plus is $29.99 per user a month with 3,000 credits, Pro $99.99 with 15,000, and Max $199.99 with 35,000 ([Lindy pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing)). When credits run out, "Lindy pauses credit-using actions" until the next cycle ([Lindy plan documentation](https://docs.lindy.ai/pricing)). Lindy says it is "SOC 2 and GDPR compliant," and Enterprise adds HIPAA with a signed BAA, audit logs, and SSO.

**What you give up by switching:** a $9 workspace price that does not grow with each teammate, a workspace of files the next run builds on, and bring-your-own keys on Pro. Our [Construct vs Lindy](/blog/construct-vs-lindy/) and [Lindy alternatives](/blog/lindy-alternatives/) go deeper.

### Viktor

Viktor calls itself "the AI employee that lives in Slack and Microsoft Teams," with more than 3,200 integrations, recurring tasks, and confirmation before high-stakes actions ([Viktor](https://viktor.com/)).

**Price:** new workspaces get "$100 in credits" with no card; paid plans start at $50 a month for 20,000 credits, per workspace ([Viktor pricing](https://viktor.com/pricing)).

**What you give up by switching:** a $9 entry, Telegram, Discord, and an agent email inbox as channels (Viktor's pages describe Slack and Teams), and a Calendar and memory you can open and correct. See [Construct vs Viktor](/blog/construct-vs-viktor/).

### ChatGPT dots

OpenAI launched dots on September 29, 2026: always-on agents with their own cloud computers, reachable in ChatGPT, Slack, and Teams ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)).

**Price:** your first dot comes with ChatGPT Pro or Business Premium. [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2272332/openai-devday-2026-announcements/) says dots are "only available on ChatGPT's Pro 200 and Business Premium plans," while The Next Web lists Pro generally. Business Premium is reported at $125 per seat monthly ([eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/chatgpt-pricing)); OpenAI's pricing page blocked our fetch. Pro users in the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK are excluded for now.

**What you give up by switching:** at least $91 a month compared with the cheapest reported Pro tier, a choice of model provider through your own keys, and a team workspace that does not depend on one AI lab's plan. See [Construct vs ChatGPT Dots](/blog/construct-vs-openai-dots/).

### Claude with Cowork

Cowork lets you "describe an outcome, step away, and come back to finished work" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)). Scheduled tasks are on every paid plan and run remotely, but "if a scheduled task requires local files or apps, it will only run locally" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)).

**Price:** Pro is $20 a month ($17 billed annually), Max starts at $100, and Team Standard is $25 per seat ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)).

**What you give up by switching:** a workspace your whole team shares without a seat price, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and email channels, and an agent email address, which is not documented in the Cowork pages we read. If you already pay for Claude, keep it for your own desk work and give Construct the recurring team jobs. See [Construct vs Claude Cowork](/blog/construct-vs-claude-cowork/).

### Gemini Spark

Google calls Spark "your 24/7 personal AI agent," with native connections to Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, and Sheets ([Gemini Spark](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/)).

**Price:** included with Google AI Pro at $19.99 a month or Ultra from $99.99 ([Google](https://gemini.google/us/subscriptions/?hl=en)). Google says it "isn't available if you sign in with a work or school Google Account," and it is not offered in the EEA, Nigeria, Switzerland, or the UK ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)).

**What you give up by switching:** work accounts. Construct connects Google Workspace Gmail and Google Calendar through its integration catalog, and the work is shared by a team rather than tied to one person's account. See [Gemini Spark alternatives](/blog/gemini-spark-alternatives/).

### Microsoft Copilot Cowork

Microsoft says Copilot Cowork runs long, multi-tool tasks in the cloud, inside your Microsoft 365 trust boundary ([Microsoft 365 blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/06/16/copilot-cowork-is-now-generally-available/)).

**Price:** a Microsoft 365 Copilot license at $25.20 per user on monthly billing, or from $18 on an annual subscription, plus usage at "$0.01 per Copilot Credit" on pay-as-you-go ([Microsoft 365 Copilot pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing)).

**What you give up by switching:** a fixed monthly price for the whole team, and one agent for work that crosses Notion, Linear, HubSpot, GitHub, and the open web. See [Construct vs Copilot](/blog/construct-vs-copilot/).

### Manus

Manus runs each task in a sandbox and sells an optional Cloud Computer, a persistent server that can keep services running between sessions ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).

**Price:** Free gives 300 credits a day, 1 concurrent task, and 2 scheduled tasks. Pro starts at $20 a month for 4,000 credits and 20 scheduled tasks. The Cloud Computer is $30 or $50 a month extra ([Manus pricing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)). If you stop paying for the Cloud Computer, its files and databases are deleted ([Cloud Computer billing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).

**What you give up by switching:** a plan price you can budget instead of per-task credits, workspace files on every plan with no server add-on, a shared team workspace, and an agent email inbox. See [Manus alternatives](/blog/manus-alternatives/).

### Meta Muse

Meta says Muse keeps working after people close the app and works directly in WhatsApp ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)). Muse for Small Business connects to Slack, Shopify, Stripe, and Asana, and is available in the US and Canada ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/)).

**Price:** free with a usage limit, Power at $20 a month, and Maximum at $100 ([Meta Help Center](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)).

**What you give up by switching:** one agent for work outside Meta's apps, a shared team workspace, and Slack, Telegram, and Discord channels. See [Muse for Small Business alternatives](/blog/muse-for-small-business-alternatives/).

### Simular Sai

Simular says "Sai runs on your device, and autonomous computers in the cloud," working across browsers and native applications ([Simular](https://www.simular.ai/)).

**Price:** Explore is free with limited access to a cloud Windows computer. Pay as you go is $50 a month for $50 of usage covering AI models and computer time. An always-on cloud computer comes with Sai Unlimited at $500 a month ([Sai pricing](https://sai.work/pricing)).

**What you give up by switching:** a $9 entry, connected-app integrations instead of clicking through screens, and a team workspace. Approval or safety language was not on Simular's homepage when we checked.

### Zapier, Make, and n8n

Trigger-action platforms run "when X happens, do Y" automations. Zapier says its agents "do work across 9,000+ apps" ([Zapier Agents](https://zapier.com/agents)), and n8n's Community Edition can be self-hosted ([n8n pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/)).

**Price:** Zapier Free includes 100 tasks, and Professional starts at $29.99 a month ([Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing)). Make Core is $9 a month ([Make pricing](https://www.make.com/en/pricing)). n8n Cloud starts at €20 a month billed annually for 2,500 executions.

**What you give up by switching:** an agent that does the research, judgment, and writing steps for you, and a workspace that keeps files between runs. You can run both: the automation platform for high-volume triggers, Construct for the work in between. See [Construct vs Zapier, Make, and n8n](/blog/construct-vs-zapier/).

### OpenClaw, self-hosted or on MyClaw

OpenClaw is open source under the MIT licence, with "No subscription. No hosted tier," and connects to WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack, Signal, and iMessage ([OpenClaw](https://openclaw.ai/)). MyClaw is an independent host that runs it for you.

**Price:** OpenClaw is free; you pay for hardware or hosting and model use. MyClaw Lite is $29 a month or $199 a year, and "AI token usage isn't included in the subscription" ([MyClaw pricing](https://myclaw.ai/pricing)).

**What you give up by switching:** a managed workspace with no servers to install, secure, update, or keep online. See [Construct vs OpenClaw](/blog/construct-vs-openclaw/) and [hosted OpenClaw alternatives](/blog/hosted-openclaw-alternatives/).

### Marblism

Marblism sells seven AI employees covering email, social, phone, and website work, with 50 hours of AI work a month ([Marblism pricing](https://www.marblism.com/pricing)).

**Price:** $44 a month billed monthly, or $24 a month billed annually, with a 7-day money-back guarantee.

**What you give up by switching:** work that does not fit one of seven fixed roles, such as a weekly pipeline report or a competitor price check across your own tools.

## Before you switch, try this

Several reasons to look elsewhere have a setup fix inside Construct. Try these first.

- **Want approval before anything goes out?** Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts in the instructions and send them yourself once you have checked them. Run a new job by hand a few times before you put it on the Calendar.
- **Hitting the 3 scheduled tasks on Lite?** Starter gives you 10 scheduled tasks and 150 steps per task for $59, and Pro gives 50 schedules and up to 1,000 steps per task. Try Pro free for 7 days before deciding.
- **Want a specific model?** Pro runs on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, either as a fallback or for every run, as the [Construct terms](/terms/) explain.
- **Your team does not live in the web app?** @mention the agent in Slack, message it on Telegram or Discord, or email its own address. Scheduled runs report in the web app with a notification. The [channels guide](/blog/ai-agent-telegram-slack-discord/) shows the setup.
- **Need several people to review the same output?** Ask Construct to build a private workspace app, such as a review queue with owners and statuses. The [internal-tools guide](/blog/build-internal-tools-with-construct/) explains how.
- **Running long scripts?** Each terminal command is capped at 300 seconds, so ask the agent to split long jobs into steps, and use Calendar schedules instead of a server that stays on.

## Already using another agent? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to drop the tool you have. Move the one recurring job it handles least reliably, or the one your teammates need to see.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test longer runs and your own model keys.
2. **Copy the instructions** you gave your current agent into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import from other agents. Write your rules into the first instruction, and Construct saves them as memory you can read and correct.

## When might an alternative still fit?

- Your company runs on Microsoft Teams or Microsoft 365: Viktor or Copilot Cowork.
- The work lives on your own computer or in desktop apps with no API: Claude with Cowork or Simular Sai.
- You need a phone answered, or high-volume branching triggers: Marblism, or Zapier, Make, and n8n.

For recurring team work that should leave files behind, run on a schedule, and cost a small business $9 to start, Construct is the better fit.

## Which should you choose?

Choose Construct. It starts at $9 for the whole workspace, keeps finished work as files your team can reopen, remembers what you teach it in a form you can correct, and on Pro runs on the model you pick. Use this guide to pick your starting point:

- **If you want recurring cross-app work done on a schedule,** choose Construct Lite at $9.
- **If you want approval before outside actions,** choose Construct and ask for drafts you send yourself.
- **If you need more than 3 schedules or longer runs,** choose Construct Starter at $59, or test Pro free for 7 days.
- **If you want your own model keys,** choose Construct Pro.
- **If you already pay for Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini,** keep it for your own desk work and add Construct for the team's recurring jobs.
- **If you need a phone line or desktop apps with no API,** Marblism or Simular Sai covers that one job; Construct still covers the rest.

For prices at the low end, see [what $10 and $20 a month buys in an AI agent](/blog/cheapest-ai-employee/).
