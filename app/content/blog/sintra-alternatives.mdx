## Why choose Construct over Sintra?

- **$9 a month instead of $97 list or $48.50 on sale.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address, on monthly billing.
- **It works without you in the chat.** Put a job on the Calendar and it runs server-side with your laptop closed, then reports in the web app with a notification.
- **Finished work becomes files, not chat history.** Reports, spreadsheets, and drafts land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still keeps steps 1 to 29.
- **One job across many apps, not one helper per role.** Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, HubSpot, Airtable, and more from the live integration catalog, add your own MCP tools, and let one agent use a browser and terminal in its workspace.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned about your business, correct a fact, or tell it to forget one.
- **The whole team, no seats.** Team workspaces have Owner, Admin, and Member roles, no member limit, and usage that draws on the owner's plan.

## What does "actually does the work" mean?

People who search for a Sintra alternative that "actually does tasks" usually mean four things. We use them to compare every product below.

1. **It acts without you in the chat.** A schedule or a trigger starts the work, not a message from you.
2. **It acts in your tools.** It sends, posts, files, or updates something, not only writes a draft for you to paste.
3. **It has somewhere to work.** A browser, files, or a computer lets it handle a website with no integration.
4. **It leaves something behind.** A file, a record, or a sent message you can check later.

Sintra covers part of this. Its help center says helpers can run recurring tasks on a schedule and can send emails and create calendar events when an integration is connected ([Sintra help](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12913572-recurring-scheduled-helper-tasks)). It also says "Helpers will not take actions on your behalf unless you explicitly ask them to (via chat) or set up a scheduled task/automation for them to run" ([Sintra integrations](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12929400-integrations-explained)). The chat is the center of the product. In Construct, the workspace is: files, schedules, run history, and memory sit in one place.

## Why do people look for a Sintra alternative?

### Credits run out

Every Sintra X plan includes 250 credits a month. A chat message uses 0.1 to 1 credit, a social post 1, a 15-second video about 40, and a website build 15 to 40. "If a workspace runs out of credits, AI employees will stop working." Credits do not roll over, and top-ups are non-refundable ([Sintra workspace credits](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12606676-workspace-credits)). Sintra's [plans article](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12606895-plans-and-pricing) lists the smallest top-up at $37.50 for 150 credits a month.

### You still direct the work

Sintra's own FAQ says "Every AI worker runs as a chatbot," and its help center says helpers act only when asked in chat or through a scheduled task or automation ([Sintra integrations](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12929400-integrations-explained)). If you wanted to hand a job over rather than supervise a chat, that is the gap.

### Sintra's pages disagree

When we checked on October 1, 2026, Sintra's pages gave different answers to basic questions:

- **Integrations.** The [pricing page](https://sintra.ai/pricing) lists "15+ integrations." The [homepage](https://sintra.ai/) says it connects over a thousand tools. The help center says over a thousand integrations "powered by Composio" in one article and 960 or more apps in [another](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12266334-what-is-sintra-ai).
- **Single helpers.** The pricing page sells each helper for $39 a month. The plans help article says "Individual Helper Plans are no longer available."
- **Annual price.** The pricing page shows a 12-month term at $52 a month regular and $15.60 on sale; the plans help article lists Sintra X at $468 a year.

Confirm the exact term and integration you need before you pay. Reviews will not settle it either: Trustpilot shows no rating for Sintra, saying "This company's rating is unavailable due to a breach of our guidelines" and "This company has been offering incentives for reviews" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/sintra.ai)).

## The Sintra alternatives, tool by tool

### 1. Construct

**Best for:** founders and small businesses whose work repeats every week across several apps and should leave files, a schedule, and correctable memory behind.

Construct is a general-purpose AI employee rather than a roster of chat helpers. Each agent has a persistent cloud workspace with files, live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, an agent email address, and a Calendar of scheduled jobs. You describe a job once, check the first result, save the steps as a workflow, and put it on the Calendar, where it runs server-side whether or not anyone is online.

**What it connects to.** Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable through its live integration catalog, plus any custom MCP tools you add. Where a site has no integration, the agent can use the browser in its workspace.

**Where you reach it.** The web app, Slack (@mentions, its threads, and DMs), Telegram, Discord, or its own email inbox, which starts work when you write to it from your own address. Scheduled runs report back in the web app with a notification.

**What it leaves behind.** Finished work is saved to workspace files as it is produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29, and next week's run can start from last week's file. When several people review the same output, Construct can build a private workspace app, such as a review queue or tracker.

**What it remembers.** Your offers, brand voice, client names, and preferred formats become memory you can open, correct, or forget. Change a fact once and every later job uses it. Teammates join a team workspace with Owner, Admin, or Member roles, with no member limit.

### 2. Marblism

**Best for:** a local business that needs its phone answered.

Marblism has seven named AI employees, including Eva for email and calendar, Sonny for social media, Penny for SEO blog posts, Stan for lead generation, and Rachel, a receptionist with her own number in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, or Mexico ([Marblism integrations](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/15278633-what-integrations-are-available)). "Eva never sends emails by herself" ([Marblism help](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/11516676-how-to-work-with-eva-the-exec-assistant)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** $44 a month, or $24 billed yearly, for 50 hours of work, all seven employees, and unlimited team members. A social post uses 30 minutes, a drafted email 5, a call 10, a blog post an hour ([Marblism billing](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/15361461-marblism-pricing-plans-billing-cadence)).

**Gaps:** "Marblism has no public API or webhooks." When hours run out, new tasks pause and the phone line pauses. Unused hours do not roll over.

**Best for:** one AI employee hired into a single role with shifts.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Starter is $8 a month for 800 credits, Basic $20 for 2,000, Pro $40 for 4,000. CellCog labels the $160 tier a "Part-time AI employee, a few shifts a month" and the $500 tier "A full-time AI employee, working weekday shifts" ([CellCog pricing](https://cellcog.ai/pricing)). **Gaps:** "All payments to CellCog are final and non-refundable." Budget from the shift labels, not the entry price. {/* TODO: link /blog/construct-vs-cellcog/ once it is live */}

## An illustrative week: posts, inbox, and a Monday report

This is an illustrative comparison, not a measured test.

A one-person consultancy wants three things each week: two LinkedIn posts drafted from last week's client notes, replies drafted for client emails, and a Monday report of open invoices from a spreadsheet.

- **On Construct,** you save the notes and the spreadsheet in the workspace, ask the agent for a dated file with the two post drafts, the reply drafts, and the invoice list, check the first run, and put it on the Calendar for Monday. It is one scheduled job instead of three helpers, it uses one of Lite's 3 scheduled tasks, and every week's file stays in Files.
- **On Sintra,** Soshie drafts the posts, the Inbox Manager drafts replies for you to approve, and Dexter can analyze the spreadsheet in chat. The posts cost about 1 credit each; the chat analysis depends on thread length.
- **On Marblism,** Sonny posts, Eva drafts replies, and the invoice report has no named employee. Two posts use an hour of the 50.

## Already using Sintra? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to cancel Sintra on day one. Start by moving the one recurring job your helpers handle least reliably, or the one that crosses the most apps.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test longer runs and your own model keys.
2. **Copy your business context into the first instruction.** Paste the brand voice, offers, and client details you gave Brain AI, and move the source files, such as client notes or an invoice sheet, into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import from Sintra: Brain AI's memory does not transfer. Write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read and correct. If you want to keep Sintra for its named marketing helpers, run the two side by side and let Construct take the recurring work that needs files and a schedule.

## When might Sintra still fit?

- You want 12 named marketing helpers and Sintra's ready-made automations, such as the meeting notetaker or Facebook comment replies, without describing the jobs yourself.
- You have already prepaid a 12-month term.
- You mainly work from Sintra's iOS or Android app.

For recurring work that should run on a schedule, leave files behind, and draw on memory you can correct, Construct is the better fit.
