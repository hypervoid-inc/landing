Construct gives every agent a persistent workspace with files, Calendar schedules, and memory your whole team shares, from $9 a month, a fixed price with published plan limits. Perplexity Computer bills in credits at $1 per 100: Max includes 10,000 a month for about $200, Pro includes none, and credits are personal to one account ([How credits work](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13838041-how-credits-work-on-perplexity)).

For founders and small teams with recurring work, Construct is the better choice. A weekly report runs on a fixed monthly plan instead of per-task credits, the file it saves is one every teammate can open, next week's run builds on this week's file, and you can read and correct what the agent remembers on every plan. Already using Perplexity? Keep it for deep one-off questions and give Construct the jobs that repeat.

## Why choose Construct over Perplexity Computer?

- **$9 a month, not credits per run.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address at a fixed monthly price. On Perplexity, a multi-step report is 350 to 950 credits, or $3.50 to $9.50, every time it runs.
- **Schedules on the plan you already pay for.** Calendar jobs run server-side with your laptop closed, within your plan's published limits. A Perplexity scheduled run is skipped if the account has no credits when it fires.
- **One workspace for the whole team.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles, with no member limit; everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. Perplexity credits cannot be transferred to another user outside limited Enterprise cases.
- **Files that build week over week.** Work is saved to workspace files as it is produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29, and next Monday's run opens this Monday's file.
- **Memory you can correct on every plan.** Open what Construct has learned, fix a fact, or forget it. Perplexity's Brain memory view is a research preview for Max subscribers.
- **More channels, and your own keys.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and the agent's own email inbox; Pro accepts keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, and xAI.

## What is Perplexity Computer?

Perplexity Computer is a cloud agent that researches across the web and app connectors, creates documents and apps, manages email, and runs on a schedule, with sub-agents, condition-based triggers, and a personal cloud sandbox with persistent memory ([Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13837784-what-is-computer)).

- **Launch:** February 2026, on Perplexity Max at $200 a month ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/perplexitys-new-computer-is-another-bet-that-users-need-many-ai-models)).
- **Models:** Perplexity picks from 20+ frontier models automatically ([Personal Computer](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/14659663-what-is-personal-computer)).
- **Where it runs:** in the cloud from web, iOS, and Android, or through Personal Computer, a desktop app for Mac and Windows that adds local files and native app control (same source).
- **Chat apps:** Slack, which needs a paid Slack plan, and Microsoft Teams ([Perplexity in Slack](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/14016915-using-perplexity-in-slack)).

## Where does Construct give you more control?

Both products run multi-step work in the cloud while you are away. Construct also fixes what you pay per run, keeps the result where your team can see it, and lets you correct the facts behind it.

### A predictable bill for recurring work

A weekly job on Perplexity costs whatever that week's run costs. On Construct, the plan sets the price. For a small team running the same five jobs every week, a fixed number on the invoice is easier to approve and easier to plan around.

### One workspace for the team

Perplexity says credits are personal to your account and cannot be transferred or assigned to another user, outside limited Enterprise cases. Construct team workspaces put people, agents, files, and apps in one place, with Owner, Admin, and Member roles ([team workspaces](/use-cases/team-workspaces/)). A report the agent saves is a file every member with access can open.

### Files and schedules that live together

Every Construct run saves its output to workspace files as it goes. A job that worked becomes a saved workflow and a Calendar schedule with result history, and next week's run can open this week's file. Perplexity's background scheduled tasks start a fresh, isolated agent for each run with no prior conversation context ([Scheduled Tasks in Computer](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11521526-perplexity-tasks)).

### See and fix what the agent remembers

In Construct, memory is something you can read, on every plan, in the same workspace as the files and workflows that depend on it. If a client changes its reporting format, you correct the stored fact once and every later job uses it. Perplexity's Brain also lets you edit or delete entries, as a research preview for Max subscribers ([Perplexity Max](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11680686-perplexity-max)). The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) shows how Construct's works.

## Already using Perplexity Computer? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up Perplexity. Start with the scheduled task whose credit cost varies most from week to week, or the one your teammates need to see.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the task's instructions** into a Construct chat, and move the reports and source files you want to keep into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog, or add your own MCP tools.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: Perplexity credits, Brain memory, and published pplx.app sites do not transfer. Purchased Perplexity credits stay valid for a year if unused, so there is no rush to spend them. Two things to set up deliberately. Construct runs start from the Calendar, by hand, or from an agent, so a condition-triggered task becomes a frequent schedule that checks for the condition. And Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Perplexity Computer still fit?

- The job is one deep research question where you want Perplexity to orchestrate many models and premium data sources.
- You want the agent on your own Mac or PC through Personal Computer, including local models on supported hardware.
- Your company runs on Microsoft Teams.

For recurring team work that needs files, schedules, correctable memory, and a bill a small business can predict, Construct is the better fit.
