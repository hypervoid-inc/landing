**Zen Mode is a calmer, chat-first way into Construct Computer, the AI agent with its own cloud computer.** You ask in one box. Your agents do the work on their computer in the background, and a single Home screen shows only what needs you, what is running, and what is coming up. The computer itself does not change: the same browser, files, inbox, schedules, and memory are all still there, and **Open OS** brings back the full desktop, mid-task, whenever you want to watch. Zen Mode also works on your phone, which the desktop never did.

- **What it is:** a second way into the same Construct workspace. Not a new version, not a separate plan, not a lite edition.
- **Where it lives:** [app.construct.computer](https://app.construct.computer). From the desktop, click **Zen Mode** in the menu bar. From Zen Mode, click **Open OS** to go back. Same account, nothing to migrate.
- **What stays the same:** your agents, files, mail, memory, automations, and Activity history.
- **What is new:** a Home screen built around what needs you, a layout that fits a phone, and keyboard-first navigation.
- **Watching is on demand:** every chat has a computer panel with the live browser, terminal, and files, and you can take control of the browser at any point.
- **Price:** included in every Construct plan, from $9/month.

If you saw a "Try V2" button in the Construct menu bar over the last few weeks, this is it, with its real name.

## Why build a calmer way into the same computer?

When we launched Construct, the pitch was a computer you could watch. Your AI got a desktop of its own, with windows, a dock, and a cursor that belonged to it, and you could see every click. People liked that enough to make Construct [#1 Product of the Day on Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/construct-computer). Watching is still the fastest way to trust an agent you have never used before, because nothing is hidden.

Then something predictable happened. Once people trusted it, watching turned into a chore.

Automation researchers saw this coming a long time ago. In _Ironies of Automation_ (1983), Lisanne Bainbridge pointed out that vigilance studies show it is "impossible for even a highly motivated human being to maintain effective visual attention towards a source of information on which very little happens, for more than about half an hour." An agent's desktop is exactly that kind of source. Long stretches of pages loading and text appearing, then one moment that actually needs a person.

If the only way to catch that moment is to watch the whole run, the agent has not taken work off your plate. It has swapped your job for a worse one: monitoring. We wrote about the same trap from the reviewing side in [Nobody merges an email](/blog/agent-verification-gap/).

So we kept the computer and changed what you look at.

**Some days you want to watch it work. Some days you just want it done.** Zen Mode is for the second kind of day. The desktop is one click away for the first.

## What is Zen Mode?

Zen Mode is the task-first shell of Construct Computer. Instead of a desktop full of windows, you get one place to ask and one place to check in, and the agents keep working on their own computer whether you are looking or not.

Here is what Home shows:

- **The ask box.** "What should Construct take off your plate?" Type a request, attach up to 8 files, @-mention files, folders, apps, agents, or teammates, and pick which agent takes it. Starter prompts such as "Research a topic" and "Schedule a daily briefing" are there the first time you open it.
- **Needs you.** Everything waiting on a human, in one list: a question an agent asked, an app it needs you to connect, a workflow run that failed, or a billing or usage limit. Each item has one button, such as Answer or View run.
- **Running now.** Every live task with an elapsed timer and a Stop button, plus Stop all when several are running.
- **Recent chats and Coming up.** Where you left off, and what your schedules will run next.

The sidebar holds the rest of the computer: Agents, Apps, Automations, Files, Mail, Memory, and Activity. Press `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows) to search chats, pages, apps, and files, or to hand the query straight to Construct as a new task.

It is a view, not a version. Both shells use the same account and the same session, so there is nothing to migrate and no setting to flip. That is also why we retired the working name "V2": a new version implies the old one is going away, and the desktop is not going anywhere.

## One request, start to finish

Here is the job from the film, step by step: "Research our top 5 competitors and brief me every Monday."

1. **Ask.** Type it on Home and send. The task appears in Running now with a timer, and you can close the tab. The work runs on Construct's cloud computer, not in your browser.
2. **It works on its own computer.** Construct searches the web, reads pricing pages in its browser, and writes the brief to Files. If you want to look, open the chat's computer panel with `⌘\`. Its Progress, Browser, Terminal, and Files tabs follow whatever the agent is using. If a site needs a human, click **Take control** on the live browser, finish that step, and give control back.
3. **Steer without restarting.** While it runs, the ask box reads "Add to the task, Construct will fold it in." Type "include each company's annual discount" and the running task picks it up.
4. **It asks when it needs you.** A question arrives as a card with options, and it lands in Needs you with an Answer button. Nothing else interrupts you.
5. **Make it recurring.** Put it on a schedule, such as "Weekly on…" Monday at 8:00 in your time zone. You get a preview of the next runs and a rule for when a run is still going ("Skip this run" or "Queue it"). The schedule then appears in Coming up.
6. **Check what it left behind.** A brief in Files, a chat you can reopen to see where each line came from, an entry in Activity describing what the agent did and why, and possibly a memory such as "Prefers briefs on Mondays" that you can correct or forget.

Curious what it is doing right now? Click **Open OS**. The same task is on the full desktop, mid-run.

## Calm is not the same as blind

The risk with any "just get it done" interface is that you stop knowing what your agent did. Zen Mode keeps every record the desktop keeps and puts each one a click away.

- **Activity** answers "what did my agents do, and why?" Filter by agent, status, and date, and export to CSV or JSON. Activity is kept for 30 days.
- **Memory** shows who learned each fact, where it came from, and which agents it applies to. Correct a fact, or forget it; forgotten memories stay in Forgotten so you can restore them. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) covers this in depth.
- **Mail** gives the agent an address of its own. When it drafts a reply, you can Send it, Ask for changes, or Discard it.
- **Stop** is always close: press `Esc` or the Stop button in a chat, type `/stop`, or use Stop all on Home.

One limit to be clear about: Zen Mode does not add an approval step before every action. An agent with access to a tool can use that tool. For anything you cannot take back, such as a payment or a message to a customer, keep review in the instructions and run the job on demand a few times before you schedule it. The interface got calmer. Deciding what to automate is still your call.

## Does Zen Mode work on a phone?

Yes. The desktop was built for a large screen, and on a phone it hands you off with a note: "Zen Mode works on this phone. Same computer, same agents." Zen Mode is built to fit. On narrow screens the sidebar becomes a drawer and the ask box docks to the bottom of Home. Open [app.construct.computer](https://app.construct.computer) in your phone's browser and sign in with the same account.

It is the mobile web app, not an App Store app, so there is nothing to install. It is best for the small moments that used to wait until you were back at a desk: answering a question an agent asked while you were out, checking Running now, reviewing a drafted reply, or starting a task from the train and letting the cloud computer do the rest.

## For people who live on the keyboard

- `⌘K` searches everything, or asks Construct.
- `G` then a letter jumps anywhere: `G N` new chat, `G H` Home, `G A` Agents, `G U` Automations, `G F` Files, `G M` Mail, `G R` Memory, `G V` Activity.
- `⌘\` shows or hides the computer panel in a chat.
- `Esc` stops the current run.
- `?` lists every shortcut.
- In the ask box, `/new`, `/stop`, `/agents`, and `/clear` do what they say.

Zen Mode also has **Groups**: one shared conversation with your team and your agents. A lead agent replies by default, and specialist agents answer when you @mention them.

## How other AI agents handle "watch it or leave it"

We build Construct. The table below is our editorial comparison of what each product documents about where its agent works and how you check in, as of **September 29, 2026**. It is not a hands-on benchmark, and prices are the lowest monthly subscription we found that includes each agent.

<ArticleTable caption="How AI agents with their own computer show their work, September 2026">

| Product | Where the agent works | How you check in | Watching and taking over | Entry price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Construct Zen Mode | Its own persistent cloud computer, with browser, terminal, files, and an inbox | Chat, plus Home: Needs you, Running now, Coming up | Computer panel in each chat, Take control of the browser, full desktop via Open OS | $9/mo (Lite) |
| Grok Bot | A cloud computer per Bot that keeps working 24/7 | Conversation; Bots "only come back when something needs your approval" | Open Agent Computer shows clicks, typing, and navigation; take control for passwords, 2FA, and payments | $20/mo (Cursor Pro) |
| Claude, with Cowork in the main chat | Anthropic's servers, in an isolated environment | One chat window that routes each request, since September 16, 2026 | Shows what Claude is planning and doing so you can steer | $20/mo (Pro) |
| ChatGPT Work | OpenAI's cloud, with a separate cloud browser | The conversation; you approve consequential actions | Follow progress in the chat; take over its computer on mobile and desktop | $20/mo (Plus) |
| Manus 2.0 | A Cloud Computer, or your own computer in an authorized session | Chat, plus event-triggered Automations | A visible workspace, which you can watch live from your phone | $20/mo (Pro) |

</ArticleTable>

The pattern is hard to miss: everyone has converged on calm by default, with the computer on demand. When Anthropic merged Cowork into the main Claude chat, TechCrunch reported the reason plainly: "customers often struggled to choose the right tab for the right task." OpenAI removed ChatGPT's standalone agent mode in August 2026 and moved browsing into Work's cloud browser, and a community thread titled "Agent mode was removed with no real replacement" shows how much some people valued seeing the browser.

Where Construct is different:

- **Two full views of one computer.** Zen Mode is not a chat with a preview window attached. The complete desktop, with its windowed browser, terminal, editor, and calendar, is one click away and shows the same work.
- **A workspace that outlives the chat.** Files, a native agent inbox, memory you can correct, schedules, workflows, and [internal apps your agents build](/blog/build-internal-tools-with-construct/) all stay in one place for the next job.
- **The lowest entry price here.** Construct Lite is $9/month, $11 below the next cheapest option in the table. That compares starting prices, not equal usage, as our [Grok Bot comparison](/blog/grokbot-alternative/) explains.

Where another product may fit better: if you already pay for Cursor, SuperGrok, ChatGPT Plus, or Claude Pro, try the agent you already have first. And Construct works on its own cloud computer, not yours. If the job depends on files or apps that only exist on your own machine, a product that can operate your computer, such as Manus in an authorized session, is the more direct fit.

## When should you open the desktop instead?

- **You want to watch a long browser job** from start to finish, or keep several apps side by side.
- **You want the windowed apps.** The standalone browser, terminal, editor, and calendar live on the desktop.
- **You are showing Construct to someone new.** Watching an agent use a computer is still the quickest way to understand what it can do.

Switching is one click each way, and nothing you do in one view is lost in the other.
