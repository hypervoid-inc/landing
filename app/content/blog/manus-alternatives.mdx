Construct runs your recurring business work, the weekly report, the Monday pipeline review, the daily digest, from a persistent cloud workspace for $9 a month, with 3 scheduled tasks and every finished file kept between runs. Manus bills credits on every run, unused credits do not roll over, a new task cannot start at zero credits, and a machine that keeps your files between tasks is a separate $30 or $50 a month add-on.

For founders and small teams moving recurring work off Manus, Construct is the better choice. The price is a monthly plan with its limits published up front, the workspace that holds last week's output comes with every plan, and a schedule you put on the Calendar runs server-side with your laptop closed. This guide compares Construct with Manus and seven other alternatives on those points, checked on October 1, 2026.

## Why choose Construct over Manus?

- **A $9 monthly plan instead of credits per run.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Manus spends credits on every run, and a heavy week can leave a scheduled run with nothing to spend ([Manus credit rules](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711097-what-are-the-rules-for-credits-consumption-and-how-can-i-obtain-them)).
- **A persistent workspace on every plan.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so this week's run opens last week's file. On Manus, persistence is the Cloud Computer add-on at $30 or $50 a month, and its files are deleted if you stop paying ([Cloud Computer billing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).
- **Nothing lost when a run stops early.** Finished work is saved as it is produced, so a run that stops at step 30 still keeps steps 1 to 29.
- **Memory you can inspect, correct, and forget.** When a client changes its report format, fix the stored fact once and every later run uses it.
- **One workspace for the whole team, with no seat fee.** Team workspaces have Owner, Admin, and Member roles and no member limit, and usage draws on the owner's plan. Manus Team is priced per seat ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)).
- **Your model, or your own keys.** Construct Pro can run on your keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI.

## What does Manus offer for recurring work?

Manus 2.0, announced on September 28, 2026, added scheduled tasks and Automations that start "when something happens in a connected service," such as an email arriving, a calendar event, a Slack message, or a Notion update ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0)).

- **Compute:** each task runs in a sandbox that closes when the task completes, with outputs kept in the chat history. The Cloud Computer is a persistent virtual machine sold separately ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392111-what-is-the-cloud-computer)).
- **Ways to start a task:** email from up to 10 approved senders, up to 30 workflow emails on paid plans ([Mail Manus](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/12059745-how-can-i-use-mail-manus)), and @Manus in Slack ([Manus in Slack](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/14431752-chatting-with-manus-agent-on-slack-channels-dms-and-file-delivery)).
- **Connectors:** Gmail, Google Calendar, GitHub, Linear, monday.com, Supabase, and Postgres, plus custom API or MCP connectors ([Manus Connectors](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/12231777-how-can-i-use-manus-connectors)).
- **Price:** Free has 300 daily credits, 1 concurrent task, and 2 scheduled tasks. Pro starts at $20 a month for 4,000 credits and 20 scheduled tasks; a $40 tier has 8,000 credits. Team starts at $20 per seat ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)). The Cloud Computer is $30 a month for 2 vCPUs, 4 GB of RAM, and 70 GB of storage, or $50 for 8 GB of RAM and 120 GB ([Cloud Computer billing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).
- **Credits:** "Unused credits from the previous month do not roll over," and you cannot start new tasks while your available credits are at or below zero ([Manus credit rules](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711097-what-are-the-rules-for-credits-consumption-and-how-can-i-obtain-them)).
- **Continuity:** Manus resumed independent operations on September 1, 2026, and some users had to back up and restore their data during that transition ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/manus-resumes-independent-operations)).

## 1. Construct: recurring work that leaves files behind

**Best for:** founders and small teams whose recurring work crosses several apps and should leave dated files, a schedule, and run history behind.

Each Construct agent works from a persistent cloud workspace with files, a browser, a sandbox terminal, its own email address, and a Calendar of scheduled jobs. The workspace comes with every plan, with no separate machine to rent: last week's report is a file this week's run can open. It reaches Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable through a live integration catalog, and you can add your own MCP tools.

Recurring work follows one path. Run a job by hand until the output is right, save it as a workflow with agent steps and connected-app actions, then put it on the Calendar. A scheduled item can run an agent prompt, a single app action, or a whole workflow, once or on a cadence, and it keeps the result history. Schedules run server-side, so the job fires with your laptop closed, and the result arrives in the web app with a notification.

What you get that a credit-per-run setup does not give you:

- **A cost you can plan.** Lite is $9 a month for up to 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Starter is $59 with 6x Lite usage, up to 5 agents, 150 steps per task, and 10 scheduled tasks. Pro is $299 with 32x Lite usage, up to 15 agents, up to 1,000 steps per task, 50 scheduled tasks, and your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, and xAI. Pro has a 7-day free trial ([Construct pricing](/pricing/)).
- **Output that accumulates.** Each Monday's file sits next to last Monday's, so the agent can compare weeks without you pasting anything back in.
- **Memory you control.** Open what the agent has learned, correct a fact, or delete it. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) shows how.
- **A shared place for the team.** Teammates join a team workspace as Owners, Admins, or Members, with no member limit, and open the same files, schedules, and run history.
- **More ways to reach it.** Message it from the web, @mention it in Slack, use it in Telegram or Discord, or email its own address.
- **Internal apps when a report is not enough.** Construct can build a private workspace app, such as a review queue or tracker, that your team uses alongside the files.

Two things to set up deliberately. Construct starts runs from the Calendar, by hand, or from an agent, not from events in other apps, so for an event-driven job such as new CRM records, schedule a regular run that picks up everything new since the last file. Workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them. ## 2. Manus

**Best for:** one-off research and build tasks, and jobs that must start on an event in a connected service.

Manus's recurring features, prices, and credit rules are summarized above. For a weekly job, budget for credits on every run plus the Cloud Computer if the job needs files from earlier runs.

## 3. Claude Cowork

**Best for:** teams already paying for Claude.

Scheduled tasks are available on all paid plans and run hourly, daily, weekly, on weekdays, or manually, including while your computer is asleep. New Cowork tasks run in the cloud from October 6, 2026 ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)). Claude Pro is $20 a month, Max starts at $100, and Team standard seats are $25 ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). A scheduled task that needs local files or apps runs only locally, and the help article does not state a cap on scheduled tasks. More in our [Claude Cowork alternatives](/blog/claude-cowork-alternatives/) guide.

## 4. Perplexity Computer

**Best for:** recurring research and monitoring with cited sources.

Scheduled Tasks run in the cloud no more often than once an hour, each conversation can own up to 15 tasks, and every run uses Computer credits ([Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11521526-perplexity-tasks)). "100 credits = $1"; Pro has no monthly credits beyond a one-time bonus, and Max includes 10,000 a month ([how credits work](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13838041-how-credits-work-on-perplexity)). The plan page lists Pro at $17 and Max at $167 a month billed annually ([Perplexity plans](https://www.perplexity.ai/pro)). Like Manus, it bills per run. See our [Perplexity Computer alternatives](/blog/perplexity-computer-alternatives/).

## 5. Lindy

**Best for:** routines around each person's inbox, meetings, and Slack.

A Lindy Routine runs on a daily, weekly, or monthly schedule or on an event such as an email or Slack message, and can be dry-run on real data ([Lindy Routines](https://docs.lindy.ai/teammate/routines)). Plus is $29.99 per user a month with 3,000 credits, Pro $99.99 with 15,000, and Max $199.99 with 35,000 ([Lindy pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing)). Billing is per user, and work pauses when the shared credit pool runs out. See [Construct vs Lindy](/blog/construct-vs-lindy/).

## 6. Viktor

**Best for:** one shared coworker in Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Viktor schedules recurring tasks, works from its own cloud computer, and asks for confirmation before high-stakes actions ([Viktor](https://viktor.com/)). Team plans start at $50 a month per workspace for 20,000 credits, and unused monthly credits roll over for one month ([Viktor pricing](https://viktor.com/pricing)). It works in Slack and Teams only. See our [Viktor alternatives](/blog/viktor-alternatives/).

## 7. Runbear

**Best for:** named agents per team function in Slack, Teams, or Discord.

A Runbear agent can fire on a schedule, a webhook, or an event in a connected app ([Runbear docs](https://docs.runbear.io/)). Team is $99 a month per workspace for 20,000 credits; Business is $399 for 100,000 credits ([Runbear pricing](https://runbear.io/pricing)). "Agents pause when the organization reaches its credit limit" ([Runbear billing](https://docs.runbear.io/administration/billing-and-credits)). A file workspace for the agent is not documented in the pages we reviewed.

## 8. Gemini Spark

**Best for:** recurring Gmail, Calendar, and Drive work on a personal Google account in a supported country.

Spark runs in the background with time-based or conditional triggers and checks with you before major actions ([Gemini Spark](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/)). It reached the $20 Google AI Pro tier in the US in July 2026; that rollout excluded the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Nigeria, and work and school accounts ([Chrome Unboxed](https://chromeunboxed.com/googles-agentic-ai-gemini-spark-is-now-available-on-the-20-pro-tier-in-the-us/)).

## 9. Grok Bot

**Best for:** Cursor or SuperGrok subscribers whose recurring job is a path through websites.

A Bot can save a path you walk it through as a skill and rerun it on a schedule, on a persistent cloud computer that keeps working when your laptop is closed ([Grok Bot docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview)). Access is included on paid individual Cursor plans from $20 a month ([Cursor Help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans), [Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)). Usage resets weekly, and tiers are described relative to each other rather than in numbers.

## An example: the Monday client report

This is an illustrative workflow, not a customer case study or a measured test.

A four-person agency sends each client a Monday update: last week's ad spend, open tasks from Linear, and three lines on what changed. Today a project manager builds it by hand every Monday morning.

**In Construct:** connect the services, then ask the agent: "Pull last week's spend from the ad account and open issues from the Linear project. Compare with last Monday's report in Files/clients/acme/. Save this week's report as a dated file in the same folder and draft, but do not send, an email to the client." Check two Mondays by hand, save it as a workflow, and put it on the Calendar. Each Monday's file sits next to last Monday's, the cost is inside the plan you already pay, and the whole team can open the folder. The [scheduled tasks guide](/blog/ai-agent-scheduled-tasks/) walks through each step.

**On Manus:** each weekly run starts a fresh task and spends credits, and keeping a running file of past reports for comparison needs the Cloud Computer add-on.

The test that matters is the eighth Monday, not the first: did every run fire, did it compare against the right file, and what did the month cost?

## Already using Manus? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up Manus to start. Move the one recurring job where credits or lost files hurt most, and keep Manus for one-off research while you compare.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** if you want longer runs or your own model keys.
2. **Copy the task prompt** from your Manus Automation into a Construct chat, and upload the files the job depends on, such as last week's report, to the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import from Manus: tasks, chat history, and Cloud Computer files do not transfer. Download the outputs you want to keep, and write your standing rules into the first instruction so Construct saves them as memory you can read.

## When might Manus still fit?

- Your job must start the moment an event happens in a connected service, such as an incoming email.
- You need a process running 24/7, such as a bot or a server, on a machine you rent.
- You mostly hand over one-off research and build tasks rather than recurring ones.

For work that repeats every week and should leave files your team can open, at a price you can budget, Construct is the better fit.

## Which Manus alternative should you choose?

Choose Construct. It starts at $9 a month instead of credits per run, keeps a persistent workspace on every plan, saves finished work as it goes, and runs your schedules server-side with your laptop closed.

- **If your job runs weekly or daily and should leave a folder of dated files,** choose Construct Lite at $9.
- **If you need more than 3 schedules or up to 5 agents,** choose Construct Starter at $59.
- **If you need long runs or your own model keys,** try Construct Pro free for 7 days.
- **If your team works in Slack, Telegram, or Discord,** Construct answers there too, with no per-seat fee.
- **If you already pay for Claude, Google AI Pro, or Cursor,** keep that plan for personal tasks and give Construct the recurring team work.
- **If you already run Manus,** keep it for one-off research and add Construct for the weekly jobs.

Pick the report that repeats every week. Run it once by hand in Construct, check the file, and ask the agent to compare it with last week's on the second run. When two runs in a row are right, save it as a workflow and put it on the Calendar, where it uses one of Lite's 3 scheduled tasks. Start on [Lite at $9](/pricing/), or try Pro free for seven days.
