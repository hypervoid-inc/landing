Construct gives your team an AI employee with a persistent workspace, its own email address, and scheduled jobs from $9 a month, and it runs on the model you choose, including Claude through your own Anthropic key on Pro. Claude Cowork comes with paid Claude plans from $20 a month, shares usage limits with chat and Claude Code, and Anthropic describes its work as seen by "just you"; sharing means Claude Team seats at $25 each, with a two-seat minimum, plus usage-billed Claude Tag in Slack.

For most founders and small teams, Construct is the better choice. It costs less to start, keeps every finished file in a workspace the whole team opens without buying seats, and lets you pick or bring your own model provider. Already paying for Claude? Keep Cowork for personal drafting and research and give Construct the recurring team work. The two run side by side.

## Why choose Construct over Claude Cowork?

- **$9 a month instead of $20, and no seats.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. A team workspace has no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan.
- **Files that stay put between runs.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 behind, and next week's run starts from last week's output.
- **Your model, including Claude.** Construct runs on bundled models, and Pro lets you bring keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI. Keep using Claude through your own key and switch later without moving a file.
- **One workspace the whole team shares.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles. Nobody needs a Claude seat to open the agent's work.
- **Its own email address.** Every plan gives the agent an inbox of its own. Forward a thread from your address and it starts work.
- **It answers where your team already talks.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and email, so teammates outside Slack are covered too.

## What is Claude Cowork?

Cowork is the part of Claude that carries out multi-step work: you describe an outcome and Claude produces documents, organized files, and research, according to [Anthropic's getting started guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork). It can use a browser, build spreadsheets and presentations, run parallel sub-agents, and run scheduled tasks.

It has changed quickly this year, which is why older comparisons are often wrong:

- **July 7, 2026:** Cowork moved to the web and mobile, with cloud sessions that "can keep running when your machine is asleep" ([Automaton Agency](https://automatonagency.com/insights/claude-cowork-mobile-and-web)).
- **August 26, 2026:** Claude got its own browser inside Cowork ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-built-in-browser)).
- **September 16, 2026:** Anthropic merged Cowork and chat into one Claude, starting with Pro and Max ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude)).
- **October 6, 2026:** Anthropic's help center says new Cowork tasks run only in the cloud; tasks that need local files still reach them through the desktop app ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)).

### What is Claude Tag?

Claude Tag is Anthropic's agent for Slack channels, in public beta on Team and Enterprise plans. Anyone in a paired Slack workspace can tag `@Claude` with a task; it works in a cloud sandbox and posts results back in the thread ([Claude Tag overview](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/overview)). Anthropic's own summary of the split: "team work → Claude Tag; personal work → Cowork or Claude Code" ([How Claude Tag works](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/concepts/how-it-works)).

So Cowork is Anthropic's personal agent and Tag is its team one. Construct does both jobs from one workspace, at a lower starting price.

## Where does Construct give you more control?

Both products put an agent to work in the cloud. Construct also gives you a say in what stays behind after a run, which model does the work, and who can see it.

### A workspace that keeps its files

Claude Tag is explicit about its lifecycle: a sandbox is built for each thread and released a few minutes after a turn finishes, and "files that exist only in the sandbox" do not survive ([How Claude Tag works](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/concepts/how-it-works)).

Construct's workspace is the durable place. Every run saves its output to workspace files as it goes, and next week's run starts from last week's output. For a team, that is the difference between a thread to scroll back through and a folder to open.

### Your choice of model, including Claude

Cowork and Tag run on Anthropic's models. Construct Pro can run on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, either as a fallback after bundled limits or for every run, as the [Construct terms](/terms/) explain. Keep using Claude through your own Anthropic key today and switch providers next quarter without moving your work. Your keys bill you directly when they are used.

### A team workspace without per-seat pricing

Sharing Claude's work means Claude Team seats, or Claude Tag in Slack with an admin setting up service accounts per tool. Construct's [team workspaces](/use-cases/team-workspaces/) put people, the agent, files, apps, and conversations in one place, with Owner, Admin, and Member roles. A teammate can open Wednesday's work without being briefed on Friday's.

### Its own email address, and more channels

Every Construct agent has its own email address. Forward a supplier thread or an invoice from your own address and the agent starts work on it; replies can go out from the agent's address instead of your personal Gmail. Claude Tag's setup asks you to create an email account for Claude yourself, and Cowork acts through your own connected accounts. Construct also takes work from Slack, Telegram, Discord, and the web. See our guide to [giving an AI agent its own email address](/blog/ai-agent-email-address/).

### Memory you can inspect and correct

Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or forget it, so a changed client preference does not keep resurfacing. Anthropic's guide describes Cowork's memory as integrated with chat history. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) explains Construct's approach.

## Already using Cowork? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up Claude. Start with the recurring job your teammates need to see, or the one whose files keep getting lost between runs.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to run it on your own Anthropic key.
2. **Copy the instructions** from your Cowork scheduled task into a Construct chat, and move the files it uses into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: Cowork sessions, Claude memory, and Claude Tag channel notes do not transfer. Write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for steps you kept on Manual approval in Cowork, such as customer emails, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Cowork still fit?

- Your work is mostly personal research and documents, and you already pay for Claude.
- The job needs your local files or desktop apps through Claude Desktop.
- You want Manual, Auto, or Skip approval modes, or Claude for Small Business's ready-made QuickBooks, Shopify, Square, and Xero workflows ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-small-business-launches-new-workflows-integrations-and-training-programs)).

For recurring team work that needs shared files, schedules, a choice of model, and a price that does not grow with every seat, Construct is the better fit.
