## Why choose Construct over a chat assistant?

- **$9 a month for an agent that acts.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address.
- **Work saved as files, not buried in a thread.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts land in workspace files as they are produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29.
- **Schedules that run without you.** Put a job on the Calendar and it runs on Construct's servers with your laptop closed: 3 scheduled tasks on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** See what the agent has stored, correct a fact when it changes, or forget it.
- **A browser, a terminal, and your apps in one place.** Construct works in live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, and the apps in its live integration catalog, plus custom MCP tools.
- **One workspace for the team, no seats.** Team workspaces have Owner, Admin, and Member roles and no member limit, and the agent answers on the web, in Slack, Telegram, Discord, and by email.

## Conversation vs execution

A chat assistant is built around a conversation. Construct is built around an assigned outcome: research a market and email a PDF, review its own inbox, or update Linear from Slack, including scheduled work while you are offline.

Chat products have added file handling, connectors, scheduled tasks, and browsing. The difference that lasts is the default unit of work: a chat product organizes around a conversation, and Construct organizes around a workspace that conversations happen inside. That is where the files, schedules, and memory for next week's run live.

## Where does Construct do more for you?

### Keep the work, not just the chat

Business work depends on files, procedures, schedules, and facts that change over time. Construct keeps all of them in one workspace, and every run saves its output there as it goes. Your team can open the result next week without scrolling back through a conversation.

### Correct what the agent knows

Storing context is common. Being able to see a stored fact, replace it when it changes, and forget it entirely is what makes memory safe to rely on for work. Construct gives you all three, and Activity records each action with a short reason so you can trace what a recent run did. [AI agent memory you can control](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) covers the model in detail.

### Put recurring work on the Calendar

Start a job in chat, then save it as a versioned workflow and schedule it on Construct's Calendar. It acts through connected apps such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable, or custom MCP tools you add. The files and run history stay available after the conversation ends.

### Reach it where your team works

Message Construct from the web app, @mention it in Slack, text it on Telegram, use it in Discord, or email its own address. Nobody on the team needs a chat assistant account to hand it work.

## The same request in both

Take: "Summarize what changed in our top accounts this week and send it to the team."

A chat assistant can write a strong summary once the data is in front of it, and can fetch some of that data depending on its connectors and plan. Gathering the inputs it cannot reach, sending the result, and repeating the exchange next week usually stay with you.

Construct treats the same request as a job: read the accounts through connected-app actions, compare against files saved by the previous run, write the summary, send it from its own inbox, and leave the files and Activity summary behind. Put it on the Calendar and it repeats without being restated. The writing may be similar; the difference is the manual steps around it and whether next week starts from something.

## Already using ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up your chat assistant. Keep it for thinking and drafting, and move the one job you repeat every week.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the prompt** you paste into your chat assistant every week into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week with your laptop closed.

There is no import: chat history and saved memories do not transfer. Write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might a chat assistant still fit?

- You want to brainstorm or draft, with nothing to run afterward.
- You have a one-shot question.
- You are exploring a problem before any work begins.

For work that repeats, crosses apps, and has to leave files and a record behind, Construct is the better fit.
