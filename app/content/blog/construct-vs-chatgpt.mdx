Chat assistants combine conversation with product- and plan-dependent tools. Construct centers those capabilities in a persistent work OS for files, memory, schedules, workflows, terminal commands, live browser runs, and connected apps.

## Methodology

This is an editorial capability comparison using the linked public vendor documentation and Construct's current first-party product scope as of the publication date. It compares documented product models, not hands-on performance or every plan-specific feature.

## Side by side

| Feature        | Construct                                                       | Chat assistants                                                    |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Category       | AI employee in a persistent work OS                             | Conversational AI products with expanding tools                    |
| Execution      | Browser, terminal, and connected-app actions                    | Tool and app actions vary by product and plan                      |
| Continuity     | Workspace files and controlled memory                           | Chat history, projects, and memory vary by product and plan        |
| Integrations   | Live integration catalog; agent can act directly                | Connected apps and actions vary by product, plan, and settings     |

| Recurring work | Native schedules and reusable workflows                         | Scheduling and automation are available in some products and plans |
| Reach          | Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord slash commands, and email         | Web, desktop, and mobile; other channels vary by product           |

## Conversation vs execution

Chat assistants are centered on conversation, while their tools and actions vary by product and plan. Construct is centered on assigned outcomes — research a market and email a PDF, review its native inbox, or update Linear from Slack — using a persistent work OS, including scheduled work while you are offline.

## Persistence and inspection

A conversation can preserve messages, but business work also depends on files, procedures, schedules, and facts that change over time. Construct keeps those parts in one workspace. Its long-term memory is inspectable and correctable, while the Activity feed keeps bounded action summaries and best-effort reasons. Bounded tool details remain in chat.

## Recurring and cross-app work

Chat is often the right interface for starting a job, but it should not be the only place the work can live. You or the agent can encode a repeatable process as a versioned linear workflow, schedule it through Construct's Calendar, and act through connected applications. The resulting files and run history remain available after the conversation ends.

## The practical boundary

Choose a chat assistant when conversation, drafting, research, or its available tools fit the job. Choose Construct when the request needs a persistent work OS, several execution surfaces, recurring operation, or a visible record of what was completed.

## When to choose Construct

- Recurring autonomous tasks
- Cross-app workflows
- Scheduled background work
- Proof of what the agent did

## When to choose a chat assistant

- Drafting and brainstorming
- One-shot Q&A
- Available chat tools and actions cover the job
