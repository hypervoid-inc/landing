Construct gives a small business a general-purpose AI employee from $9 a month, with its own workspace, files, schedules, and email address, and from $59 a team workspace with no per-seat fee. Sintra X lists at $97 a month (often on sale) and meters work in credits per workspace, Marblism costs $44 a month for 50 hours of work, and Lindy is $29.99 per user a month, so every teammate who uses it takes a paid seat. For founders and small teams, Construct is the better choice: it costs the least to start, it is not boxed into a fixed set of roles, its recurring jobs run across your apps and leave files behind, and adding a teammate does not add a seat.

Already paying for one of the three? Keep it for the role it covers, such as Marblism's phone line, and give Construct the recurring reports and cross-app jobs. The comparison of all three, with sources, follows below.

## Why choose Construct instead of Sintra, Marblism, or Lindy?

- **$9 to start.** Construct Lite is $9 a month ($7.50 billed annually) for up to 2 agents, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Sintra lists at $97, Marblism at $44, and Lindy at $29.99 for each user.
- **No seats to count.** On any plan, a team workspace has Owner, Admin, and Member roles, no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. On Lindy, anyone who uses it takes a seat.
- **One employee for any recurring job, not a roster of roles.** Construct researches and writes cited reports, analyzes files in your workspace, reads and sends email from its own address, updates connected apps, runs scripts, and builds private internal tools.
- **Work that lands in files.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts are saved to workspace files as they are produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29, and next week's run builds on last week's file.
- **Schedules that run without you.** Save a job as a workflow and put it on the Calendar: 3 scheduled tasks on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro, all run server-side with your laptop closed.
- **Your apps and your channels.** A live integration catalog, including Gmail, Notion, HubSpot, and Airtable, plus your own MCP tools. Reach it from the web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, or email.

## Sintra

Sintra sells 12 helpers, including Soshie for social media, Penn for copywriting, Seomi for SEO, Emmie for email marketing, Cassie for customer support, Dexter for data analysis, and Vizzy as a virtual assistant. They share one memory, Brain AI, and Sintra says they can draw on each other for complex tasks ([What is Sintra AI?](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12266334-what-is-sintra-ai)). It runs on the web, iOS, and Android, and its pricing page says it is powered by Claude 4.5 Sonnet.

Sintra's own FAQ says "Every AI worker runs as a chatbot" ([Sintra](https://sintra.ai/)). Helpers act only when you ask in chat or set up a scheduled task or one of five automations: weekly social posts, inbox labeling with drafts you approve, a meeting notetaker, a daily summary, and Facebook comment replies ([automations](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/9823490-automations-explained)). Scheduled tasks can send emails and create calendar events when the integration is connected ([recurring tasks](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12913572-recurring-scheduled-helper-tasks)).

**Price:** Sintra X lists at $97 a month and was on sale at $48.50 for a one-month term, $23.60 a month on a 3-month term, and $15.60 a month on a 12-month term, each with 250 credits a month ([Sintra pricing](https://sintra.ai/pricing)). A social post costs 1 credit, a chat message 0.1 to 1, and a 15-second video about 40 ([workspace credits](https://help.sintra.ai/en/articles/12606676-workspace-credits)).

**Watch for:** "If a workspace runs out of credits, AI employees will stop working," and top-ups are non-refundable. Sintra's pages disagree on its integration count and on whether single-helper plans still exist. Trustpilot hides its rating, saying "This company has been offering incentives for reviews" ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/sintra.ai)). Our [Sintra alternatives guide](/blog/sintra-alternatives/) covers those details.

## Marblism

Marblism's seven AI employees are Eva (executive assistant), Sonny (social media), Stan (lead generation), Penny (SEO blog posts), Rachel (receptionist), Linda (legal documents), and Walter (websites) ([Marblism](https://www.marblism.com/)). Rachel gets her own phone number in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, or Mexico, and books into your Google or Microsoft calendar. Sonny posts to Instagram, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, Threads, Pinterest, TikTok, and YouTube ([integrations](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/15278633-what-integrations-are-available)). To use your current business number, you forward it to Rachel through your carrier; you cannot port it ([Marblism help](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/14827083-can-rachel-answer-calls-to-my-existing-business-number)).

On email, "Eva never sends emails by herself. She only drafts replies" ([working with Eva](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/11516676-how-to-work-with-eva-the-exec-assistant)).

When it launched on Hacker News in September 2024, the founders described Marblism as "an LLM-based dev platform to generate and iterate on full-stack web apps" ([Launch HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41568343)). Walter, the website builder, is the part of today's roster closest to that origin.

**Price:** $44 a month, $33 quarterly, or $24 billed yearly for 50 hours of work, with larger plans up to 10,000 hours. Each task type uses a set amount of time: an email draft 5 minutes, a call 10 minutes, a social post 30 minutes, a blog post an hour. Chatting with the employees is free, and unused hours do not roll over ([plans and billing](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/15361461-marblism-pricing-plans-billing-cadence)). A 7-day money-back guarantee applies ([Marblism pricing](https://www.marblism.com/pricing)).

**Watch for:** "Marblism has no public API or webhooks." When hours run out, Marblism shows "Your team went to the beach": work in progress finishes, new tasks pause, and Rachel's line pauses.

## Lindy

Lindy's documentation describes it as an AI employee that lives in Slack and connects to your tools. The whole team shares one Lindy in channels, and each person gets a private Lindy in DMs for inbox, meetings, and scheduling. It can also be texted over iMessage and SMS, keeps memory as files you can edit with version history, and lets you require approval in Slack before a write action. Lindy lists SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, and PIPEDA compliance ([Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/)). Our [Construct vs Lindy comparison](/blog/construct-vs-lindy/) covers it in depth.

**Price:** Plus is $29.99 per user a month with 3,000 credits, Pro $99.99 with 15,000, and Max $199.99 with 35,000 ([Lindy pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing)). A credit is "about one cent"; everyday tasks cost 2 to 250 credits and deep work 250 to 1,000 ([plan documentation](https://docs.lindy.ai/pricing)).

**Watch for:** "Anyone who uses Lindy takes a seat," including someone who only @mentions it in Slack. Credits do not roll over, and work pauses when the pool is empty. The plan documentation shows computer use on Pro and above, while the pricing page lists it on every plan. Lindy's Trustpilot page showed 1.7 out of 5 from 40 reviews, with recent complaints about credit use, billing, and reliability ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lindy.ai)).

## The small-business lens: what does a real month cost?

These are illustrative figures from published limits, not measured usage.

**A three-person shop.** Construct Starter is $59 a month for one team workspace with up to 5 agents, the same price for three people as for ten. Sintra also costs the same for three people as for one, because team members are free and share the workspace's 250 credits. Marblism is flat at $44, with unlimited team members. Lindy is $89.97 a month on Plus, because each person is a seat, with 9,000 pooled credits.

**A busy month.** Each allowance is counted in a different unit:

- **Construct Lite** runs 3 scheduled jobs on the Calendar, each with up to 50 steps, within the plan's published usage; Starter raises that to 10 jobs, 150 steps each, and 6x Lite's usage.
- **Marblism's 50 hours** cover about 600 drafted emails, or 300 ten-minute calls, or 100 social posts, if you spend them on nothing else.
- **Sintra's 250 credits** cover up to 250 social posts at 1 credit each, but chat messages, images, and videos draw from the same pool, and a 15-second video takes about 40.
- **Lindy's 3,000 credits per seat** cover 12 deep-work tasks at 250 credits each, or 3 at 1,000, at Lindy's published ranges.

**Who you can trust with customers.** Marblism's Eva only drafts. Lindy can require approval on each write action. Sintra's scheduled tasks can send email when the integration is connected. In Construct, set this up deliberately: its workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

**Lock-in.** Sintra's deepest discounts require paying 3 or 12 months upfront. Marblism's yearly price is $24 a month against $44 monthly. Lindy is monthly per seat. Construct is monthly from $9, with an annual option at $7.50 a month on Lite. Start on monthly billing until you have run your real work for a few weeks.

## Already using Sintra, Marblism, or Lindy? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to cancel anything. Move the recurring report or cross-app job your current tool handles least well.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test longer runs and your own model keys.
2. **Copy the instructions** you gave your current helper into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import from Sintra, Marblism, or Lindy: write your rules into the first instruction, and Construct saves them as memory you can read and correct.

## When might Sintra, Marblism, or Lindy still fit?

- You need your phone answered on a business number (Marblism's Rachel).
- You want ready-made marketing roles with Facebook comment replies built in (Sintra).
- You want per-action approvals in Slack, or texting over iMessage and SMS (Lindy).

For recurring reports and cross-app jobs that leave a file behind, at one price for the whole team, Construct is the better fit.

Choose Construct. It starts at $9 instead of $29.99 to $97, covers a team without per-seat fees from $59, does any recurring job across your apps instead of a fixed role, and leaves every finished file in a workspace your team shares. If Marblism already answers your phone, keep it for that and let Construct run the reports and follow-ups.

Give Construct your Monday report first: connect your CRM and inbox, ask for a dated file of new leads and open client emails, check it once, and put it on the Calendar. Start on [Lite at $9](/pricing/), or try Pro free for seven days.
