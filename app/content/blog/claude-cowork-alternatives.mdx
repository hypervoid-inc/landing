## What changed with Claude Cowork in 2026?

Cowork is the part of Claude that carries out multi-step work: you describe an outcome and come back to documents, organized files, or research ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)). Four changes this year make older comparisons unreliable:

- **July 7, 2026: cloud sessions.** Cowork moved beyond the Mac and Windows desktop apps to the web and mobile, and sessions "can keep running when your machine is asleep" ([Automaton Agency](https://automatonagency.com/insights/claude-cowork-mobile-and-web)).
- **August 26, 2026: its own browser.** Claude can open a browser inside the desktop app to navigate sites and fill forms, with logins you choose to import ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-built-in-browser)).
- **September 16, 2026: one Claude.** Anthropic merged Cowork and chat into a single interface, starting with Pro and Max ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude)). The pricing page now says "Claude Cowork is now just Claude."
- **October 6, 2026: cloud by default.** Anthropic's help center says new Cowork tasks run only in the cloud and the "Only on your computer" setting is removed. Tasks that need local files still reach them through the desktop app.

## Why do people still look for a Claude Cowork alternative?

**It is a personal agent.** Anthropic's documentation says Cowork work is seen by "just you," and sums up the split as "team work → Claude Tag; personal work → Cowork or Claude Code" ([How Claude Tag works](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/concepts/how-it-works)). Claude Tag, in public beta on Team and Enterprise plans, runs in a sandbox per Slack thread whose files are released when it goes idle, bills channel work by usage under a spend limit, and needs a Claude Owner and a Slack admin to set up ([Claude Tag overview](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/overview)).

**The cloud move changed local workflows.** "Claude Cowork still can't persistently reference a local dir, broken ever since they moved to their cloud project system," one user wrote on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49821965) in September.

**Too many surfaces.** Before the merge, the desktop app had Chat, Cowork, and Claude Code, each behaving differently, and one commenter described a routine web task that would work on one surface and not another ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49730104)).

**One model vendor.** Cowork runs on Anthropic's models and draws on the same usage limits as chat and Claude Code. Teams that want to mix providers, or keep their procedures independent of one lab, look elsewhere.

## The alternatives, ranked

### 1. Construct

**Best for:** founders and small teams whose work repeats every week and crosses several apps.

Construct gives each agent a persistent cloud workspace: files, live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, its own email address, and a Calendar of scheduled jobs. It connects to Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable through a live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools. You reach it from the web, Slack, Telegram, Discord slash commands, or by email.

The difference from Cowork is where the work lives and who can see it. Finished work lands in the workspace as it is produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29, and the account manager can open the same report the founder asked for.

What a Cowork user gets with it:

- **A shared team workspace.** Invite teammates as Owner, Admin, or Member. There is no member limit and no per-seat fee; usage draws on the owner's plan.
- **Claude, or any other model.** Pro takes your own keys for Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, either as a fallback after bundled limits or for every run. Your keys bill you directly.
- **Recurring jobs on a Calendar.** Save a run as a workflow and schedule it: 3 scheduled tasks on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro. Schedules run server-side with every laptop closed.
- **Memory you control.** Tell it once that a client wants figures in euros. If that changes, open the stored fact and correct it.
- **Internal apps.** Ask for a review queue or a client tracker and Construct builds it as a private app in the workspace.
- **Channels beyond one app.** Mention it in Slack, message it on Telegram or Discord, or email its inbox. Only mail from your own address starts a run.

Two things to set up deliberately. Construct works in its cloud workspace rather than on your computer, so put the files a job needs in Files once and every later run can use them. And workflows run straight through without an approval step, so for customer emails and payments, ask for drafts and act on them yourself.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Lite is $9 a month for up to 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Starter is $59 with 6x Lite usage, up to 5 agents, 150 steps per task, and 10 scheduled tasks. Pro is $299 with 32x Lite usage, up to 15 agents, up to 1,000 steps per task, 50 scheduled tasks, and BYOK, with a 7-day trial ([Construct pricing](/pricing/)). ### 3. Microsoft Copilot Cowork

**Best for:** companies that run on Microsoft 365.

Microsoft's Cowork carries out long-running, multi-tool tasks in Microsoft's cloud, so tasks keep running with your laptop off. It became generally available worldwide on June 16, 2026, running on Anthropic's Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 4.6 at launch ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/06/16/copilot-cowork-is-now-generally-available/)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** it needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, then usage is billed in Copilot Credits from $0.01 each. Metered cost depends on model use, retrieval, tool calls, and runtime.

## An illustrative example: the Monday client report

This is an illustrative workflow, not a customer case study or a measured test.

A four-person agency uses Cowork for a Monday client report: pull last week's numbers from HubSpot, draft a summary, save it to a shared folder. It works for the founder, but the account manager cannot see the runs, and the report breaks when a teammate changes the folder.

**In Construct:** connect HubSpot, save the report template in Files, and ask the agent for this week's report with the source of each figure. Check two weeks by hand, then save it as a workflow and schedule it on the Calendar. Invite the account manager to the team workspace as a Member so they can open the report and the run history. Keep the client email as a draft.

## Already using Cowork? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to cancel Claude. Keep Cowork for your own one-off tasks and give Construct the recurring job your team needs to see.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to run Construct on your own Anthropic key.
2. **Copy the prompt** from your Cowork task or scheduled task into a Construct chat, and upload the files it uses to Files.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** and invite the teammates who need the output.

There is no import: Cowork's history, projects, and learned context do not transfer, so write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read.

## When might Cowork still fit?

- You work alone, already pay for Claude, and your tasks are one-off.
- Your tasks depend on folders that live on your own computer.
- Your whole team is already on Claude Team and works in Slack threads with Claude Tag.

For a team's recurring work that should leave shared files, run on a schedule, and stay independent of any one model vendor, Construct is the better fit.
