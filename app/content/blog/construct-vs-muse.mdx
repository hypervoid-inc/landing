Construct gives your whole team an AI employee with its own workspace, files, schedules, and email address from $9 a month, while Meta's Muse for Small Business is a personal agent on one Meta account, offered only to adults in the US and Canada. Muse is free to start, with Power at $20 and Max at $100 a month metered in weekly tokens, and Meta does not publish the free tier's limit in numbers.

For most founders and small teams, Construct is the better choice. Its work lives in a workspace your team can open, it reaches the Gmail, CRM, and tracker work that happens outside Meta's apps, and a job that works once can be saved and put on a schedule. Already using Muse for your Instagram and Facebook ads? Keep it there and give Construct the reports, follow-ups, and trackers everyone else on the team depends on. The two run side by side.

## Why choose Construct over Muse for Small Business?

Construct is built for a team's recurring work across many apps, with published plan limits from $9; Muse is one person's agent inside Meta's apps.

- **One workspace for the whole team.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles. A team workspace has no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. Meta's Muse pages describe a personal agent and mention no team plan or staff accounts.
- **Built for the apps outside Meta.** Construct's live integration catalog covers Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable, and you can add your own MCP tools.
- **Recurring jobs that run themselves.** Save a task as a workflow and put it on the Calendar. Schedules run on Construct's servers with your laptop closed: 3 on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro.
- **Limits you can plan around.** Each plan states its agents, steps per task, and scheduled tasks. Muse meters tokens per week, and its free-tier limit is not published in numbers.
- **Work that outlives the chat.** Reports and spreadsheets land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 behind.
- **Your model, and more ways to reach it.** Pro runs on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI. You can message the agent on the web, in Slack, Telegram, or Discord, or by email.

## What is Meta Muse for Small Business?

Muse for Small Business is Meta's personal Muse agent connected to business apps and to your Facebook, Instagram, and Meta ad accounts, launched on September 29, 2026. Muse itself launched on September 8 and runs on "Muse Secure VM, a dedicated secure computer with its own browser," on Meta's Muse Spark model, in the Muse app, on muse.ai, and in WhatsApp ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).

[Meta's Muse for Small Business announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/), last updated October 8, 2026, lists:

- **Connectors:** Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Granola, HighLevel, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Lovable, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe, and Zoom, plus custom connectors.
- **Meta accounts:** Instagram professional account analytics, Facebook Pages, and Meta ad accounts.
- **Who can use it:** people 18 and over in the US and Canada ([Muse for Small Business](https://muse.ai/business)).
- **Approvals:** "Nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your approval." For purchases, Meta's [security write-up](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse) says Muse issues a single-use card number tied to one merchant and amount.

Meta does not review custom connectors: its help center says it "doesn't review custom connectors or how they use your information" ([Meta Help Center](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1687253048996149/)). We found no new Muse for Small Business features announced between October 1 and October 11, 2026.

If you are still weighing several options, our guide to [Muse for Small Business alternatives](/blog/muse-for-small-business-alternatives/) compares nine. This page is the head-to-head with Construct.

Not in the pages Meta publishes: Muse is tied to one Meta account, and Meta describes no way to invite an employee or a bookkeeper into the same agent. ### It reaches the apps a small business uses outside Meta

Construct's live integration catalog includes Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable, and you can add custom MCP tools when a connector does not exist. When a structured connection is not enough, the agent still has a live browser and a terminal in its workspace, and you can save an export from your store or ads account to Files for it to work from.

Each Construct agent also has its own email address. Forward an invoice or a vendor thread from your own address and the agent starts work on it, without the job running through a personal inbox. Our guides to [giving an AI agent its own email address](/blog/ai-agent-email-address/) and [letting an agent use your Gmail safely](/blog/ai-agent-gmail-access-safely/) cover the setup.

### Channels with their own access rules

Message Construct from the web, @mention it in Slack, text it on Telegram, use it in Discord, or email it. Access is set per channel: for a Slack, Telegram, or Discord install, allow and deny lists decide who can use the agent, and an install with no rules admits only the person who installed it.

### Your choice of model

Muse runs on Meta's Muse Spark. Construct Pro can run on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, as the [Construct terms](/terms/) explain, so your procedures and files stay put if you change providers. Your keys bill you directly when they are used.

## How to test both on the same job

The fairest way to choose is to give both the same real job for two weeks and compare the results. We have not published a measured run, so use this checklist on your own data:

1. **Pick one recurring job,** such as a Monday summary of last week's orders, refunds, top five products, and ad spend, saved as a dated report, with "do not send anything to anyone" in the prompt.
2. **Use the identical prompt** on Muse for Small Business and on Construct, with the same store and ad data on each.
3. **Record setup minutes** and minutes to the first usable report.
4. **Count approval prompts, errors, and retries.**
5. **Check week 2:** did it compare correctly with week 1?
6. **Check where the report was saved** and whether a teammate could open it without your login.
7. **Note the cost:** plan price, plus any usage meter warnings.

On the Construct side, save last week's report and the format you want in the workspace, connect the apps the job needs, and save a weekly ad export to Files for the Meta numbers. Ask the agent to compare this week with last week and name the source of each figure. Check the first two reports, then save the job as a workflow and put it on the Calendar, where it uses one of Lite's 3 scheduled tasks. The [workflow guide](/blog/ai-workflow-automation/) walks through each step.

## Already using Muse? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up Muse. Start with the one job your team needs to see, or the one that lives outside Meta's apps.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the instructions** you gave Muse into a Construct chat, and download the memory and files you want to keep (Meta says you can) into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: Muse's memory, connectors, and approval settings do not transfer. Write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing or anything that spends money, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Muse still fit?

- Your selling and advertising happen almost entirely on Instagram and Facebook, and you want first-party access to Meta ad accounts.
- You want approval on every send and spend, plus single-use card numbers for purchases, built in today.
- You run the business alone from your phone and WhatsApp.

For recurring work that a team shares, that crosses Gmail, a CRM, and the web, and that should run on a schedule, Construct is the better fit.
