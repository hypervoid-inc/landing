Construct gives your business an AI employee with its own workspace, files, schedules, and email address from $9 a month, and your whole team can work with it from one place. Muse for Small Business is Meta's personal agent: it runs on one person's Meta account, is offered only to adults in the US and Canada, and meters usage in weekly tokens with free-tier limits Meta does not publish in numbers.

For most founders and small teams, Construct is the better choice. Its work lives in a workspace your team can open, it reaches the Gmail, CRM, and tracker work that happens outside Meta's apps, and a job that works once can be saved and put on a schedule. Already using Muse for your Instagram and Facebook ads? Keep it there and give Construct the reports, follow-ups, and trackers everyone else on the team depends on. The two run side by side.

## Why choose Construct over Muse for Small Business?

- **One workspace for the whole team.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles. A team workspace has no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. The Muse pages we read describe a personal agent and mention no team plan.
- **Built for the apps outside Meta.** Construct's live integration catalog covers Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable, and you can add your own MCP tools.
- **Recurring jobs that run themselves.** Save a task as a workflow and put it on the Calendar. Schedules run on Construct's servers with your laptop closed: 3 on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro.
- **Limits you can plan around.** Each plan states its agents, steps per task, and scheduled tasks. Muse meters tokens per week, and its free-tier limits are not published in numbers.
- **Work that outlives the chat.** Reports and spreadsheets land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 behind.
- **Your model, and more ways to reach it.** Pro runs on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI. You can message the agent on the web, in Slack, Telegram, or Discord, or by email.

## What is Muse for Small Business?

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent, launched on September 8, 2026. It runs on "Muse Secure VM," a dedicated virtual machine for the agent and your data, and works in the iOS and Android apps, on muse.ai, and in WhatsApp ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)).

On September 29, Meta launched [Muse for Small Business](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/), the same agent connected to business tools:

- **Connectors:** Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Granola, HighLevel, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Lovable, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe, and Zoom, plus custom connectors.
- **Meta accounts:** Instagram professional accounts, Facebook Pages, and Meta ad accounts.
- **Who can use it:** people 18 and over in the US and Canada, signed in with a Meta account ([Muse for Small Business](https://muse.ai/business)).
- **Approvals:** nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your approval, according to Meta's [security write-up](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse).

Meta does not review custom connectors: its help center says it "doesn't review custom connectors or how they use your information" ([Meta Help Center](https://www.meta.com/help/artificial-intelligence/1687253048996149/)).

If you are still weighing several options, our guide to [Muse for Small Business alternatives](/blog/muse-for-small-business-alternatives/) compares nine. This page is the head-to-head with Construct.

## Where does Construct give you more control?

Both products give an agent a computer of its own. Construct also gives your team a shared place for the work, a way to make it repeat, and a reach beyond Meta's apps.

### The work lives in a workspace your team can open

Muse is tied to one Meta account. The pages we read describe no way to invite an employee or a bookkeeper into the same agent.

Construct's [team workspaces](/use-cases/team-workspaces/) hold the agent, files, apps, and conversations together, and people join with roles instead of sharing a login. Finished work lands in Files as it is produced, so the next person, or next week's run, starts from what already exists.

### Recurring jobs are saved, scheduled, and repeatable

Construct is built around jobs that repeat. Once a task produces the right result, you save it as a workflow and put it on the Calendar, which runs it on Construct's servers whether or not anyone is online and keeps each run's history. The [scheduling page](/use-cases/scheduling/) shows how runs are kept.

### Channels with their own access rules

Message Construct from the web, @mention it in Slack, text it on Telegram, use it in Discord, or email it. Access is set per channel: for a Slack, Telegram, or Discord install, an allow list decides who can use the agent, and an install with no rules admits nobody.

### Your choice of model

Muse runs on Meta's models. Construct Pro can run on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, as the [Construct terms](/terms/) explain, so your procedures and files stay put if you change providers. Your keys bill you directly when they are used.

## Already using Muse? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up Muse. Start with the one job your team needs to see, or the one that lives outside Meta's apps.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the instructions** you gave Muse into a Construct chat, and download the memory and files you want to keep (Meta says you can) into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: Muse's memory, connectors, and approval settings do not transfer. Write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing or anything that spends money, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Muse still fit?

- Your selling and advertising happen almost entirely on Instagram and Facebook, and you want first-party access to Meta ad accounts.
- You want approval on every send and spend, plus single-use card numbers for purchases, built in today.
- You run the business alone from your phone and WhatsApp.

For recurring work that a team shares, that crosses Gmail, a CRM, and the web, and that should run on a schedule, Construct is the better fit.

## Which should you choose?

Choose Construct. It gives a small business one AI employee the whole team can use, with stated plan limits from $9, saved workflows on a schedule, and finished files in a shared workspace. If Muse already runs your Meta ads, keep it for that and let Construct handle the reports, follow-ups, and trackers that live everywhere else.

Pick the report, follow-up list, or tracker that keeps sliding into your evening, give Construct the inputs, and judge the saved result. Start on [Lite at $9](/pricing/), or try Pro free for seven days.
