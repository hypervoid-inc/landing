Construct gives every agent a persistent workspace with files, a browser, a terminal, and Calendar schedules from $9 a month, a fixed price with published plan limits. Manus bills by credits that do not roll over, from $20 a month on Pro, and its per-task sandbox closes when the task ends; a persistent machine is a Cloud Computer add-on at $30 or $50 a month more ([Manus pricing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus), [Cloud Computer billing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).

For founders and small teams with recurring work, Construct is the better choice. The Monday report can open last Monday's file without an add-on, the plan is a fixed monthly price, the whole team shares one workspace without per-seat fees, and you can read and correct what the agent remembers. Already using Manus? Keep it for one-off builds and give Construct the jobs that repeat.

## Why choose Construct over Manus?

- **A persistent workspace on every plan, from $9.** Files, workflows, memory, and run history stay in one place. On Manus, keeping working files on a machine between tasks means adding a Cloud Computer.
- **A fixed monthly price, not credits that reset.** Construct plans set usage, agents, and scheduled tasks at a fixed monthly price with published limits. Manus monthly credits do not roll over, and you cannot start a new task at zero.
- **One team workspace, no seats.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles; there is no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. Manus Team starts at $20 per seat.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or forget it. A memory view is not documented in the Manus pages we reviewed.
- **More places to hand it work.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and the agent's own email inbox.
- **Your model, or your own keys.** Construct Pro accepts keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, and xAI. Manus manages model choice itself.

## What is Manus?

Manus runs your request as a task in a sandbox with a browser and tools, and returns a report, slides, a website, code, or media. Manus 2.0 was announced on September 28, 2026 ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0)).

- **Automations:** tasks run on a schedule or start when something happens in a connected service, such as a new email, a calendar event, a Slack message, or a Notion update (same source).
- **Cloud Computer:** a paid add-on, a persistent virtual machine that runs apps, bots, and scripts 24/7 with a dedicated public IP ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392111-what-is-the-cloud-computer)).
- **Desktop:** Manus Studio works with local files and, in an authorized session, in a separate workspace on your computer ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/17190150-what-is-new-in-manus-2-0)).
- **Channels:** web, desktop, and mobile apps, email from approved senders ([Mail Manus](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/12059745-how-can-i-use-mail-manus)), and @Manus in Slack ([Manus in Slack](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/14431752-chatting-with-manus-agent-on-slack-channels-dms-and-file-delivery)).

Sources: [Construct pricing](/pricing/), [Manus membership pricing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus), and [Cloud Computer billing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing). Manus offers a 17% discount for annual billing.

How Manus credits work: they are consumed by model tokens, virtual machines, third-party APIs, and task complexity. Monthly credits do not roll over, and you cannot start new tasks while your available credits are at or below zero. Free daily credits are capped at 1,500 a month and work only with Manus 2.0 lite ([Manus credit rules](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711097-what-are-the-rules-for-credits-consumption-and-how-can-i-obtain-them)).

What this means for your budget:

- **Recurring work with files that persist:** on Construct this is Lite at $9. On Manus, keeping working files on a machine between tasks means Pro plus a Cloud Computer, $50 or $70 a month.
- **A busy month:** a Construct plan is a fixed monthly price with published plan limits. On Manus, a heavy month spends credits faster, and unused credits do not carry over to a quiet one.
- **A team of five:** Manus Team starts at $100 a month for five seats. Construct Starter at $59 covers up to 5 agents for a whole workspace with no member limit.

## Where does Construct give you more control?

Both products give an agent a computer. Construct also keeps that computer's work for you, puts it on a schedule you can inspect, and lets you correct the facts it relies on.

### Keep the workspace without an add-on

On Manus, the per-task sandbox closes when the task completes and outputs stay in chat history. The Cloud Computer adds persistence, and its working files are deleted if you stop paying for it ([What is the Cloud Computer?](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392111-what-is-the-cloud-computer)). On Construct, the workspace is the product on every plan. Every run saves its output to workspace files as it goes, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29, and this Monday's report can open last Monday's file.

### Put recurring work on a schedule you can inspect

Construct turns a job that worked into a saved workflow with agent steps and connected-app actions, then schedules it on the Calendar, where it runs server-side with your laptop closed. A scheduled item can run an agent prompt, an app action, or a whole workflow, and it keeps its result history. The [scheduled tasks guide](/blog/ai-agent-scheduled-tasks/) walks through the setup.

### See and fix what the agent remembers

In Construct, memory is something you can read. If a client changes its reporting format, you correct the stored fact once and every later run uses it. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) shows how.

### Reach it from more places

Message Construct from the web app, @mention it in Slack, text it on Telegram, use it in Discord, or email its own address. In Slack, each install has an access policy with an allow list and a deny list. Teams that coordinate on Telegram or Discord are covered without a new app to learn.

### Pick the model, or bring your own keys

Construct Pro can run on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, either as a fallback after bundled limits or for every run, as the [Construct terms](/terms/) explain. Your keys bill you directly when they are used.

## Already using Manus? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up Manus. Start with the Automation you run every week, or the one whose output your team needs to find again.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the Automation prompt** into a Construct chat, and move the files it needs, including any you download from chat or a Cloud Computer, into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog, or add your own MCP tools.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files, then run it again to see it compare against the first file.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: Manus credits, published sites, and Cloud Computer processes do not transfer, so back up anything on a Cloud Computer before you cancel it. Two things to set up deliberately. Construct runs start from the Calendar, by hand, or from an agent, so an event-triggered Automation becomes a frequent schedule that checks for new items, or an email you forward to the agent from your own address. And Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Manus still fit?

- You want a free plan for occasional one-off research, decks, or sites.
- A job must start the moment an event happens in a connected service.
- You need a bot, scraper, or server running 24/7, or a public website with a custom domain.

For recurring team work that needs files, schedules, correctable memory, and a bill a small business can predict, Construct is the better fit.

Choose Construct. It keeps a persistent workspace on every plan from $9, runs your weekly jobs on the Calendar for a fixed monthly price, lets the whole team in without seats, and keeps memory you can correct.
