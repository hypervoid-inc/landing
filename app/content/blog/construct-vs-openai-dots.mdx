Construct gives you an AI employee with its own workspace, files, schedules, and email address from $9 a month, and it runs on the model you choose. ChatGPT Dots, OpenAI's new always-on agents, need a ChatGPT Pro plan at $100 or $200 a month, run only on OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, and are not offered to personal Pro subscribers in the UK, the EU, or Switzerland.

For most founders and small teams, Construct is the better buy. It costs less than a tenth as much to start, keeps every finished file in a workspace your team can open, lets you read and correct what the agent remembers, and keeps your work independent of any one AI lab. Already paying for ChatGPT Pro? Keep your dot for personal follow-ups and give Construct the recurring team work. The two run side by side.

## Why choose Construct over ChatGPT Dots?

- **$9 a month instead of $100 to $200.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. There is nothing else to subscribe to first.
- **Your model, or your own keys.** Construct runs on bundled models, and Pro lets you bring keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI. Dots run on GPT-6 Astra only.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or delete it. The Dots coverage we reviewed documents no way to see or edit what a dot has learned.
- **Work that outlives the chat.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 behind.
- **One workspace for the whole team, no seats.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles. A team workspace has no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan.
- **It answers where your team already talks.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and its own email inbox.

## What is ChatGPT Dots?

OpenAI launched dots at DevDay on September 29, 2026. A dot is an agent inside ChatGPT that you give a goal; it works on its own cloud computer and browser, using the apps you connect, and brings the result back for review ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-always-on-ai-agents-in-chatgpt-with-their-own-cloud-computers/)).

- **Model:** GPT-6 Astra, with a dedicated cloud computer and browser per dot ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)).
- **Apps:** more than 4,000 through OpenAI's plugins ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-always-on-ai-agents-in-chatgpt-with-their-own-cloud-computers/)).
- **Channels:** ChatGPT on desktop, web, and mobile, plus Slack and Microsoft Teams ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)).
- **Background work:** read-only "proactive research" while you are away, so a dot cannot send messages or change accounts during that time ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-always-on-ai-agents-in-chatgpt-with-their-own-cloud-computers/)).

Dots are a different product from chatting with ChatGPT. For the chat assistant itself, read [Construct vs ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini](/blog/construct-vs-chatgpt/).

## Where does Construct give you more control?

Both products give an agent a computer. Construct also gives you a say in the model it runs on, the facts it relies on, and where the finished work ends up.

### Pick the model, or bring your own keys

A dot runs on GPT-6 Astra. Construct Pro can run on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, either as a fallback after bundled limits or for every run, as the [Construct terms](/terms/) explain. Use OpenAI through your own key today and switch providers next quarter without moving a single file. Your keys bill you directly when they are used.

### See and fix what the agent remembers

Fortune reports that dots learn your preferences and remember past conversations. The coverage we reviewed shows no way to open that memory, check it, or delete one wrong fact.

In Construct, memory is something you can read. If a client changes its reporting format, you correct the stored fact once and every later job uses it. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) shows how.

### Keep the work in a workspace, not a chat thread

Every Construct run saves its output to workspace files as it goes. Files, schedules, and run history sit in one place your team can open next week without scrolling back through a conversation. When several people review the same output, Construct can build a private workspace app, such as a review queue with owners and statuses; the [internal-tools guide](/blog/build-internal-tools-with-construct/) explains how.

### Reach it from more places

Message Construct from the web app, @mention it in Slack, text it on Telegram, use it in Discord, or email its own address. Teams on Telegram or Discord, and clients who send work by email, are covered without anyone needing a ChatGPT account.

### Stay independent of one AI lab

Dots lives inside ChatGPT, so access depends on your ChatGPT plan and region. Construct is your own workspace on the models you pick. Your procedures, files, and memory stay put when a lab changes its plans or prices.

## Already using Dots? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up your dot. Start by moving the one job your dot handles least reliably, or the one your teammates need to see.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the goal and instructions** you gave your dot into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: a dot's learned preferences do not transfer. Write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Dots still fit?

- You already pay for ChatGPT Pro 200 and only want a personal assistant inside ChatGPT.
- Your company runs on Microsoft Teams, or you want to talk to your agent by voice call.
- You need built-in approval rules on every account action today.

For recurring team work that needs files, schedules, a choice of model, and a price a small business can justify, Construct is the better fit.

## Which should you choose?

Choose Construct. It starts at $9 instead of $100 to $200, runs on the model you pick, keeps memory you can correct, and leaves every finished file in a workspace your team shares. If you already have a dot, keep it for personal follow-ups and let Construct run the work your team depends on.

Pick the report, price check, or tracker that keeps slipping into your evening, give Construct the inputs, and judge the saved result. Start on [Lite at $9](/pricing/), or try Pro free for seven days.
