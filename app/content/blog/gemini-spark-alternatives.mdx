## Why choose Construct over Gemini Spark?

- **Its own plan, from $9.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. It does not hinge on a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription.
- **Google and everything else in one job.** Construct reaches Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable through its live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools when a connector does not exist.

- **Your choice of model.** Spark runs on Gemini 3.5. Construct Pro can run on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI.

## What is Gemini Spark?

Gemini Spark is Google's always-on agent inside the Gemini app. Google announced it at I/O on May 19, 2026 as a cloud-based agent that runs on Gemini 3.5 and keeps working in the background ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/next-evolution-gemini-app/)). Press coverage describes it running on dedicated Google Cloud virtual machines ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317144/20260525/gemini-spark-googles-24-7-cloud-ai-agent-now-executes-tasks-third-party-apps.htm)).

Spark connects natively with Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, YouTube, and Google Maps ([Gemini Spark page](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/)). A June update added a macOS app and connected apps including Canva, Dropbox, Instacart, OpenTable, and Zillow Rentals ([Google, June 30, 2026](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-spark-updates-june-2026/)). You can have up to 15 tasks running at a time, and Spark asks for confirmation before sending communications, modifying your data, making purchases, or submitting web forms ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)).

## Can you use Gemini Spark with a Google Workspace work account?

Not today, for most people. Google's help page says you must "sign in to the Gemini app with a personal Google Account," and work or school accounts are not currently supported ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)).

Google has announced a business version: on May 19, 2026, Google Workspace said "Gemini Spark in Google Workspace will be available soon in preview for business customers in the Gemini app" ([Google Workspace blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/workspace/workspace-updates/)), and the Spark page mentions "select business users" ([Gemini Spark page](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/)). We found no general availability date for Workspace accounts on the Google pages we checked.

That gap is what sends many small teams looking. A company on Google Workspace keeps its email, calendar, and files under work accounts, so a personal-account agent cannot see the inbox that matters.

## Is Gemini Spark available in the UK or EU?

No. Google lists Spark as "available wherever Gemini Apps are supported, except in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom" ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)). One availability tracker counts that as 33 excluded countries: the 30 EEA countries plus Nigeria, Switzerland, and the UK ([AI Agents Library](https://aiagentslibrary.com/blog/gemini-spark-availability)). A May report expected EU and UK availability in Q3 2026 ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317144/20260525/gemini-spark-googles-24-7-cloud-ai-agent-now-executes-tasks-third-party-apps.htm)); as of October 1, 2026, Google's help page still lists those regions as excluded.

## How much does Gemini Spark cost?

Spark has no separate price; it comes with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)). It launched as a beta for US Ultra subscribers, with Ultra reported at $100 a month ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317144/20260525/gemini-spark-googles-24-7-cloud-ai-agent-now-executes-tasks-third-party-apps.htm)). In late July it came to the $20 Pro tier ([Chrome Unboxed, July 27, 2026](https://chromeunboxed.com/googles-agentic-ai-gemini-spark-is-now-available-on-the-20-pro-tier-in-the-us/)), and one tracker dates the global Pro expansion to July 30 ([AI Agents Library](https://aiagentslibrary.com/blog/gemini-spark-availability)). Older articles quoting only the Ultra price are out of date.

## Why people look for a Gemini Spark alternative

Three reasons come up repeatedly: a work account, a location, or work that lives outside Google.

- **The account rule.** "Many new Gemini functionalities are only accessible using a consumer account instead of a Workspace account," one Hacker News commenter wrote in July ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48994600)).
- **The region.** Another, writing from Europe, listed Spark among features that "get to Europe later" ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48463560)).
- **The scope.** Spark is built around Google's apps plus a list of partner connections. If your CRM, project tracker, or support queue lives elsewhere, you want an agent whose home is not one vendor's suite. Our comparison of [Construct with Microsoft Copilot and Google Workspace AI](/blog/construct-vs-copilot/) covers that in more depth.

### 1. Construct

**Best for:** founders and small teams that want recurring work across Gmail, Google Calendar, and non-Google apps, with the files and schedule kept in one place.

Construct gives each agent a persistent cloud workspace: files, live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, its own email address, and a Calendar of scheduled jobs. Calendar schedules run on Construct's servers, so the 8:00 digest runs whether or not your laptop is open. It reaches Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable through its live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools when a catalog entry does not exist ([Construct product guide](/blog/ai-employee/)). You can assign work from the web, Slack (@mentions and DMs), Telegram, Discord, or by emailing the agent's own address.

Where Spark is a personal assistant inside the Gemini app, Construct is a shared employee with its own workspace:

- **A whole team, one plan.** Teammates join as Owner, Admin, or Member, with no member limit and no per-seat fee.
- **The model you pick.** On Pro, bring your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI.
- **Its own inbox.** Even Lite includes an agent email address, so the agent can send you the digest from its own address.

### 2. Claude with Cowork

**Best for:** people already paying for Claude, including on a Workspace account in the UK or EU.

Anthropic's Google Workspace connectors let Claude search and send Gmail, manage Google Calendar, and read and save Drive files, asking for approval before each of these actions by default ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10166901-use-google-workspace-connectors)). On Team and Enterprise plans an Owner must enable them first, and if your Google Workspace admin restricts apps, the admin marks Claude as "Trusted" in the Google Admin console. Cowork's scheduled tasks are on all paid plans and run remotely while your computer is asleep or the desktop app is closed ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)). Anthropic lists the United Kingdom, EU countries such as Germany, France, and Ireland, and Switzerland as supported ([Anthropic supported countries](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Pro is $20 billed monthly, Max starts at $100, Team Standard is $25 per seat and Team Premium $125 per seat billed monthly ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). The pricing page now says "Claude Cowork is now just Claude."

**Limits:** agentic work draws on the same plan usage as chat, and a task that needs local files or apps runs only on your machine.

### 3. ChatGPT dots

**Best for:** businesses already on ChatGPT, including in the UK and EU through Business Premium.

OpenAI launched dots on September 29, 2026. Each dot runs on GPT-6 Astra, works from its own cloud computer, can connect to more than 4,000 apps through plugins, and can be messaged in Slack and Teams ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)). Custom rules decide what a dot may do on its own, and sensitive operations such as password changes always need a human. For Pro users, "the eligible markets do not currently include the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the UK," while Business Premium users get dots across supported regions.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** the first dot is included in Pro and Business Premium. A Business Premium seat is $125 per user monthly or $100 billed annually, and Pro comes in $100, $200, and $500 tiers, according to a pricing guide updated September 9, 2026 ([Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/chatgpt-pricing)). {/* TODO: verify ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium prices on chatgpt.com/pricing (blocked our fetch on October 1). */}

### 4. Microsoft Copilot Cowork

**Best for:** companies that run on Microsoft 365 rather than Google Workspace.

Copilot Cowork runs long, multi-tool tasks in Microsoft's cloud, so tasks keep running when your laptop is off. It became generally available to Microsoft 365 Copilot customers worldwide on June 16, 2026, running on Anthropic's Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 4.6 at launch ([Microsoft 365 blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/06/16/copilot-cowork-is-now-generally-available/)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** it requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, then usage is metered in Copilot Credits at $0.01 each on pay-as-you-go, with a discounted prepaid option. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business is listed at $25.20 per user on a monthly subscription, or from $18 per user a month on an annual subscription, down from $21 ([Microsoft 365 Copilot pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing)).

**Limits:** metered cost depends on model use, retrieval, tool calls, and runtime, so budget from a pilot. It is built for Microsoft 365, not Google Workspace.

### 5. Manus

**Best for:** one-off research, analysis, and build tasks.

Manus 2.0, released September 28, 2026, added a dedicated Cloud Computer and event-triggered automations, so a new email, a calendar event, a Slack message, or a Notion update can start a task ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0)). Apps run on web, macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Free gives 300 credits a day and 1 concurrent task. Pro starts at $20 a month for 4,000 credits, 20 concurrent tasks, and 20 scheduled tasks, and a $40 tier doubles the credits. Team starts at $20 per seat ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)). The Cloud Computer is an extra $30 or $50 a month ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)). Prices are set in USD for all users, payable in local currency ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711125-what-is-the-pricing-unit-for-manus-globally-can-i-pay-in-local-currency)).

**Limits:** if you stop paying for the Cloud Computer, "the persistent virtual machine will be shut down" and its files and databases are deleted. Manus resumed independent operations on September 1, 2026, and some users had to back up and restore data during that transition ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/manus-resumes-independent-operations)).

**Limits:** "Anyone who uses Lindy takes a seat," including someone who only mentions it in Slack, and when credits run out, "Lindy pauses credit-using actions" until the next cycle. The pricing page lists computer use among core features, while the plan documentation shows it on Pro, Max, and Enterprise only ([Lindy docs](https://docs.lindy.ai/pricing)). **Price, checked October 1, 2026:** included with Cursor Pro at $20 a month and Cursor Teams at $40 per user ([Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)), and with eligible SuperGrok and X Premium+ accounts linked to Cursor ([Cursor Help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)).

**Limits:** usage limits are described in relative tiers, not numbers, and the docs we read do not mention regional availability or Google Workspace accounts. Our [Grok Bot comparison](/blog/grokbot-alternative/) goes further.

## An illustrative example: a morning inbox digest in Construct

This is an illustrative workflow, not a customer case study or a measured test.

A two-person design studio runs on Google Workspace. The founder wants what Spark promises: every weekday at 8:00, a short list of client emails that need a reply and the day's meetings with a line of context for each. Spark is ruled out by the work account.

1. **Connect Gmail and Google Calendar** from Construct's integration catalog.
2. **Give the agent the job:** "Read yesterday's and this morning's Gmail. List client emails that still need a reply, oldest first, with a one-line summary. Then list today's Calendar events with who is attending. Save it as a dated file in Files and email it to me from your inbox."
3. **Check the first few files.** Correct anything it gets wrong, and Construct keeps the correction as memory you can read.
4. **Schedule it** on the Calendar for weekdays at 8:00. Lite's 3 scheduled tasks cover it.

Each morning's digest stays in Files, so you can see why an email was included and compare one week with the next. The test that matters is the tenth morning, not the first. In Claude, the same prompt can run as a Cowork scheduled task once your Workspace admin has trusted Claude.

## Already using Spark? Add Construct in 15 minutes

Keep Spark for personal errands in your Google account and give Construct the work your team shares.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test longer runs and your own model keys.
2. **Copy the task instructions** you gave Spark into a Construct chat, and move any source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs, Google and non-Google, from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import from Spark, so write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for client emails, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.
