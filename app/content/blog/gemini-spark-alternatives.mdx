**Gemini Spark only works with a personal Google account on a Google AI Pro or Ultra plan (Pro is about $20 a month in the US), and Google's help page lists it as unavailable in the UK, the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and Nigeria.** If you have a Workspace work account or live in Europe, the closest alternatives are Claude with scheduled Cowork tasks (from $20 a month), ChatGPT dots on a Business Premium seat ($125 a month), Microsoft Copilot Cowork for Microsoft 365 shops, and Construct (from $9 a month) for recurring work across Gmail, Google Calendar, and other apps.

Manus, Lindy, and Grok Bot round out the list for people who want an always-on agent outside Google's apps altogether. We build Construct, one of the seven, and we say below where each of the others is the better choice.

## What is Gemini Spark?

Gemini Spark is Google's 24/7 personal AI agent inside the Gemini app. Google announced it at I/O on May 19, 2026 as an agent that "runs on Gemini 3.5" and, "because it is a cloud-based agent," keeps working in the background ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/next-evolution-gemini-app/)). Press coverage describes it running on dedicated Google Cloud virtual machines ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317144/20260525/gemini-spark-googles-24-7-cloud-ai-agent-now-executes-tasks-third-party-apps.htm)).

Its strength is depth inside Google. Spark "can connect natively" with Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, YouTube, and Google Maps ([Gemini Spark page](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/)). A June update added the macOS app and connected apps including Canva, Dropbox, Instacart, OpenTable, and Zillow Rentals ([Google, June 30, 2026](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-spark-updates-june-2026/)).

Google's help page adds the working limits: you can have up to 15 tasks running at a time, and Spark asks for confirmation before sending communications, modifying your data, making purchases, or submitting web forms ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)).

## Can you use Gemini Spark with a Google Workspace work account?

Not today, for most people. Google's help page says you must "sign in to the Gemini app with a personal Google Account," and that work or school accounts are not currently supported ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)).

Google has signalled a business version. On May 19, 2026, Google Workspace said "Gemini Spark in Google Workspace will be available soon in preview for business customers in the Gemini app" ([Google Workspace blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/workspace/workspace-updates/)), and the Spark product page mentions "select business users" ([Gemini Spark page](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/)). We found no general availability date for Workspace accounts on the Google pages we checked.

That gap is what sends many small teams looking. A company on Google Workspace keeps its email, calendar, and files under work accounts, so a personal-account agent cannot see the inbox that matters.

## Is Gemini Spark available in the UK or EU?

No. Google lists Spark as "available wherever Gemini Apps are supported, except in the European Economic Area, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom" ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)). One availability tracker counts that as 33 excluded countries: the 30 EEA countries plus Nigeria, Switzerland, and the UK ([AI Agents Library](https://aiagentslibrary.com/blog/gemini-spark-availability)).

A May report expected EU and UK availability in Q3 2026 ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317144/20260525/gemini-spark-googles-24-7-cloud-ai-agent-now-executes-tasks-third-party-apps.htm)). As of October 1, 2026, Google's help page still lists those regions as excluded.

## How much does Gemini Spark cost?

Spark has no separate price; it comes with a Google AI Pro or Ultra subscription ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)). Reports disagree on the entry price because the rollout changed. Spark launched as a beta for US Ultra subscribers, with Ultra reported at $100 a month ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317144/20260525/gemini-spark-googles-24-7-cloud-ai-agent-now-executes-tasks-third-party-apps.htm)). In late July it came to the $20 Pro tier ([Chrome Unboxed, July 27, 2026](https://chromeunboxed.com/googles-agentic-ai-gemini-spark-is-now-available-on-the-20-pro-tier-in-the-us/)), and one tracker dates the global Pro expansion to July 30 ([AI Agents Library](https://aiagentslibrary.com/blog/gemini-spark-availability)). Older articles quoting only the Ultra price are out of date.

## Why people look for a Gemini Spark alternative

Three reasons come up repeatedly: a work account, a location, or work that lives outside Google.

The account rule surprises people who assumed their paid Workspace plan would unlock Google's newest agent. One Hacker News commenter put it this way in July: "many new Gemini functionalities are only accessible using a consumer account instead of a Workspace account" ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48994600)). Another, writing from Europe, listed Spark among features that "get to Europe later" ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48463560)).

The third reason is scope. Spark is built around Google's apps plus a list of partner connections. If your CRM, project tracker, or support queue lives elsewhere, you want an agent whose home is not one vendor's suite. Our comparison of [Construct with Microsoft Copilot and Google Workspace AI](/blog/construct-vs-copilot/) covers that trade-off in more depth.

## The Gemini Spark alternatives, tool by tool

### Claude with Cowork

**Best for:** people on a Google Workspace work account, in the UK or EU, who want scheduled background work on Gmail, Calendar, and Drive.

Anthropic's Google Workspace connectors let Claude search and send Gmail, manage Google Calendar, and read and save Drive files. Claude "asks for your approval before each of these actions" by default ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10166901-use-google-workspace-connectors)). The connectors work with whichever Google account you connect. On Team and Enterprise plans an Owner must enable them first, and if your Google Workspace admin restricts apps, the admin marks Claude as "Trusted" in the Google Admin console.

For the Spark-like part, Cowork's scheduled tasks are available on all paid plans and "run remotely, so they run on their cadence even when your computer is asleep or the Claude Desktop app is closed" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)). A task that needs local files or apps runs only on your machine.

Anthropic lists the United Kingdom, EU countries such as Germany, France, and Ireland, and Switzerland as supported for Claude ([Anthropic supported countries](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Pro is $20 billed monthly, Max starts at $100, Team Standard is $25 per seat and Team Premium $125 per seat billed monthly ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). The pricing page now says "Claude Cowork is now just Claude," so you are buying Claude, not a separate add-on.

**Limits:** agentic work draws on the same plan usage as chat, and scheduled tasks that depend on your computer need it awake. Claude is an assistant you direct, not a workspace with its own inbox and file system for a team.

### ChatGPT dots

**Best for:** businesses already on ChatGPT that want an always-on agent, including in the UK and EU through Business Premium.

OpenAI launched dots on September 29, 2026. Each dot runs on GPT-6 Astra, works from its own cloud computer, can "connect to more than 4,000 apps through plugins," and can be messaged in Slack and Teams ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)). Custom rules decide what a dot may do on its own and what needs approval, and sensitive operations such as password changes always need a human.

The regional detail matters for this article. For Pro users, "the eligible markets do not currently include the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the UK," while Business Premium users get dots across supported regions ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)). So a UK company can get dots today on a business seat but not on a personal Pro plan.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** the first dot is included in Pro and Business Premium. A Business Premium seat is $125 per user monthly or $100 billed annually, and Pro comes in $100, $200, and $500 tiers, according to a pricing guide updated September 9, 2026 ([Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/chatgpt-pricing)). {/* TODO: verify ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium prices on chatgpt.com/pricing (blocked our fetch on October 1). */}

**Limits:** dots are a week old, rolling out gradually, and we could not fetch OpenAI's own announcement or help pages to check the fine print. The Next Web's launch coverage carries OpenAI's own caution that dots "can still make mistakes, so always review consequential work."

### Construct

**Best for:** founders and small teams that want recurring work across Gmail, Google Calendar, and non-Google apps, with the files and schedule kept in one place.

Construct gives each agent a persistent cloud workspace: files, live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, a native agent email address, and a Calendar of scheduled jobs. It reaches Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable through its live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools when a catalog entry does not exist ([Construct product guide](/blog/ai-employee/)). You can assign work from the web desktop, Slack, Telegram, Discord slash commands, or by emailing the agent.

Where it differs from Spark is ownership of the output. Finished work lands in the workspace as it is produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29 and the next run starts from what exists. Memory can be inspected, corrected, or forgotten. A job that works can be saved as a workflow and scheduled.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Lite is $9 a month for 2 agents, 50 steps per task, and 3 scheduled tasks. Starter is $59 with 6x Lite usage, up to 5 agents, and 10 scheduled tasks. Pro is $299 with 32x Lite usage, up to 15 agents, up to 1,000 steps per task, 50 scheduled tasks, and bring-your-own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, and xAI. Pro has a seven-day trial ([Construct pricing](/pricing/)).

**Limits:** Spark's Google integration is native and reaches Docs, Sheets, Slides, YouTube, and Maps; Construct reaches Google through connected apps, so check the catalog for the actions you need. Workflows are linear today, with no branching, delays, or approval gates. Activity summaries and tool records are useful operating records, not a complete audit log. {/* TODO: confirm Construct availability in the UK, EU, and Switzerland before publishing. */}

### Microsoft Copilot Cowork

**Best for:** companies that run on Microsoft 365 rather than Google Workspace.

If your Workspace question is really "what is the Microsoft equivalent," this is it. Microsoft says Copilot Cowork "executes complex, long-running, multi-tool tasks" and returns "a completed result, not just a draft." Cloud hosting means "your tasks keep running even when your laptop is off" ([Microsoft 365 blog, June 16, 2026](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/06/16/copilot-cowork-is-now-generally-available/)). It became generally available to Microsoft 365 Copilot customers "worldwide" on June 16, 2026, running on Anthropic's Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 4.6 at launch.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** it requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot license, then usage is metered in Copilot Credits at $0.01 each on pay-as-you-go, with a discounted prepaid option. Microsoft 365 Copilot Business is listed at $25.20 per user on a monthly subscription, or from $18 per user a month on an annual subscription, down from $21 ([Microsoft 365 Copilot pricing](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/pricing)).

**Limits:** the metered cost depends on model use, retrieval, tool calls, and runtime, so budget from a pilot rather than the license price. It is not the answer for a Google Workspace company.

### Manus

**Best for:** research, analysis, and build tasks, with an optional always-on server.

Manus 2.0, released September 28, 2026, added a dedicated Cloud Computer and event-triggered automations: "A new email, a change in ad performance, a calendar event, a Slack message, or a Notion update can all trigger a task" ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0)). Apps run on web, macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Free gives 300 credits a day and 1 concurrent task. Pro starts at $20 a month for 4,000 credits, 20 concurrent tasks, and 20 scheduled tasks, and a $40 tier doubles the credits. Team starts at $20 per seat ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)). The Cloud Computer is an extra $30 or $50 a month ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)). Prices are set in USD for all users, and you can pay in local currency ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711125-what-is-the-pricing-unit-for-manus-globally-can-i-pay-in-local-currency)).

**Limits:** if you stop paying for the Cloud Computer, "the persistent virtual machine will be shut down" and its files and databases are deleted. Manus resumed independent operations on September 1, 2026, and some users had to back up and restore data during that transition ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/manus-resumes-independent-operations)).

### Lindy

**Best for:** teams whose work already happens in Slack.

Lindy is a Slack-native teammate with scheduled routines, meeting notes, thousands of integrations, and up to 5 connected inboxes ([Lindy pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing)). It lists GDPR and SOC 2 compliance, though the pages we checked do not say where data is processed.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Plus is $29.99 per user a month with 3,000 credits, Pro $99.99 with 15,000, and Max $199.99 with 35,000 ([Lindy pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing)).

**Limits:** "Anyone who uses Lindy takes a seat," including someone who only mentions it in Slack. When credits run out, "Lindy pauses credit-using actions" until the next cycle. Lindy's pricing page lists computer use among core features, while its plan documentation shows it on Pro, Max, and Enterprise only ([Lindy docs](https://docs.lindy.ai/pricing)), so confirm before choosing Plus.

### Grok Bot

**Best for:** people who already pay for Cursor or an eligible SuperGrok plan.

Grok Bot gives you named Bots on "a persistent cloud computer with a browser, filesystem, and terminal," and a Bot can save a path you demonstrate "as a skill and rerun it on a schedule" ([Grok Bot docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview)). Browser sessions persist, and Grok Bot's docs recommend a Marketplace connector over clicking through a website where one exists ([Grok Bot computer and apps](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/computer-and-apps)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** included with Cursor Pro at $20 a month and Cursor Teams at $40 per user ([Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)), and with eligible SuperGrok and X Premium+ accounts linked to Cursor ([Cursor Help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)).

**Limits:** usage limits are described in relative tiers, not numbers, and the docs we read do not mention regional availability or Google Workspace accounts. Our [Grok Bot comparison](/blog/grokbot-alternative/) goes further.

## An illustrative example: a morning inbox digest on a work account

This is an illustrative workflow, not a customer case study or a measured test.

A two-person design studio in Manchester runs on Google Workspace. The founder wants what Spark promises: every weekday at 8:00, a short list of client emails that need a reply and the day's meetings with a line of context for each. Spark is ruled out twice, by the work account and by the UK.

**In Construct:** connect Gmail and Google Calendar from the integration catalog. Ask the agent: "Read yesterday's and this morning's Gmail. List client emails that still need a reply, oldest first, with a one-line summary. Then list today's Calendar events with who is attending. Save it as a dated file in Files and email it to me from your inbox." Check the first few files, then schedule the job. Lite's 3 scheduled tasks cover it.

**In Claude:** connect the Gmail and Google Calendar connectors (your Workspace admin may need to trust Claude first), run the same prompt, and turn it into a Cowork scheduled task so it runs while your laptop is closed.

Either way, the test that matters is the tenth morning, not the first: is the list still right, and can you see why an email was included?

## Which Gemini Spark alternative should you choose?

- **If you have a personal Google account and live outside the excluded regions:** try Gemini Spark first. It is included with Google AI Pro, and nothing else here reaches Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Maps as natively.
- **If you are on a Google Workspace work account and want scheduled help with Gmail, Calendar, and Drive:** start with Claude, or wait for Google's Workspace preview if you would rather stay inside Google.
- **If you are in the UK or EU and your company already pays for ChatGPT Business:** a Business Premium seat gets you dots now. A personal ChatGPT Pro plan does not, yet.
- **If your company runs on Microsoft 365:** Copilot Cowork is the native option.
- **If the work crosses Gmail, a CRM, a tracker, and the web, and you want the files, schedule, and memory kept together:** Construct, from $9 a month.
- **If you mostly hand over one-off research or build tasks:** Manus.
- **If your team lives in Slack:** Lindy.
- **If you already pay for Cursor or SuperGrok:** Grok Bot costs you nothing extra to try.
