## Perplexity Computer

Perplexity launched Computer on February 27, 2026. It "runs entirely in the cloud," uses 19 different AI models, and creates sub-agents for parts of a problem ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/perplexitys-new-computer-is-another-bet-that-users-need-many-ai-models)). On May 7, Perplexity brought a local version, Personal Computer, to the Mac for Pro and Max subscribers. It is designed to run on an always-available machine such as a Mac mini, with tasks started and approved from an iPhone ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/07/perplexitys-personal-computer-is-now-available-everyone-on-mac/)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** TechCrunch reported the cloud Computer as available only on Perplexity Max at $200 a month. A third-party cost guide, [Sliq](https://getsliq.com/blog/perplexity-computer-cost), says Max includes 10,000 credits a month, that "there's no way to access Computer on the Pro plan ($20/month)," and that a monthly spending cap starts at $200 and can be raised to $2,000. ## Claude Cowork

Cowork runs tasks in the cloud, and sessions keep going when you close the app. From October 6, 2026, Anthropic's help center says new Cowork tasks run only in the cloud; tasks that need local files reach them through the Claude Desktop app ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)). On September 16, Cowork and chat became one Claude ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude)).

Scheduled tasks run "hourly, daily, weekly, on weekdays, or manually," and run on their cadence even when your computer is asleep or the Claude Desktop app is closed ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)). They use the same connectors, skills, and plugins as any Cowork task. You choose an approval mode: Manual, Auto, or Skip.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Pro is $20 a month ($17 billed annually), Max starts at $100, and Team Standard is $25 per seat with a two-seat minimum ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). Usage is shared with chat and Claude Code, on a rolling five-hour window with weekly caps on paid plans.

**For background work:** there are no event triggers in the help articles we read, and the work is personal: Anthropic's documentation says Cowork work is seen by "just you" ([How Claude Tag works](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/concepts/how-it-works)). For team work in Slack, Anthropic points to Claude Tag on Team and Enterprise plans. Our [Construct vs Claude Cowork](/blog/construct-vs-claude-cowork/) page covers both.

## Manus

Manus 2.0, released September 28, 2026, made two background changes. Automations can now start when "a new email, a change in ad performance, a calendar event, a Slack message, or a Notion update" happens, and a Cloud Computer gives projects persistent infrastructure ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0)). The Cloud Computer is "a persistent cloud server (Ubuntu Server)" that can run services outside your active Manus sessions ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Free gives 300 credits a day, 1 concurrent task, and 2 scheduled tasks. Pro starts at $20 a month for 4,000 credits, 20 concurrent tasks, and 20 scheduled tasks; a $40 tier doubles the credits; Team starts at $20 per seat ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)). The Cloud Computer is $30 a month for 2 vCPUs, 4 GB of RAM, and 70 GB of storage, or $50 for 8 GB and 120 GB.

**For background work:** Manus documents schedules, event triggers, concurrency, and a machine that stays on. Two cautions. If you stop paying for the Cloud Computer, "the persistent virtual machine will be shut down" and its files and databases are deleted. And Manus resumed independent operations on September 1, 2026, after a transition in which some users had to back up and restore data ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/manus-resumes-independent-operations)). ## The small-business lens

### How predictable is the monthly cost?

Construct is a fixed monthly price, from $9, with published plan limits and no per-message billing. Cowork is a fixed subscription with usage windows shared with chat. Manus combines a subscription with credits, plus a fixed add-on if you need the Cloud Computer. Perplexity Computer, per Sliq, has the widest range: a $200 subscription plus credits up to whatever cap you set.

### Where do the results live?

In Construct, every result is a workspace file, and the run history sits on the Calendar, so next week's run can compare against last week's file. Cowork sessions persist in your Claude account across devices. Manus keeps delivered outputs "in your chat history" even if the Cloud Computer is shut down, but files on the Cloud Computer itself go. Perplexity's file handling was not documented in the sources we could fetch.

### Who can see the work?

A Construct team workspace, available on every plan, lets every colleague open the same files and run history, with no per-seat fee. Cowork work is visible to you, with Claude Team and Claude Tag for shared use. Manus has a Team plan per seat. We found no team details for Perplexity Computer in the sources we could fetch.

### What needs your approval?

Cowork's Manual mode pauses on every action, and Auto reviews writes and deletes. Manus says that on your own computer it works with "the files, browser, and apps you've approved." Perplexity's Personal Computer lets you approve tasks from an iPhone, per TechCrunch. In Construct, set this up deliberately: its workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, have the scheduled run save drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

### Where is it available?

Anthropic lists the UK, EU countries, Switzerland, and Canada as supported for Claude ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries)). Manus prices everything in US dollars for all countries and lets your bank convert ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711125-what-is-the-pricing-unit-for-manus-globally-can-i-pay-in-local-currency)). We could not check Perplexity's regional terms. Construct needs no other subscription; you sign up directly.

## Already using Cowork, Manus, or Perplexity? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to cancel anything. Move the one weekly job that most needs to build on last week's result, or the one your teammates need to see.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the prompt or task instructions** into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week with your laptop closed.

There is no import from Claude, Manus, or Perplexity: write your rules into the first instruction, and Construct saves them as memory you can read. Construct's schedules are time-based, so for work that arrives as it happens, forward it from your own address to the agent's inbox, which starts a run.

For a small team's recurring jobs that should build on last week's file, at a fixed monthly price, Construct is the better fit.
