Construct is a Genspark Claw alternative that gives you an AI employee with its own cloud workspace, files, schedules, and email address for a fixed $9 a month, with model usage included inside published plan limits. Genspark Claw's cheapest cloud computer is $9.99 a month, but that pays only for the machine: every message, scheduled run, and Heartbeat check also spends Genspark credits, which come from a separate membership such as Plus at $24.99 a month, and each Claw belongs to one person.

For most founders and small teams, Construct is the better choice. One fixed-price plan replaces a machine subscription plus a credit balance that every message draws down, and the files, memory, and schedules sit in a workspace the whole team can open. Already running a Claw? Keep it for the chat apps only it covers and give Construct the recurring team work. The two run side by side.

## Why choose Construct over Genspark Claw?

- **A fixed $9 a month, with model usage included.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents (Construct and one more), 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address, with no per-message billing. Claw's machine price does not include the credits its work consumes.
- **One workspace for the whole team.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles. A team workspace has no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. Genspark says "Claw is never shared."
- **Apps connected through a catalog, not a remembered login.** Gmail, Notion, Linear, GitHub, HubSpot, Airtable, and more connect from Construct's live integration catalog, plus your own MCP tools.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or delete it before the next run relies on it.
- **Workflows with run history.** Save a process as a workflow, put it on the Calendar, and keep every run's result. A run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 in your files.
- **Your own model keys.** Pro runs on your keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI. A way to bring your own keys to Claw is not documented in the sources we reviewed.

## What is Genspark Claw?

Genspark Claw is a personal AI assistant that runs on a dedicated cloud computer Genspark rents to you, or on your own Mac or Windows PC, and answers in your chat apps. Genspark's help center calls it "your first AI employee," a "personal assistant that works on your behalf, whether running on a dedicated cloud computer or locally on your own machine" ([Genspark Claw help center](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/genspark-claw)). It launched on March 12, 2026 ([Business Wire via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/genspark-claw-launches-genspark-first-160000169.html)).

The help center lists what the cloud version includes:

- **Machine:** a dedicated cloud computer with a remote desktop, a Terminal tab, a Files tab, and a fixed IP address.
- **Chat apps:** WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Signal, Google Chat, and Feishu or Lark, plus its own Claw Email address.
- **Services:** you log in to Gmail, Outlook, social accounts, GitHub, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Stripe, Jira, and others through the cloud computer's browser, and Claw keeps the session.
- **Schedules:** recurring tasks in plain language or cron, templates such as Weekly Pipeline Report, and a Heartbeat that monitors your inbox, calendar, and other connected services.
- **Skills:** "30+ built-in skills," including slides, docs, image and video generation, and phone calls.

### Does the Genspark Claw desktop app run when your computer is off?

No. The macOS and Windows app needs no Claw subscription, only credits, "but Claw only runs while your computer is on and the app is open." For schedules that run with every device off, you need the cloud computer, or a hosted workspace such as Construct's.

### Is Genspark Claw built on OpenClaw?

Sliq's explainer reports that Claw "takes the OpenClaw agent framework and wraps it in a managed cloud service" ([Sliq](https://getsliq.com/blog/genspark-claw-explained)). Genspark's Claw help page does not mention OpenClaw, though its [GenTeam help article](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/genteam) lists a Genspark Claw computer and "your own OpenClaw" side by side as agent types. If you are weighing Claw against other managed OpenClaw options, our guide to [hosted OpenClaw alternatives](/blog/hosted-openclaw-alternatives/) covers Hostinger, MyClaw, and hosted Hermes.

## How much does Genspark Claw cost?

Genspark Claw costs $9.99, $19.99, or $39.99 a month for its Lite, Standard, or Powerful cloud computer, plus the Genspark credits its work consumes. Construct is one fixed-price plan with published limits, model usage included, and no per-message billing.

Claw has two bills. The cloud computer is "a flat monthly fee," and an idle one uses no credits. Credits are "the fuel for Claw's AI work," spent on "every conversation message and tool call," "each run of a scheduled task or Heartbeat," and "setup and configuration tasks" ([Genspark Claw help center](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/genspark-claw)). When you need more, Genspark's answer is to "upgrade to a Plus or Pro plan" ([Genspark membership plans](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/membership-plans)). ## Where does Construct give you more control?

Both products give an agent a computer in the cloud. Construct also gives your team a shared place for the work, one fixed-price plan, and a say in what the agent remembers.

### A workspace your team shares

Genspark is explicit that "each Claw belongs to one person." To share results, you send a share link or export files somewhere else. Construct is built around a shared workspace: people, the AI employee, files, apps, and conversations in one place, with invitations, roles, and access controls ([team workspaces](/use-cases/team-workspaces/)). When a client report is the team's work, it lives where the whole team can open it.

### Apps connected through the catalog

Claw connects most services by having you log in through a remote desktop on its cloud computer, and "if a session expires... just repeat the steps above to reconnect." Construct connects Gmail, Notion, Linear, GitHub, HubSpot, Airtable, and more through its live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools for systems that are not listed. When a structured connection is not enough, the agent still has a live browser and a terminal.

### Memory you can see and correct

Claw's help center advises saying "remember this" explicitly when you want something stored. In Construct, memory is something you can read: if a client changes its reporting format, you correct the stored fact once and every later job uses it. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) shows how.

### Workflows with run history

Construct saves a process as a workflow made of agent steps and connected-app actions, then runs it from the Calendar on Construct's servers with your laptop closed. Each run keeps its result history, and finished work lands in the workspace as it is produced. The [scheduled tasks guide](/blog/ai-agent-scheduled-tasks/) walks through the setup.

### Bring your own model keys

Claw lets you switch models from its Home tab, and a Model Optimizer skill can assign cheaper models to scheduled tasks to save credits. Construct Pro accepts your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, either as a fallback after bundled limits or for every run, as the [Construct terms](/terms/) explain.

## How to test both yourself

We have not run a timed, side-by-side test of the two. If you want one before you decide, run the same job on both for two weeks and record the same things:

1. **Pick one recurring job,** such as a weekday 8am check of three competitors' pricing and changelog pages, with a short alert when something changed and a dated summary file every Friday.
2. **Time the setup** from sign-up to the first scheduled run.
3. **Track the cost:** Claw's machine price plus the credits each run uses, against your Construct plan.
4. **Count failed runs** and note how each product told you about them.
5. **Ask a teammate to open Friday's file** without your help.

Here is how the Construct side runs, as an illustrative walkthrough rather than a measured result. Save the three competitor URLs in a workspace file. Ask the agent to check each pricing and changelog page, note what changed since yesterday's file, and save a dated summary. Check the first two results, then save the steps as a workflow and put it on the Calendar for weekday mornings. Each run's result lands in Files with a notification in the web app, and it uses one of Lite's 3 scheduled tasks. The [workflow guide](/blog/ai-workflow-automation/) covers each step.

## Already using Genspark Claw? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up your Claw. Start with the job your teammates need to see, or the schedule whose credits you watch most closely.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Ask Claw to write a summary** of the instructions and facts that matter, and save it as a document, since Genspark notes that "there's no built-in way to share or export your Claw chat history."
3. **Move the files** from Claw's Files tab into the Construct workspace, and connect the apps the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** as a workflow so it runs whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import. Paste your summary into the first instruction and Construct saves the rules as memory you can read. If you later cancel Claw, download everything first: Genspark says that once cancellation takes effect, the cloud computer is reclaimed and "all data stored on it is permanently erased" ([Genspark membership plans](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/membership-plans)). One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Genspark Claw still fit?

- Your assistant has to live in WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, LINE, or Signal.
- You want a full always-on machine with a remote desktop, or a local mode on your own computer.
- You want ready-made templates and built-in media skills such as slides, image and video generation, and phone calls.

For recurring team work that needs shared files, a fixed monthly price, and memory and schedules everyone can see, Construct is the better fit.
