Construct gives you an AI employee with its own cloud workspace, files, schedules, and email address from $9 a month, at a fixed monthly price with published plan limits and no per-message billing. Genspark Claw is reported at $39.99 a month for its Standard cloud computer, and every message, scheduled run, and Heartbeat check also draws on your Genspark credits; each Claw belongs to one person and is never shared.

For most founders and small teams, Construct is the better choice. It costs less than a quarter as much to start, comes as one fixed-price plan instead of a machine subscription plus credits that every message draws on, and keeps the files, memory, and schedules in a workspace the whole team can open. Already running a Claw? Keep it for the chat apps only it covers and give Construct the recurring team work. The two run side by side.

## Why choose Construct over Genspark Claw?

- **A fixed $9 a month, with no per-message billing.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Claw adds a cloud computer subscription on top of credits that every task consumes.
- **One workspace for the whole team.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles. A team workspace has no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. Genspark says "Claw is never shared."
- **Apps connected through a catalog, not a remembered login.** Gmail, Notion, Linear, GitHub, HubSpot, Airtable, and more connect from Construct's live integration catalog, plus your own MCP tools.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or delete it before the next run relies on it.
- **Workflows with run history.** Save a process as a workflow, put it on the Calendar, and keep every run's result. A run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 in your files.
- **Your own model keys.** Pro runs on your keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI. We found no documented way to bring your own keys to Claw.

## What is Genspark Claw?

Genspark's help center describes Claw as "your first AI employee," a "personal assistant that works on your behalf, whether running on a dedicated cloud computer or locally on your own machine" ([Genspark Claw help center](https://www.genspark.ai/helpcenter/genspark-claw)). It launched on March 12, 2026, and Sliq's explainer reports that it "takes the OpenClaw agent framework and wraps it in a managed cloud service" ([Sliq](https://getsliq.com/blog/genspark-claw-explained)). Genspark's own pages do not mention OpenClaw.

The help center lists what the cloud version includes:

- **Machine:** a dedicated cloud computer with a remote desktop, a Terminal tab, a Files tab, and a fixed IP address.
- **Chat apps:** WhatsApp, Slack, Microsoft Teams, Telegram, LINE, Discord, Signal, Google Chat, and Feishu or Lark, plus a Claw email address.
- **Services:** you log in to Gmail, Outlook, social accounts, GitHub, Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Stripe, Jira, and others through the cloud computer's browser, and Claw keeps the login.
- **Schedules:** recurring tasks in plain language or cron, templates such as Weekly Pipeline Report, and a Heartbeat that checks your inbox and calendar.

A desktop app for macOS and Windows runs Claw on your own computer with credits only, but "Claw only runs while your computer is on and the app is open."

If you are weighing Claw against other managed OpenClaw options, our guide to [hosted OpenClaw alternatives](/blog/hosted-openclaw-alternatives/) covers Hostinger, MyClaw, and hosted Hermes.

## Where does Construct give you more control?

Both products give an agent a computer in the cloud. Construct also gives your team a shared place for the work, one fixed-price plan, and a say in what the agent remembers.

### A workspace your team shares

Genspark is explicit that "each Claw belongs to one person." To share results, you export files or post them somewhere else. Construct is built around a shared workspace: people, the AI employee, files, apps, and conversations in one place, with invitations, roles, and access controls ([team workspaces](/use-cases/team-workspaces/)). When a client report is the team's work, it lives where the whole team can open it.

### Apps connected through the catalog

Claw connects most services by having you log in through a remote desktop on its cloud computer, and "if a session expires... just repeat the steps above to reconnect." Construct connects Gmail, Notion, Linear, GitHub, HubSpot, Airtable, and more through its live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools for systems that are not listed. When a structured connection is not enough, the agent still has a live browser and a terminal.

### Memory you can see and correct

Claw's help center advises saying "remember this" explicitly, because passing mentions may not be recalled after a long conversation is compressed. In Construct, memory is something you can read: if a client changes its reporting format, you correct the stored fact once and every later job uses it. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) shows how.

### Workflows with run history

Construct saves a process as a workflow made of agent steps and connected-app actions, then runs it from the Calendar on Construct's servers with your laptop closed. Each run keeps its result history, and finished work lands in the workspace as it is produced. The [scheduled tasks guide](/blog/ai-agent-scheduled-tasks/) walks through the setup.

Claw lets you switch models from its Home tab, and a Model Optimizer skill can assign cheaper models to scheduled tasks. ## Already using Genspark Claw? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up your Claw. Start with the job your teammates need to see, or the schedule whose credits you watch most closely.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Ask Claw to write a summary** of the instructions and facts that matter, and save it as a document, since Genspark notes that "there's no built-in way to share or export your Claw chat history."
3. **Move the files** from Claw's Files tab into the Construct workspace, and connect the apps the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** as a workflow so it runs whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import. Paste your summary into the first instruction and Construct saves the rules as memory you can read. If you later cancel Claw, download everything first: Genspark says the cloud computer is reclaimed and "all data stored on it will be permanently erased." One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Genspark Claw still fit?

- Your assistant has to live in WhatsApp, Microsoft Teams, LINE, or Signal.
- You want a full always-on machine with a remote desktop, or a local mode on your own computer.
- You want ready-made templates and built-in media skills such as slides, image and video generation, and phone calls.

For recurring team work that needs shared files, a fixed monthly price, and memory and schedules everyone can see, Construct is the better fit.
