## Why choose Construct over CellCog?

- **A fixed $9 for scheduled work.** Construct Lite includes 3 scheduled tasks for a fixed monthly price, and Starter at $59 includes 10. CellCog's own labels start shift work at $160 a month.
- **Work saved as files you can open.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts are saved to workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 behind.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or forget it. How you view or edit CellCog's memory is not documented in the pages we read.
- **More places to assign work.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and the agent's own email inbox. We did not find Slack or Telegram as places to message a CellCog employee in the pages we read.
- **An inbox strangers cannot command.** Only mail from your own address starts a Construct run, so a stranger who emails the agent cannot hand it instructions.
- **Your own model keys.** Construct Pro accepts keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, and xAI. CellCog's pages do not name its model providers or offer your own keys.

## What is CellCog?

CellCog sells AI employees you hire for a role. Each has its own email inbox, a task board, persistent memory, and a schedule of shifts, and it delivers a written report at the end of every working day ([CellCog](https://cellcog.ai/)).

- **Task board:** To Do, In Progress, Waiting on You, and Done. With permission, an employee can work on your machine behind an approval card or in your Chrome with your logins ([AI employees](https://cellcog.ai/ai-employees)).
- **Phone:** since September 18, 2026, an employee can have its own phone number in about fifty countries ([CellCog blog](https://cellcog.ai/blog/ai-employees-have-phone-numbers/)).
- **Meetings:** it can join Google Meet and Zoom calls under its own name, camera off, and defers anything that commits you, such as a price or a date ([AI employees in meetings](https://cellcog.ai/ai-employees/meetings)).
- **Depth:** you choose Flash, Core, or Max, which sets how deeply it thinks and how many credits it spends.

What to know about CellCog's billing, from its pricing page:

- **Sessions, not seats.** Hiring is free. A working session on the fast Flash tier typically runs under $5, and on Max up to about $25. New accounts get 200 credits with no card.
- **Credit lifetime.** Subscription credits stay valid for 60 days after the billing period ends, and top-ups, at 90 credits per $1, last 12 months.
- **No refunds.** All payments are final and non-refundable, including subscription fees, automatic renewals, and credit top-ups.
- **Phone time is extra.** A number is a recurring monthly charge in credits, and calls are metered by the minute.

What this means for your budget:

- **Three weekly jobs:** Construct Lite at $9 includes 3 scheduled tasks. CellCog's labels put a few shifts a month at $160.
- **A busy month:** a Construct plan is a fixed monthly price with published plan limits. A CellCog employee's cost follows how many shifts and sessions you give it.
- **A team with several agents:** Construct Starter at $59 covers up to 5 agents and a workspace with no member limit.

## Where does Construct give you more control?

Both call their product an AI employee. CellCog sells a role by the shift; Construct gives you a workspace where each recurring job is defined, scheduled, and saved.

### A predictable bill for recurring jobs

If the work is a set of weekly jobs, a fixed monthly plan is easier to budget than shifts. For a founder who wants a Monday report, a Friday client update, and a daily inbox digest, Construct is the cheaper way to find out whether delegation works, and the Calendar runs each job server-side with your laptop closed.

### Keep the work in files you can open

Every Construct run saves its output to workspace files as it goes, and the next run starts from what exists. For a process several people review, Construct can build a private workspace app, such as a review queue with owners and statuses, as our [internal tools guide](/blog/build-internal-tools-with-construct/) shows.

### See and fix what the agent remembers

In Construct, memory is something you can read. If a client changes a preference, you correct the stored fact once and it stops resurfacing. Our [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) explains the controls.

### Assign work from where you already talk

Message Construct from the web app, @mention it in Slack, text it on Telegram, use it in Discord, or email its own address, as our [channels page](/use-cases/channels/) shows. The agent can email people when you ask it to, and only mail from your own address starts a run, so instructions from a stranger never become work.

### Pick the model, or bring your own keys

Construct Pro can run on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, so a team with existing provider accounts can use them. Your keys bill you directly when they are used.

## Already using CellCog? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to let your CellCog employee go. Start with the recurring job that uses the most credits, or the one whose output you need to find again.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the role brief and standing instructions** for that job into a Construct chat, and move the documents it needs from CellCog's Drive into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog, or add your own MCP tools.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: phone numbers, meeting notes, the task board, shift reports, and CellCog's memory do not transfer. Write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. Because CellCog payments are non-refundable, time any downgrade to the end of a billing period and use remaining credits first. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might CellCog still fit?

- You need an AI employee that answers its own phone line or sits in your Google Meet and Zoom calls.
- You need video, images, audio, or podcasts as output.
- You want one worker to plan its own shifts in your logged-in Chrome, with approval cards before risky steps.

For recurring jobs across several apps that should leave files behind on a bill you can predict, Construct is the better fit.
