## Why choose Construct over Muse for Small Business?

- **A fixed monthly price with published plan limits.** Lite is $9 a month for 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address, with no per-message billing. Muse's paid plans are metered in tokens per week.
- **Work that lives in your workspace.** Reports, trackers, and spreadsheets land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 behind.
- **Your whole stack, not one platform.** Construct reaches Gmail, Notion, Linear, GitHub, HubSpot, Airtable, and more through a live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools when a connector does not exist.
- **Memory you can inspect.** See what the agent has learned about your business, correct a stale fact, or tell it to forget something.
- **One workspace for the team, no seats.** Invite teammates as Owner, Admin, or Member. A team workspace has no member limit, and usage draws on the owner's plan.
- **It answers where your team works.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and its own email inbox.

## What is Meta's Muse for Small Business?

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent, [announced September 8, 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/). It runs on a dedicated virtual machine Meta calls Muse Secure VM, a separate agent called Sentinel checks what leaves that machine, and Muse asks for approval before it sends email or makes a purchase. It works in the iOS and Android apps, on the web, and inside WhatsApp.

On September 29, Meta launched [Muse for Small Business](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/), which connects the same agent to Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Granola, HighLevel, Intuit QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Lovable, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe, and Zoom, plus Facebook and Instagram business accounts and custom connectors. [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/meta-puts-popular-new-tool-hands-small-businesses) adds Meta ad accounts and describes jobs such as drafting ad campaigns and writing a week of posts. [CNBC Africa](https://www.cnbcafrica.com/2026/meta-launches-muse-for-small-business-as-zuckerberg-pushes-beyond-consumer-ai-market) cites 200 million small businesses on Facebook as the audience.

**Price:** Meta says Muse is free for most uses, with subscription plans for more. The business version uses the same pricing as the consumer app: free with a usage limit, then Power at $20 a month or Maximum at $100, metered in tokens per week. [DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/muse-agent) reports that Muse asks for a payment card even on the free tier.

## Why do small businesses look for a Muse alternative?

- **Trusting Meta with business data.** Meta says Muse does not share conversations or VM data with its ad systems, that users can opt out of model training, and that a Confidential VM encrypted with a key only you hold is coming. Ebrahim Bagheri of the University of Toronto told [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/12077192/metas-ai-agent-muse-privacy/) that "the more you want accomplish with agents, the more you have to give away your access to your data." That applies to every agent, ours included; the question is which company holds the access.
- **Staying inside Meta's apps.** [TechCrunch's analysis of app data](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/metas-muse-is-outpacing-chatgpts-early-mobile-launch/) found that "over 95% of Muse's users are also Facebook users." That is convenient if your business runs on Meta and limiting if your team works in Slack, a CRM, or email.
- **Availability.** Muse for Small Business is US and Canada only, and [Meta's subscription help page](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/) says Muse subscriptions "are in limited testing and aren't available in all locations yet."
- **Budgeting a busy month.** Weekly token metering is hard to translate into jobs done.
- **Websites that block agents.** On September 21, Amazon blocked Muse from buying on Amazon.com, [TechCrunch reported](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/metas-ai-agent-has-been-blocked-from-using-amazon-com/). Any agent that clicks through websites can be refused by a site, Construct included, so use a connected app or API where one exists; Construct's integration catalog and custom MCP tools give you that route.

## The 9 best Muse for Small Business alternatives, ranked

### 1. Construct

**Best for:** founders and small teams whose work crosses several apps and repeats every week.

Construct is an AI employee that works from a persistent cloud workspace. Each agent has files, live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, its own email address, and a Calendar of scheduled jobs. Calendar schedules run on Construct's servers, so the Monday report runs whether or not your laptop is open. It connects to Gmail, Notion, Linear, GitHub, HubSpot, Airtable, and more through a live integration catalog, and you can add a custom MCP tool when a connector does not exist. You can hand it work from the web, Slack (@mentions and DMs), Telegram, Discord, or by emailing its own address.

Where it differs from Muse is where the work lives and who can use it:

- **Files, not chat history.** Finished work lands in the workspace as it is produced. A run that fails at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29, and the next run starts from what exists.
- **Memory you can correct.** If a client changes its reporting format, you fix the stored fact once and every later job uses it. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) shows how.
- **Processes the whole team can rerun.** A job that works becomes a workflow with agent steps, connected apps, and notifications. Version it, schedule it, and anyone on the team can run it again.
- **Your own internal tools.** For a repeated process, Construct can build a private workspace app such as an order review queue, as our [internal tools guide](/blog/build-internal-tools-with-construct/) explains.
- **Your choice of model.** Pro lets you bring keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI.

## A worked example: the Monday sales and ads summary in Construct

This is an illustrative workflow, not a customer case study or a measured test.

A two-person online store wants a Monday summary: last week's orders and refunds, which products sold, what the Instagram ads spent, and three things to do this week.

1. **Save the format.** Put the report template and last week's report in the workspace.
2. **Connect the inputs.** Connect the apps the job needs from the integration catalog, or upload an order export if a connector is not available. Drop in the Ads Manager export for ad spend.
3. **Ask for the report.** "Compare this week's orders, refunds, and ad spend with last week's report. List the top five products, flag anything that moved more than 20 percent, suggest three actions, and save a dated file with the source of every figure."
4. **Check it and schedule it.** After a few good runs, save the steps as a workflow and put it on the Calendar. Lite's 3 scheduled tasks cover it, and Starter adds more when you add jobs.

The report history stays in Files, so each week starts from the last one, and your teammate can open the same file. On Muse, the same job runs inside your Meta account, with direct access to Meta ad data.

Judge the result by whether the numbers match your store, whether each figure is traceable, and how many minutes of editing it takes.

## Already using Muse? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You can keep Muse for Instagram and Facebook work and give Construct the jobs that cross the rest of your stack.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test longer runs.
2. **Copy the instructions** you give Muse into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import from Muse, so write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for customer emails, posts, or payments, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Muse still fit?

- Most of your selling and advertising happens on Instagram and Facebook, and you want the agent reading Meta ad accounts directly.
- You are in the US or Canada and want to start for nothing.
- You want the agent inside WhatsApp and Meta's apps.

For recurring work across your inbox, CRM, store, and shared files, on one plan your team shares, Construct is the better fit.
