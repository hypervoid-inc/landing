Everything else at this price is a free tier with daily or weekly limits (Manus, Meta Muse), an automation tool with an AI step (Make, Zapier), or, at $20, an agent bundled into a chat or coding subscription that is bought per person (Claude Pro, Google AI Pro, Cursor Pro with Grok Bot, Manus Pro, Muse Power, ChatGPT Plus). For founders and small teams, Construct Lite is the best value: it costs less than any $20 bundle, needs no other subscription, keeps your recurring jobs on a Calendar, and leaves files the next run can build on.

## Why is Construct Lite the best $9 you can spend on an AI agent?

- **A whole workspace, not a chat tier.** Each agent gets a persistent cloud workspace with files, a live browser, a terminal, connected apps, and its own email address. Cheap chat tiers at this price do not include the agent: Google AI Plus leaves out Gemini Spark, and ChatGPT Go is reported without agent mode.
- **Scheduled work that runs without you.** Lite includes 3 scheduled tasks. Calendar runs happen on Construct's servers with your laptop closed and keep the result history.
- **Files that outlive the run.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts land in workspace files as they are produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29, and next week's run starts from last week's file.
- **A fixed monthly price with published limits.** $9 a month, or $7.50 a month billed annually, with no per-message billing and no second subscription to buy first.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what the agent has learned, correct a fact, or delete it before the next run relies on it.
- **Room to grow without seats.** Starter at $59 gives 6x Lite's usage, up to 5 agents, and 10 scheduled tasks for a busier team workspace, and no plan charges per seat. Pro at $299 adds your own model keys, and you can try it free for 7 days.

## What can you get for $10 a month or less?

Very little else that runs on its own. Most products at this price are cheap chat tiers or automation tools with an AI step added.

- **Construct Lite, $9 a month** ($7.50 a month billed annually). Up to 2 agents, up to 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, an agent email address, and 2 parallel background jobs ([pricing](/pricing/)). Each agent gets a persistent cloud workspace with files, a browser, a terminal, and connected apps from a live catalog that includes Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable.
- **Manus Free, $0.** 300 credits a day, 1 concurrent task, and 2 scheduled tasks ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)).
- **Meta Muse, free with a usage limit.** Meta says Muse "keeps working after people close the app" ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)), and its help page says "Muse is available for free with a usage limit" ([Meta Help Center](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)). It is rolling out in the US; the small business version is US and Canada only.
- **Make Core, $9 a month.** An automation platform that lists "Make AI Agents (beta)" on all plans, with 10,000 credits on its starting tier ([Make pricing](https://www.make.com/en/pricing)). You build the scenario; it is not an agent with its own workspace.
- **Zapier Free, $0.** Zapier says all plans include basic access to Agents, with 400 agent activities a month on Free ([Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing)), and that agents work across its apps "on command and while you sleep" ([Zapier Agents](https://zapier.com/agents)).
- **Cheap chat tiers.** Google AI Plus is $4.99 a month ([Google](https://gemini.google/us/subscriptions/?hl=en)) and does not include Gemini Spark, which needs AI Pro or Ultra ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)). ChatGPT Go is reported at $8 a month without agent mode ([Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/chatgpt-pricing)).

Two products offer free credits before paying more than $20: Viktor gives "$100 in credits" with no card before its $50 plan ([Viktor pricing](https://viktor.com/pricing)), and Lindy's homepage offers "$50 in free credits" before its $29.99 per-user plan ([Lindy](https://www.lindy.ai/)).

## What does $20 a month buy?

At $20, the agent usually comes with a subscription bought for one person. That works if you already use the subscription; it means paying for a chatbot to reach an agent if you do not.

- **Claude Pro, $20** ($17 billed annually). Includes Cowork and scheduled tasks, which run remotely on their cadence even when your computer is asleep ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork); [Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). Usage runs on a rolling five-hour window with weekly caps.
- **Google AI Pro, $19.99.** Includes Gemini Spark, which Google says works in the background 24/7 ([Gemini Spark](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/)). Personal Google accounts only, 18 and over, and not available in the EEA, Switzerland, Nigeria, or the UK; up to 15 tasks running at once ([Gemini Apps Help](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16596215?hl=en)).
- **Cursor Pro with Grok Bot, $20.** Every paid individual Cursor plan includes Grok Bot, with a usage grant that resets weekly ([Cursor help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans); [Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)). Bots work on a persistent cloud computer and can rerun a saved skill on a schedule ([Grok Bot docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview)).
- **Manus Pro, from $20.** 4,000 credits a month, 20 concurrent tasks, and 20 scheduled tasks. The always-on Cloud Computer is extra, from $30 a month ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).
- **Meta Muse Power, $20.** 500 million Muse tokens a week ([Meta Help Center](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)).
- **ChatGPT Plus, $20.** Agent mode is included, but its messages "draw from the same rolling cap as regular flagship-model messages," and dots, OpenAI's always-on agents, need Pro or Business Premium ([Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/chatgpt-pricing); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)). OpenAI's own pricing page blocked our fetch.
- **Microsoft 365 Premium, $19.99.** Microsoft lists "Agentic AI features that get things done for you" in its consumer Premium plan ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/buy/compare-all-microsoft-365-products)). We did not find documentation of scheduled background tasks for this plan.
- **Make Pro, $16.** The same automation platform with more features and AI Agents in beta ([Make pricing](https://www.make.com/en/pricing)).

Just over the line: MyClaw hosts OpenClaw for you at $29 a month, or $199 a year (about $16.58 a month), and "AI token usage isn't included in the subscription" ([MyClaw pricing](https://myclaw.ai/pricing)). Zapier Professional is $29.99 a month, or $19.99 billed annually ([Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing)).

## What does the sticker price hide?

**Usage caps.** Every plan here has a ceiling, expressed in a different unit: steps per task, credits, tokens per week, a rolling five-hour window, or a weekly grant. Price a busy week, not an average one. Construct publishes its limits per plan, and Starter at $59 gives 6x Lite's usage when a job outgrows Lite.

**Who it is for.** Chat-bundled agents are personal. Claude Pro, Google AI Pro, and Cursor Pro are bought per person, and Gemini Spark does not work with Google Workspace work accounts. A Construct team workspace, available on every plan, covers everyone on one plan with no per-seat fee.

**Model costs.** Self-hosted agents look free but are not. OpenClaw is free software, and you pay for hosting and model tokens separately. Construct Lite and Starter include model usage in the plan; Pro adds bring-your-own keys, billed by your provider when used.

**Where the work goes.** Some agents leave output in a chat thread. Construct leaves files in a workspace the next run can build on. For a job that repeats weekly, that difference matters more than a few dollars.

**What it can reach.** An agent that cannot connect to your CRM or your inbox is cheap and also not much use for business work. Construct's live integration catalog includes Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable, and you can add your own MCP tools.

## Already paying for a $20 agent? Add Construct for $9

You do not have to cancel anything. Keep your chat subscription for conversations and move one recurring job to Construct.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test longer runs and your own model keys.
2. **Copy the prompt or instructions** you use today into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week with your laptop closed.

There is no import from other agents: write your rules into the first instruction, and Construct saves them as memory you can read. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might another option still fit?

- You want to spend nothing at all while you experiment (Manus Free, or Meta Muse in the US).
- Your work is fixed data mapping between apps rather than judgment (Make or Zapier).
- You already pay for Claude, Google AI Pro, or Cursor and only want a personal assistant inside it.

For recurring business work that should run on a schedule and leave files behind, Construct Lite is the cheapest fit.

## Methodology

We build Construct, so it leads this list. It fits small teams because Lite is the cheapest paid plan we found that gives an agent its own cloud workspace, scheduled jobs, and files that persist between runs.
