## Why choose Construct over Lindy?

- **$9 a month instead of $29.99 per person.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address.
- **No seats to count.** Team workspaces have Owner, Admin, and Member roles and no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. On Lindy, anyone who uses it takes a seat.
- **Work that lands as files.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts are saved to the workspace as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still keeps steps 1 to 29, and next week's run starts from last week's output.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or tell it to forget one.
- **Schedules that run with your laptop closed.** Jobs on the Calendar run on Construct's servers at the time you set.
- **More places to reach it than Slack.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and the agent's own email inbox, all backed by the same agent and workspace.

## Why do people look for a Lindy alternative?

The reasons come straight from Lindy's own billing rules.

**Seats.** Lindy's [plan documentation](https://docs.lindy.ai/pricing) says: "Anyone who uses Lindy takes a seat, whether they sign up directly, join your workspace, or @mention Lindy in Slack." A five-person team on Plus pays about $150 a month before anyone runs a heavy job.

**Credits that pause the work.** A credit is "about one cent." Everyday tasks cost 2 to 250 credits, deep work 250 to 1,000, and major builds 1,000 to 2,500. Credits pool across the workspace, and the [pricing page](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing) says they "don't roll over." When the pool is empty, "Lindy pauses credit-using actions" until the next cycle, unless an admin buys top-ups at $10 per 1,000 credits.

**Browser work on the entry plan.** Lindy's pricing page lists computer use on every plan. Its plan documentation shows it on Pro, Max, and Enterprise only, so confirm which applies before you choose Plus for a job that drives a website.

**A product that changed shape.** Lindy's [changelog](https://www.lindy.ai/changelog) records Lindy 3.0 in August 2025, with an agent builder. Today the documentation centers on Lindy as a teammate in Slack.

**Reviews.** Lindy's homepage cites a 4.9-star G2 rating, while [Lindy's Trustpilot page](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lindy.ai) showed 1.7 out of 5 from 40 reviews on October 1, 2026, with one reviewer reporting daily credit use rising from "about 600-1000 credits per day" to "1000-4000." Forty reviews is a small, self-selected sample, so treat it as a reason to model your own month.

## AI employees with their own computer

These take a job rather than a trigger, and they work in a computer environment with files and a browser. They are the closest replacement for Lindy's "comes back with the work done" promise.

### 1. Construct

**Best for:** solo founders and small teams whose work repeats every week and should leave files, a schedule, and correctable memory behind.

Construct gives each agent a persistent cloud workspace with files, live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, an agent email address, and a Calendar of scheduled jobs. It reaches Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable through its live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools when an entry does not exist. You can assign work from the web desktop, Slack, Telegram, Discord slash commands, or its own inbox, as the [channels page](/use-cases/channels/) shows.

The difference from Lindy is where the work lands. A report or spreadsheet is saved as a file in the workspace, so next week's run starts from last week's output and any teammate can open it. Finished work is kept as it is produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29.

What a Lindy user gets with it:

- **A team without seat math.** Invite teammates into a team workspace as Owner, Admin, or Member. There is no member limit, and the work draws on the owner's plan, so the person who asks for one report a month adds no seat fee.
- **Slack where you need it.** Mention the agent in a channel, reply in its thread, or DM it. Scheduled runs report in the web app with a notification rather than posting into Slack, so the finished file is always in one place.
- **Memory you control.** Tell it once that a client wants figures in euros, and it remembers. If that changes, open the stored fact and correct it.
- **Recurring jobs on the Calendar.** Save a run as a workflow and schedule it: 3 scheduled tasks on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro. Schedules run server-side, so nobody has to be online.
- **Internal tools.** Ask for a review queue or tracker and Construct builds it as a private app inside the workspace.
- **Your own model keys on Pro.** Bring keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI.

An illustrative first job: every Monday at 8:00, read the last week of the shared inbox through the Gmail integration, list each customer email that still needs a reply with a one-line summary, draft the replies without sending them, and save the list as a dated file. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows run straight through and do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Lite is $9 a month for up to 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Starter is $59 with 6x Lite usage, up to 5 agents, 150 steps per task, and 10 scheduled tasks. Pro is $299 with 32x Lite usage, up to 15 agents, up to 1,000 steps per task, 50 scheduled tasks, and bring-your-own keys, with a 7-day trial ([Construct pricing](/pricing/)).

### 2. CellCog

**Best for:** hiring one AI worker into a defined role that works scheduled shifts.

Each CellCog employee gets its own email inbox, a task board, persistent memory, and a schedule of shifts, and files a written report at the end of each working day ([CellCog](https://cellcog.ai/)). It can work in your own Chrome with your logins behind an approval card ([AI employees](https://cellcog.ai/ai-employees)), and since September 2026 can have a phone number in about fifty countries ([CellCog blog](https://cellcog.ai/blog/ai-employees-have-phone-numbers/)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Starter is $8 a month for 800 credits, Basic $20 for 2,000, and Pro $40 for 4,000. CellCog labels the $160 tier "Part-time AI employee, a few shifts a month" and the $500 Nitro tier "A full-time AI employee, working weekday shifts" ([CellCog pricing](https://cellcog.ai/pricing)). New accounts get 200 free credits. The pricing FAQ says "All payments to CellCog are final and non-refundable." Model providers and bring-your-own keys are not documented in the pages we read.

### 3. Claude with Cowork

**Best for:** people who already pay for Claude and want background work for themselves.

Cowork runs multi-step tasks on Anthropic's servers in a cloud beta ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)). Scheduled tasks are on all paid plans and run while your computer is asleep, though they cannot use folders on your own computer ([scheduled tasks](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)). Agentic work draws on the same usage as chat.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Pro is $20 billed monthly, Team Standard seats $25, and Premium seats $125 ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)).

### 4. Manus

**Best for:** one-off research, analysis, and build tasks.

Manus 2.0, launched September 28, 2026, can start work when something happens in a connected service, such as a new email or a Slack message ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0)). An optional Cloud Computer is a persistent virtual machine.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** a free plan with 300 daily refresh credits and 2 scheduled tasks; Pro from $20 a month for 4,000 credits and 20 scheduled tasks; Team from $20 per seat ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)). The Cloud Computer is $30 or $50 a month extra, and if you stop paying, "the persistent virtual machine will be shut down" and its files are deleted ([Cloud Computer billing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).

## A shared teammate in Slack or Teams

### 5. Viktor

**Best for:** a team that wants a coworker inside Slack or Microsoft Teams only, billed per workspace.

Viktor works in Slack and Microsoft Teams, runs code on its own cloud computer, connects to over 3,200 tools, runs recurring workflows, and asks for confirmation before actions such as sending emails ([Viktor](https://viktor.com/)). It says it is SOC 2 Type 1 compliant. iMessage, a meeting recorder, and channels outside Slack and Teams are not documented in the pages we read.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** $100 in starting credits with no card, then Team plans from $50 a month for 20,000 credits, which Viktor estimates at roughly 40 to 200 tasks. Pricing is per workspace, and unused plan credits roll over for one month ([Viktor pricing](https://viktor.com/pricing)).

## If you liked Lindy's agent builder

If you want to design every trigger and step yourself, a builder takes more setup and gives you control of each step. If you would rather describe the job and let the agent work out the steps, Construct saves a successful run as a workflow you can schedule.

### 6. Gumloop

**Best for:** teams that want to build agents themselves and share them without paying per seat.

Gumloop's [pricing page](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing) lists unlimited agents, more than 35 models, bring-your-own API keys, and unlimited seats on Pro, from $37 a month with 20,000 credits and a 14-day trial, checked October 1, 2026. The page also lists an orchestration fee on usage.

### 7. Zapier Agents

**Best for:** teams that already run their automations on Zapier.

Zapier agents work across more than 9,000 apps, on command or on a schedule ([Zapier Agents](https://zapier.com/agents)). The free tier includes 400 activities a month, and the Agents Pro add-on gives 1,500 activities from about $33.33 a month billed annually. An activity is "any billable action an agent takes," including a web search or a page browse, separate from Zap tasks ([Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing)). A workspace for files is not documented.

### 8. n8n

**Best for:** technical owners who want self-hosting and to design every step.

n8n prices "based on monthly workflow executions, regardless of complexity," with unlimited users and workflows on every plan ([n8n pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/)). Cloud Starter is €20 a month billed annually for 2,500 executions, Pro €50 for 10,000, and monthly billing costs about 17% more. Self-hosting is free under n8n's fair-code license, and you maintain the server and workflows.

### 9. Relevance AI

**Best for:** sales and support teams that want to design multi-agent teams.

The public [pricing page](https://relevanceai.com/pricing) shows only a sales-led Enterprise plan. The [pricing documentation](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/pricing) still lists Pro at $29 a month ($19 billed annually) with 2,500 actions, $20 of vendor credits, and 2 build users, and Team at $349. Confirm which applies before you plan around it.

## A role-based alternative

### 10. Marblism

**Best for:** a local business that mainly needs a phone receptionist and inbox drafts.

Marblism sells seven role-based AI employees, including an email assistant that drafts but does not send, and a receptionist with a phone number in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, or Mexico ([Marblism integrations](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/15278633-what-integrations-are-available), [working with Eva](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/11516676-how-to-work-with-eva-the-exec-assistant)). It is $44 a month, or $24 billed yearly, for 50 hours of work and unlimited team members; a drafted email uses 5 minutes and a handled call 10, and new tasks pause when hours run out ([Marblism billing](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/15361461-marblism-pricing-plans-billing-cadence)). Marblism says it has no public API or webhooks.

We also looked at Sintra, which sells 12 role-based helpers; its homepage FAQ says "Every AI worker runs as a chatbot." Our [roundup of AI employee platforms](/blog/best-ai-employee-platforms/) covers it.

## Already using Lindy? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to switch everything at once. Start with the one job that eats the most credits or needs a teammate who does not have a Lindy seat.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test longer runs and your own model keys.
2. **Copy the instructions** you gave Lindy into a Construct chat, and move any reference files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs, such as Gmail, Notion, or HubSpot, from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: Lindy's agents, settings, and history do not transfer, so write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. Lindy's per-action approvals also have no direct equivalent in a scheduled Construct workflow, so ask for drafts on anything customer-facing and send them yourself.

## When might Lindy still fit?

- You want iMessage or SMS, or a built-in meeting recorder.
- You need approval in Slack before each individual write action.
- Your whole team already has Lindy seats and the credit pool covers your month.

For recurring work that should leave files, run on a schedule, and serve a whole team without per-seat billing, Construct is the better fit.

## Which Lindy alternative should you choose?

Choose Construct. It starts at $9 instead of $29.99 per person, adds teammates without seats, saves every finished file to a workspace your team shares, and keeps memory you can correct.

- **If seat costs are why you are leaving,** choose Construct: Starter at $59 covers the workspace for any number of members.
- **If you want recurring jobs to leave files and run on a schedule,** choose Construct, from $9.
