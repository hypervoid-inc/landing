## Why choose Construct over Lindy?

Construct is cheaper to start, does not bill per person, and keeps every result as a file your team can open. The details:

- **$9 a month instead of $29.99 per person.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address.
- **No seats to count.** [Team workspaces](/use-cases/team-workspaces/) have Owner, Admin, and Member roles and no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. On Lindy, anyone who uses it takes a seat.
- **Work that lands as files.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts are saved to the workspace as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still keeps steps 1 to 29, and next week's run starts from last week's output.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or tell it to forget one.
- **Schedules that run with your laptop closed.** Jobs on the Calendar run on Construct's servers at the time you set.
- **More places to reach it than Slack.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and the agent's own email inbox, all backed by the same agent and workspace.

## How much does Lindy cost in 2026?

Lindy has three plans: Free, with $50 in credits that last 7 days and no card, then Team, billed per active user, and custom Enterprise. Team starts at $29.99 per active user a month for 3,000 credits, though Lindy is testing its price and its [pricing page](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing) shows some visitors $49.99 for 5,000 credits instead, so confirm the figure you are shown before you budget.

**Seats.** Lindy's [pricing page](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing) and [plan documentation](https://docs.lindy.ai/pricing) both say: "Anyone who uses Lindy takes a seat, whether they sign up directly, join your workspace, or @mention Lindy in Slack." New teammates who join through Slack get a 7-day trial before their seat is billed. A five-person team at $29.99 a seat pays about $150 a month before anyone runs a heavy job.

**Credits that pause the work.** Lindy's [homepage](https://www.lindy.ai/) describes a credit as "about a cent." Its pricing page puts everyday asks at 2 to 250 credits, deep work at 250 to 1,000, and big builds at 1,000 to 2,500. Credits pool across the workspace and "don't roll over." When the pool is empty, "Lindy pauses credit-using actions until your credits reset at the start of your next cycle," unless an admin buys top-ups at $10 per 1,000 credits.

**Computer use on the entry plan.** The Team plan card on Lindy's pricing page lists computer use, while the feature list further down the same page says "Computer use on Pro and Max." Confirm which applies before you choose the entry plan for a job that drives a website.

**A product that changed shape.** Lindy's [changelog](https://www.lindy.ai/changelog) records Lindy 3.0 in August 2025, with an agent builder. Today the site and documentation center on Lindy as a teammate in Slack.

**Reviews.** [Lindy's Trustpilot page](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/lindy.ai) showed 1.7 out of 5 from 40 reviews on October 11, 2026, with one reviewer reporting daily credit use rising from "about 600-1000 credits per day" to "1000-4000." Forty reviews is a small, self-selected sample, so treat it as a reason to model your own busy month rather than as a verdict.

## Is there a free Lindy alternative?

Yes, with limits: Manus has a free plan, Zapier Agents has a free tier of 400 activities a month, and n8n can be self-hosted with no license fee. None of them is a free AI employee with a persistent workspace, and Construct has no free plan, but Lite costs $9 and Pro has a 7-day trial.

- **Manus Free:** 300 credits refreshed daily and 2 scheduled tasks ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)).
- **Zapier Agents Free:** 400 activities a month, where a web search or a page browse each counts as one ([Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing)).
- **n8n self-hosted:** n8n is "fair-code," distributed under a royalty-free [Sustainable Use License](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n/blob/master/LICENSE.md), and you run and maintain the server yourself.
- **Free starting credits:** Viktor gives $100 in credits with no card ([Viktor pricing](https://viktor.com/pricing)), CellCog gives new accounts 200 credits ([CellCog pricing](https://cellcog.ai/pricing)), and Lindy itself gives $50 that lasts 7 days.

## AI employees with their own computer

These take a job rather than a trigger, and they work in a computer environment with files and a browser. They are the closest replacement for Lindy's promise that it "comes back with the work done."

### 1. Construct

**Best for:** solo founders and small teams whose work repeats every week and should leave files, a schedule, and correctable memory behind.

Construct gives each agent a persistent cloud workspace with files, live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, an agent email address, and a Calendar of scheduled jobs. It reaches Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, and Airtable through its live integration catalog, and you can add custom MCP tools when an entry does not exist. You can assign work from the web desktop, Slack, Telegram, Discord slash commands, or its own inbox, as the [channels page](/use-cases/channels/) shows.

Work lands in the workspace. A report or spreadsheet is saved as a file, so next week's run starts from last week's output and any teammate can open it. Finished work is kept as it is produced, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29.

What a Lindy user gets with it:

- **A team without seat math.** Invite teammates into a team workspace as Owner, Admin, or Member. There is no member limit, and the work draws on the owner's plan, so the person who asks for one report a month adds no seat fee.
- **Slack where you need it.** Mention the agent in a channel, reply in its thread, or DM it. Scheduled runs report in the web app with a notification rather than posting into Slack, so the finished file is always in one place.
- **Memory you control.** Tell it once that a client wants figures in euros, and it remembers. If that changes, open the stored fact and correct it, as the [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) shows.
- **Recurring jobs on the Calendar.** Save a run as a workflow and [schedule it](/use-cases/scheduling/): 3 scheduled tasks on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro. Schedules run server-side, so nobody has to be online.
- **Internal tools.** Ask for a review queue or tracker and Construct builds it as a private app inside the workspace.
- **Your own model keys on Pro.** Bring keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI.

An illustrative first job: every Monday at 8:00, read the last week of the shared inbox through the Gmail integration, list each customer email that still needs a reply with a one-line summary, draft the replies without sending them, and save the list as a dated file. One thing to set up deliberately: Construct's workflows run straight through and do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself.

**Price, checked October 11, 2026:** Lite is $9 a month for up to 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Starter is $59 with 6x Lite usage, up to 5 agents, 150 steps per task, and 10 scheduled tasks. Pro is $299 with 32x Lite usage, up to 15 agents, up to 1,000 steps per task, 50 scheduled tasks, and bring-your-own keys, with a 7-day trial ([Construct pricing](/pricing/)).

### 2. CellCog

**Best for:** hiring one AI worker into a defined role that works scheduled shifts.

Each CellCog employee gets its own email inbox, a task board, persistent memory across shifts, and a schedule, and files a written report at the end of each shift ([CellCog AI employees](https://cellcog.ai/ai-employees)). It can work in your own Chrome with your logins behind an approval card, and since September 19, 2026 it can have a local phone number in about fifty countries ([CellCog blog](https://cellcog.ai/blog/ai-employees-have-phone-numbers/)).

**Price, checked October 11, 2026:** Starter is $8 a month for 800 credits, Basic $20 for 2,000, and Pro $40 for 4,000. CellCog labels the $160 tier "Part-time AI employee, a few shifts a month" and the $500 Nitro tier "A full-time AI employee, working weekday shifts" ([CellCog pricing](https://cellcog.ai/pricing)). New accounts get 200 free credits. The pricing FAQ says "All payments to CellCog are final and non-refundable." Bring-your-own model keys are not documented in the pages we read.

### 3. Claude (formerly Cowork)

**Best for:** people who already pay for Claude and want background work for themselves.

On September 16, 2026, Anthropic announced that "Claude Cowork is now just Claude," rolling out first to Pro and Max ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude)). Cowork tasks run in the cloud on Anthropic's servers, which its help center labels "in beta on Team and Enterprise plans" ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)). Scheduled tasks are on all paid plans and run while your computer is asleep, except tasks that need local files or apps, which only run on your computer ([scheduled tasks](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)).

**Price, checked October 11, 2026:** Pro is $20 billed monthly, Team Standard seats $25, and Premium seats $125 ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)).

### 4. Manus

**Best for:** one-off research, analysis, and build tasks.

Manus 2.0, launched September 28, 2026, can start work when something happens in a connected service, such as a new email or a Slack message ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0)). An optional Cloud Computer is a persistent virtual machine.

**Price, checked October 11, 2026:** a free plan with 300 daily refresh credits and 2 scheduled tasks; Pro from $20 a month for 4,000 credits and 20 scheduled tasks; Team from $20 per seat ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)). The Cloud Computer is $30 or $50 a month extra, and if you stop paying, "the persistent virtual machine will be shut down" and its files are deleted ([Cloud Computer billing](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).

### 5. Viktor

**Best for:** a team that wants a coworker inside Slack or Microsoft Teams only, billed per workspace.

Viktor works in Slack and Microsoft Teams, runs code on its own cloud computer, connects to over 3,200 tools, runs recurring workflows, and asks for confirmation before high-stakes actions such as sending emails ([Viktor](https://viktor.com/)). Its FAQ says it is "SOC 2 Type 1 compliant." iMessage, a meeting recorder, and channels outside Slack and Teams are not documented in the pages we read.

Pricing is per workspace, and unused plan credits roll over for one month ([Viktor pricing](https://viktor.com/pricing)).

A builder takes more setup and gives you control of each trigger and step. If you would rather describe the job and let the agent work out the steps, Construct saves a successful run as a workflow you can schedule.

### 6. Gumloop

**Best for:** teams that want to build agents themselves and share them without paying per seat.

Gumloop's [pricing page](https://www.gumloop.com/pricing) lists unlimited agents, more than 35 models, bring-your-own API keys, and unlimited seats on Pro, from $37 a month with 20,000 credits and a 14-day trial, checked October 11, 2026. The same page lists an 8% orchestration fee.

### 7. Zapier Agents

**Best for:** teams that already run their automations on Zapier.

Zapier agents work across more than 9,000 apps, on command and in the background ([Zapier Agents](https://zapier.com/agents)). The free tier includes 400 activities a month, and the Agents Pro add-on gives 1,500 activities from about $33.33 a month billed annually. An activity is "any billable action an agent takes," including a web search or a page browse, and "Using activities does not affect your Zapier task usage" ([Zapier pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing)). A workspace for files is not documented.

### 8. n8n

**Best for:** technical owners who want self-hosting and to design every step.

n8n prices "based on monthly workflow executions, regardless of complexity," and "All plans include unlimited users & workflows" ([n8n pricing](https://n8n.io/pricing/)). Cloud Starter is €20 a month billed annually for 2,500 executions and Pro €50 for 10,000; annual billing saves 17%. Self-hosting carries no license fee under n8n's fair-code license, and you maintain the server and workflows.

### 9. Relevance AI

**Best for:** sales and support teams that want to design multi-agent teams.

The public [pricing page](https://relevanceai.com/pricing) shows only a sales-led Enterprise plan. The [pricing documentation](https://relevanceai.com/docs/get-started/pricing) still lists Pro at $29 a month ($19 billed annually) with 2,500 actions, $20 of vendor credits, and 2 build users, and Team at $349. Confirm which applies before you plan around it.

**Best for:** a local business that mainly needs a phone receptionist and inbox drafts.

Marblism sells seven role-based AI employees, including an email assistant that "never sends emails by herself," and a receptionist with a phone number in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, or Mexico ([Marblism integrations](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/15278633-what-integrations-are-available), [working with Eva](https://help.marblism.com/en/articles/11516676-how-to-work-with-eva-the-exec-assistant)). ## How to test a Lindy alternative yourself

The fairest way to choose is to give two or three products the same job for two weeks. We have not run this comparison, so here is a checklist rather than results.

1. **Pick one real recurring job**, such as "every Monday at 8:00, list last week's customer emails that still need a reply, draft replies without sending, and save the list."
2. **Write the instruction once** and paste it unchanged into each product.
3. **Use the same inputs**: the same inbox or a copy of it, the same files, the same connected apps.
4. **Record setup time** and minutes to the first usable result.
5. **Check accuracy**: how many emails were correctly flagged, and how many drafts you could send without edits.
6. **Check safety**: anything sent, deleted, or changed that you did not ask for.
7. **Check what is left behind**: where the result is saved, and whether a teammate without an account or seat can open it.
8. **Price four weekly runs**: convert credits or activities to dollars, or count the share of a flat plan.

On Construct, connect Gmail from the integration catalog, run the job once, check the saved file, then save the steps as a workflow and put it on the Calendar, where it uses one of Lite's 3 scheduled tasks. The [workflow guide](/blog/ai-workflow-automation/) walks through each step.

## Already using Lindy? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to switch everything at once. Start with the one job that eats the most credits or needs a teammate who does not have a Lindy seat.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test longer runs and your own model keys.
2. **Copy the instructions** you gave Lindy into a Construct chat, and move any reference files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs, such as Gmail, Notion, or HubSpot, from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: Lindy's agents, settings, and history do not transfer, so write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read. Lindy holds actions with outside impact for approval, which a scheduled Construct workflow does not do yet, so ask for drafts on anything customer-facing and send them yourself.

## When might Lindy still fit?

- You want to text your agent over iMessage or SMS, or want a built-in meeting recorder.
- You want every action with outside impact, such as sending an email, held for approval by default.
- Your whole team already has Lindy seats and the credit pool covers your month.

For recurring work that should leave files, run on a schedule, and serve a whole team without per-seat billing, Construct is the better fit.
