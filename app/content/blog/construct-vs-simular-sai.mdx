Construct gives a small team an AI employee with a shared workspace, connected apps, a live browser, a terminal, and its own email address from $9 a month. Simular Sai is free to try, but regular use is $50 a month of pay-as-you-go credits that also pay for the cloud machine's running time, and an always-on computer comes with the $500 Unlimited plan ([Sai pricing](https://www.sai.work/pricing)).

For founders and small teams whose tools already connect through integrations, Construct is the better choice. It costs a fraction as much for regular use, it works through structured app connections before it reaches for the screen, it gives the whole team one workspace with shared files and memory you can correct, and it takes work from Slack, Telegram, Discord, and email. If some of your work lives in desktop software with no API, keep Sai for that and give Construct everything else.

## Why choose Construct over Simular Sai?

- **$9 a month with published plan limits.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Sai's paid entry is $50 of credits that cover both AI usage and machine time.
- **Structured connections first.** Construct reaches Gmail, Google Calendar, Notion, Linear, Jira, GitHub, HubSpot, Airtable, and more through its live integration catalog, plus your own MCP tools, with a live browser and terminal for the rest. There is no screen layout to break the run.
- **One workspace for the whole team.** Invite teammates with Owner, Admin, or Member roles; a team workspace has no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan.
- **Hand it work where your team already talks.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and the agent's own email inbox.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or forget it, next to the files and workflows that rely on it.
- **Your own model keys.** Construct Pro accepts keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, and xAI. Supplying your own keys is not documented in the Sai pages we reviewed.

## What is Simular Sai?

Sai is a computer-use agent from Simular that clicks and types through browsers and desktop apps on a private cloud virtual machine or your own device ([Sai](https://sai.work/)). Simular announced it on September 16, 2026 ([Simular](https://www.simular.ai/articles/sai-your-first-robosecretary)), and release coverage dated September 23 reported general availability on cloud machines Simular provisions or on your own device ([release coverage](https://ohsem.me/2026/09/simular-releases-sai-ushers-in-new-era-of-autonomous-computer-fleets/)).

- **Where it runs:** its own cloud VM, so tasks do not need your laptop open, or your own Mac mini, Mac, or Windows PC ([Sai](https://sai.work/)).
- **Recurring work:** you teach a task once and Sai reruns it on a schedule (same source).
- **Approvals:** you approve critical steps and get results through iMessage, SMS, or Telegram (same source).
- **Persistence:** setup, logins, preferences, and context persist between sessions on its machine (same source).

## Where does Construct give you more control?

Sai uses the screen the way a person does. Construct uses structured connections first, keeps the results in a workspace your team shares, and lets you correct the facts behind the work.

### Structured connections before screen clicks

When an app has an integration, a structured connection is the more direct path: no screen to read, and no layout change to break the run. Construct reaches the apps in its live integration catalog directly, and you can add your own MCP tools for internal systems. Our guide to [custom MCP and permission boundaries](/blog/ai-agent-custom-mcp/) covers how. When there is no integration, the same task can use a live browser and a sandbox terminal, as our guide to [browser and terminal work](/blog/ai-agent-browser-and-terminal/) shows.

### A workspace the team shares

Construct is built around a shared workspace: people, the AI employee, files, apps, and shared conversations in one place, with invitations and roles ([team workspaces](/use-cases/team-workspaces/)). Every run saves its output to workspace files as it goes, so a run that stops at step 30 keeps steps 1 to 29, and the report the agent writes is a file everyone on the team can open next week.

### Assign work from where the team already talks

Message Construct from the web app, @mention it in Slack, text it on Telegram, use it in Discord, or email its own address. Mail from your own address starts a job, so you can forward a supplier's message with your instructions at the top.

### See and fix what the agent remembers

In Construct, memory is something you can read. If a client changes a preference, you correct the stored fact once and it does not resurface next week. The [memory guide](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) explains how.

### Pick the model, or bring your own keys

Construct Pro can run on your own keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI, either as a fallback after bundled limits or for every run, as the [Construct terms](/terms/) explain. Your keys bill you directly when they are used.

## Already using Sai? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to give up Sai. Start with a job that touches apps Construct connects to directly, such as Gmail, HubSpot, Notion, or Google Sheets.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Write the steps you taught Sai** as plain instructions in a Construct chat, and move the files the job needs into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** from the integration catalog, or add your own MCP tools.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import: Sai's saved logins, skills, and learned workflows do not transfer, so back up any files on Sai's cloud computer before you change plans. Two things to set up deliberately. Construct cannot operate Windows or Mac desktop apps, so keep jobs that depend on desktop software on Sai. And Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## When might Simular Sai still fit?

- The work happens inside desktop software, legacy tools, or portals with no API.
- You want an always-on machine, or an agent running on your own Mac or PC.
- You want to approve every critical step from your phone.

For recurring team work across apps that connect through integrations, with shared files, correctable memory, and a fixed monthly price, Construct is the better fit.

Choose Construct. It starts at $9, connects to your apps directly instead of clicking through their screens, gives your team one workspace with shared files and memory you can correct, and takes work from Slack, Telegram, Discord, and email.
