Construct gives you an AI employee that does the work a chat assistant describes: from $9 a month it reads your apps, drafts and sends from its own inbox, saves the finished files, and runs the same job again next week from its Calendar. A chat assistant hands the result back to you instead: you gather the data, you do the sending, and next month the exchange starts over in a new thread.

For founders and small teams with recurring work, Construct is the better tool. It executes across a live browser, a sandbox terminal, workspace files, and connected apps; it keeps files, procedures, and memory you can correct in one workspace; and it leaves a record of what it did. Keep your chat assistant for thinking out loud, and give Construct the jobs that have to get done.

Capabilities vary by product and plan. OpenAI documents ChatGPT's available tools in its [capabilities overview](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9260256-chatgpt-capabilities-overview), and Anthropic describes Claude's product surfaces in [Getting started with Claude](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8114491-getting-started-with-claude). This article compares the workspace model, not every feature of every chat product.

## Why do this work in Construct?

- **It finishes the job, not just the draft.** Construct gathers the data through connected apps or a live browser, writes the result, and sends it or holds it for your review.
- **Work lands in files, not a thread.** Reports and spreadsheets are saved to workspace files as they are produced, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 behind.
- **It runs again on its own.** Calendar schedules run server-side with your laptop closed: 3 scheduled tasks on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Construct remembers your audience and format between jobs, and you can inspect, correct, or forget any stored fact.
- **One workspace for the team, no seats.** Invite teammates as Owner, Admin, or Member; a team workspace has no member limit and draws on the owner's plan.
- **It answers where you already talk.** Web, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and its own email address.

## The execution gap

A chat assistant can explain how to update a spreadsheet, prepare a report, and email a summary to the team. Construct uses live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, workspace files, and connected applications to perform those steps and returns with the finished artifact, chat tool records, and Activity summaries.

That gap shows up in three places:

1. **Unified persistence**: chat products may offer history, projects, files, or memory depending on the product and plan; Construct keeps files, procedures, schedules, and inspectable memory in one workspace.
2. **Execution surfaces**: available chat tools vary by product and plan, while Construct combines connected apps with live browser runs, a sandbox terminal, native email, and workspace files in the same task.
3. **Inspectable work**: Construct keeps Activity summaries, chat tool records, sent messages, connected-app updates, and workspace files together after the job is done.

## The same request, handled two ways

Take a request that sounds simple: "Check whether any of our top ten accounts have gone quiet this month, and draft follow-ups for the ones that have."

**In Construct.** The agent reads the accounts through a connected-app action, compares against the figures saved in workspace files from last month, drafts the follow-ups, and either sends them from its own inbox or leaves them for review. The files, the sent messages, and the Activity summary stay in the workspace. Next month, the same job runs from the Calendar.

**In a chat assistant.** You export account activity yourself, paste it in, and get back an analysis and draft follow-ups. You did the data gathering, you will do the sending, and next month the whole exchange starts over from an empty thread.

The drafted text might be comparable. The difference is how much of the surrounding work a person still has to do, and whether anything is left behind that makes the next run cheaper.

## A workspace changes the relationship

A chat thread is a good place to discuss work. A persistent workspace is where the rest of the job lives. Construct brings Files, Memories, Calendar items, and Workflows together with live Browser runs, a sandbox Terminal, native Email, connected apps, and Activity summaries in one web desktop.

That continuity matters for recurring operations. A weekly research report reuses a saved workflow and remembers the intended audience. An inbox task can refer to earlier customer context. A file-analysis job saves the transformed output beside the original instead of losing it when the conversation closes.

## Repeating work without rebuilding it

Construct's native Calendar schedules one-time or recurring agent jobs and workflow runs, and they run server-side whether or not anyone is online. A workflow can combine agent reasoning, connected-app actions, and in-app notifications, then keep run history for inspection. Workflows run as a straight sequence of steps, so put any decision inside an agent step, where the agent reads the situation and chooses what to do.

## Control without constant monitoring

Construct is built for supervised execution. You can inspect outputs, interrupt a running turn, answer a question when the agent needs a decision, and review or correct long-term memory. Activity records each action with a short reason, and tool inputs and outputs remain in chat. Construct's workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

Large jobs can also be split into bounded parallel subtasks. Plans allow 2, 4, or 8 temporary-agent jobs to run concurrently; additional jobs queue, and browser or terminal work may still contend for shared execution surfaces.

## When might a chat assistant still fit?

- Thinking through a problem before any work begins.
- One-shot drafting, such as rewriting a paragraph or summarizing a pasted document.
- Questions with no side effects, where nothing needs to be executed or saved.

For work that has to execute, recur, and leave a record, Construct is the better fit, and the two run side by side.
