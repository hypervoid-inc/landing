**Construct gives you a cloud AI employee with its own workspace, files, schedules, and email address for $9 a month, on a plan whose limits are published up front.** Perplexity Computer launched on Perplexity Max at about $200 a month and bills every task in credits on top, at $1 per 100, and those credits are personal to one account.

For founders and small teams, Construct is the better Perplexity Computer alternative. It costs a set monthly plan price with published limits instead of a credit bill that varies by task, every finished report lands in workspace files your team can open, scheduled jobs run on its Calendar with your laptop closed, and one workspace serves the whole team with no per-seat fee. Below, we compare Construct with seven other options under $200 and with staying on Perplexity Pro plus credits.

## Why choose Construct over Perplexity Computer?

- **$9 a month instead of about $200.** Construct Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address. Starter at $59 and Pro at $299 add usage, agents, and schedules.
- **A cost you can budget.** Perplexity says its credit ranges "are examples, not guarantees," and a scheduled run is skipped if you are out of credits when it starts. Construct's plans publish their usage, steps per task, and scheduled tasks up front, so you know what a month includes before you start.
- **Finished work saved as files.** Reports, CSVs, and scripts land in the workspace as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still keeps steps 1 to 29, and next week's run can compare against this week's file.
- **One agent for the whole team.** Perplexity credits "are personal to your account." A Construct team workspace has Owner, Admin, and Member roles, no member limit, and usage that draws on the owner's plan.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or delete it, and every later run uses the correction.
- **Your own model keys on Pro.** Construct Pro can run on your keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI.

## What is Perplexity Computer, and what does it cost?

Perplexity describes Computer as "an independent digital worker that completes tasks and workflows for you." It works in a "Personal Cloud Sandbox" with persistent memory, can run on a schedule, and connects to apps such as Gmail, Outlook, GitHub, Linear, Slack, Notion, and Salesforce ([Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13837784-what-is-computer)). It launched in February 2026 on Perplexity Max at "$200/month," using 19 AI models ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/perplexitys-new-computer-is-another-bet-that-users-need-many-ai-models)).

### How Perplexity Computer credits work

Computer is billed in credits on top of the subscription ([Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13838041-how-credits-work-on-perplexity)):

- **The rate.** "100 credits = $1." Typical tasks use 100 to 350 credits for light work, 350 to 950 for multi-step reports, 875 to 2,275 for heavy documents, and 2,400 to 9,800 for large projects.
- **What each plan includes.** Pro has "No monthly allocation," with a one-time 4,000-credit bonus that expires in 30 days. Max includes 10,000 credits a month, plus a one-time 35,000-credit bonus.
- **Running out.** "Active tasks pause. They do not cancel." New tasks are blocked until you buy credits or your monthly credits reset, and unused monthly credits do not roll over.
- **Spending caps.** Max accounts have a monthly cap on credit purchases: "The default is $200," adjustable up to $5,000.
- **No refunds for expensive runs.** "Credits that cost more than you expected for a task that ran as designed" are not refunded.

Scheduled tasks use credits on every run, can repeat no more often than once an hour, and if you are out of credits at run time "that run is skipped" ([Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11521526-perplexity-tasks)).

## 2. Manus

**Best for:** one-off research, slides, and web builds handed over a task at a time.

Manus runs each task in a sandbox. Manus 2.0, announced September 28, 2026, added Automations that start when something happens in a connected service, and a Cloud Computer, a persistent virtual machine for apps and scripts that run around the clock ([Manus](https://manus.im/blog/introducing-manus-2-0), [Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392111-what-is-the-cloud-computer)).

**Price:** Free has 300 daily credits, 1 concurrent task, and 2 scheduled tasks. Pro starts at $20 a month for 4,000 credits and 20 scheduled tasks ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/11711111-what-is-the-current-membership-pricing-for-manus)). The Cloud Computer is $30 or $50 a month on top. Monthly credits do not roll over, and if you stop paying for the Cloud Computer, its "working files, installed tools, and databases" are deleted ([Manus Help Center](https://help.manus.im/en/articles/15392078-understanding-cloud-computer-plans-and-billing)).

## 3. Claude Cowork

**Best for:** people already paying for Claude.

Cowork scheduled tasks are on all paid Claude plans and run "even when your computer is asleep or the Claude Desktop app is closed," with hourly, daily, weekly, or weekday cadences ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)). A task that needs local files or apps "will only run locally."

**Price:** Claude Pro is $20 a month, Max starts at $100, and Team standard seats are $25 a month ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). Our [Claude Cowork alternatives](/blog/claude-cowork-alternatives/) guide goes further.

## 4. Gemini Spark

**Best for:** personal Google accounts in a supported country.

Spark is Google's background agent for Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, and Sheets, with time-based or conditional triggers ([Gemini Spark](https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/)). It is included with Google AI Pro and Ultra ([Google AI plans](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/)); Chrome Unboxed reported on July 27, 2026 that it reached the "$20-a-month Google AI Pro tier in the US." That rollout "excludes the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, and Nigeria," and work and school accounts were still excluded ([Chrome Unboxed](https://chromeunboxed.com/googles-agentic-ai-gemini-spark-is-now-available-on-the-20-pro-tier-in-the-us/)). See our [Gemini Spark alternatives](/blog/gemini-spark-alternatives/).

## 5. Grok Bot

**Best for:** existing Cursor or SuperGrok subscribers.

"Grok Bot access is included on every paid individual Cursor plan and on Cursor Teams," with usage that "resets weekly" ([Cursor Help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)). Cursor's individual plan is $20 a month ([Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)). Usage tiers are not published as numbers, and a Cursor plan and a SuperGrok link "do not stack." Our [Grok Bot comparison](/blog/grokbot-alternative/) goes deeper.

## 6. Viktor

**Best for:** a team that wants one coworker inside Slack or Microsoft Teams.

Viktor works in Slack and Microsoft Teams with its own cloud computer ([Viktor](https://viktor.com/)). Team plans start at $50 a month for 20,000 credits per workspace, and unused monthly credits roll over for one month ([Viktor pricing](https://viktor.com/pricing)). See [Construct vs Viktor](/blog/construct-vs-viktor/).

## 7. Lindy

**Best for:** per-person inbox and meeting help in Slack.

Plus is $29.99 per user a month with 3,000 credits ([Lindy pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing)). The pricing page lists computer use on every plan, while Lindy's plan documentation shows it from Pro upward, so confirm before choosing Plus ([Lindy docs](https://docs.lindy.ai/pricing)).

## 8. Perplexity Pro with purchased credits

**Best for:** occasional Computer tasks inside Perplexity.

If Perplexity's help center is right that Pro includes Computer access, you pay for Pro, use the one-time 4,000-credit bonus in the first 30 days, then buy credits at $1 per 100 or turn on auto-refill. Purchased credits last a year if unused ([Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/13838041-how-credits-work-on-perplexity)). You give up Max's 10,000 monthly credits and Brain, the memory system in research preview for Max subscribers ([Perplexity Max](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11680686-perplexity-max)).

Perplexity's desktop app, Personal Computer, can also run some work on your own Mac or PC; on supported hardware, "Work the local model handles does not consume Computer credits," while cloud steps still do ([Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/14659663-what-is-personal-computer)).

## What does a weekly research report cost on each?

This is an illustrative calculation from published prices and Perplexity's typical credit ranges, not a measured test.

Say you want a weekly competitor report: check five pricing pages and two news sources, compare with last week, and save a short brief. Perplexity's credits article puts "Build a competitive landscape report with pricing and feature comparisons" in its Complex band, 350 to 950 credits.

- **Construct Lite:** $9 a month, using one of Lite's 3 scheduled tasks, with the CSV and brief saved in Files each week. If the job needs more than 50 steps per run, Starter at $59 gives 150.
- **Perplexity Pro plus credits:** about 4.3 runs a month at $3.50 to $9.50 each is $15 to $41 in credits, plus the Pro subscription, so roughly $35 to $61 a month, varying with each run.
- **Perplexity Max:** about $200 a month, with 10,000 credits included.
- **Manus Pro:** $20 a month for 4,000 credits; Manus does not publish per-task ranges, so test a run first.
- **Claude Pro:** $20 a month, within plan usage, as a Cowork scheduled task.

The deciding questions are whether you know the monthly cost before you start and whether next week's run can see this week's file. Construct answers both for $9.

## Already using Perplexity Computer? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to cancel Perplexity to try this. Keep it for ad hoc questions and move the one recurring job that eats the most credits.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** if you want longer runs or your own model keys.
2. **Copy the task instructions** you gave Computer into a Construct chat, and save the source list or last report in the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the file** it saves in Files.
5. **Save it as a workflow and put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week with your laptop closed.

There is no import: Perplexity's tasks and memory do not transfer. Write your rules into the first instruction and Construct saves them as memory you can read and correct.

## When might Perplexity Computer still fit?

- Research across many models in one run is the main thing you pay for.
- You run Computer only occasionally and already pay for Perplexity Pro.
- Your team works in Microsoft Teams.

For recurring work that needs files, schedules, a shared workspace, and a price a small business can plan around, Construct is the better fit.

## Which Perplexity Computer alternative should you choose?

Choose Construct. It starts at $9 instead of about $200, publishes its plan limits up front, saves every finished report as a workspace file, runs on a Calendar with your laptop closed, and serves your whole team from one plan. If you keep Perplexity Pro for quick research, add Construct for the work that repeats.
