### What a human VA costs in 2026

Human VA pricing varies by where the person is based and how the engagement is structured. Hourly rates run $5 to $75 depending on location and skill level, and a part-time arrangement covering daily email and calendar management typically runs $1,000 to $3,000 a month, according to Tendem's breakdown of virtual assistant costs (source: [tendem.ai](https://tendem.ai/blog/virtual-assistant-vs-ai-agent-which-do-you-need)).

Full-time engagements show a wide domestic-versus-offshore split. Cherry Assistant puts an offshore managed agency at $640 to $2,400 a month full-time, against $4,700 to $8,300 a month as the realistic total cost of a US-based in-house assistant once salary, benefits, payroll taxes, and recruiting are included (source: [cherryassistant.com](https://www.cherryassistant.com/blog/how-much-does-a-virtual-assistant-cost)). Stealth Agents' research on Filipino VA agencies lands in a similar range: $700 to $2,500 a month all-in for a managed agency hire, versus $4,800 to $12,000 a month for a comparable US-based hire (source: [stealthagents.com](https://stealthagents.com/research/cost-of-hiring-a-virtual-assistant-in-the-philippines-2026)).

Two more numbers matter beyond the sticker price. A structured offshore executive VA onboarding typically reaches full productivity between day 14 and day 30, and a rushed one can take 60 to 90 days or end in early turnover (source: [outsourcedscale.com](https://www.outsourcedscale.com/blog/how-to-onboard-an-offshore-executive-virtual-assistant-so-they-perform-from-day-one/)). Once someone is up to speed, outsourced VA teams still see about 18% annual turnover, and each replacement costs an estimated $3,600 to $5,400 in lost productivity and rehiring (source: [365outsource.com](https://www.365outsource.com/virtual-assistance/virtual-assistant-retention-hiring-strategy/)).

### Where the gap comes from

Customer-service benchmarks illustrate part of the structural cost difference. Those figures are for customer service, not every VA task, but they show why narrow, repeatable work is where an AI employee pays for itself first.

## Why inspectable work beats a chat-based "AI VA"

A chatbot that drafts a reply for you to copy and send does not itself record what happened in your inbox, calendar, or CRM. Construct does. Activity records each action with a short reason, chat keeps the tool records, and the resulting files and messages stay in the workspace, so you can check a week of delegated work in minutes. Activity covers a limited window and is an operating record rather than a complete audit log, so save anything you need to keep long term as a file.

Construct's memory also carries your preferences from one job to the next, and you can read, correct, or forget any stored fact; see [AI agent memory](/blog/ai-agent-memory/) for how it works. Memory learns from conversations and public web research, so add anything learned from a private file or app explicitly when a future job depends on it.

The category is young, and the right way in is one bounded, recurring job. McKinsey's 2025 State of AI survey found that no more than 10% of organizations report scaling agentic AI within any single business function, while 23% are actively scaling in at least one function and 39% are still experimenting (source: [forbes.com](https://www.forbes.com/sites/josipamajic/2026/03/22/10-of-enterprise-functions-use-ai-agents-mckinsey-finds/)). A small team that hands Construct its weekly inbox review or CRM update today is ahead of most of that curve.

## When might a human VA still fit?

- Customer-facing conversations that turn on tone or empathy. In the cited analysis, 93.4% of US consumers say they prefer a human for customer service work, and human-handled interactions scored 88% satisfaction against 60% for AI-handled ones (source: [stealthagents.com](https://stealthagents.com/research/ai-virtual-assistant-vs-human-virtual-assistant-statistics-2026)).
- Relationship work where the other party wants a person: high-stakes client calls, sensitive HR conversations, negotiations.
- Anything in the physical world, such as in-person coordination. As Oana Manolache, founder and CEO of Sequel.io, put it about the evolving assistant role: "it doesn't replace what an executive assistant does now as the role has evolved" (source: [fortune.com](https://fortune.com/2026/07/05/ai-displacement-executive-assistants-secretaries-skills-adaptation/)).

For the recurring, cross-app work that fills most of a VA's week, inbox review, CRM updates, scheduled research, and status reports, Construct is faster, cheaper, and leaves a record.
