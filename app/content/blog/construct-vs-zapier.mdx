## Why choose Construct over Zapier, Make, or n8n?

- **Describe the outcome, not every step.** Construct plans the browser, app, and file steps at run time, so you are not wiring a branch for every edge case.
- **$9 a month to start.** Lite includes 2 agents, 50 steps per task, 3 scheduled tasks, and an agent email address, at a fixed monthly price with published plan limits.
- **A browser and a terminal, not only connectors.** Construct drives websites with no integration in live browser runs and runs scripts in a sandbox terminal.
- **Work saved as files.** Reports and spreadsheets land in workspace files as they are produced, so next week's run compares against this week's.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Correct a fact once, such as a client's new reporting format, and every later run uses it.
- **Schedules that run without you.** Calendar jobs run on Construct's servers with your laptop closed: 3 on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro.

## Flows vs goals

On an automation platform, the thinking happens at build time: the person wiring the flow decides every path, and the runtime executes that plan. With Construct, the thinking moves to run time. When inbox review needs judgment or research spans several sites, Construct reads the situation, then continues through connected-app actions, live browser work, files, or in-app notifications.

The practical test is an unusual input. A trigger-action flow handles the cases its builder anticipated. Construct attempts to interpret the unusual case, so an item that would otherwise sit unnoticed gets handled or flagged in the run's output.

## A concrete scenario: onboarding a new customer

A deal closes, and the customer needs a welcome email, a project workspace, a kickoff meeting, and an internal handoff note.

**On an automation platform,** a trigger fires on the CRM stage change and action steps create the workspace, send a templated welcome, create the calendar event, and post the handoff. When a deal closes with missing contact details or an unusual plan, a filter or router has to have been built for it.

**With Construct,** a scheduled job checks for newly closed deals. The agent reads the deal record, notices the missing contact detail, checks prior email history to fill it, drafts a welcome that reflects what the customer actually bought, and writes a handoff note in its own words. The files, drafts, and Activity summary stay in the workspace. The variance that would otherwise land on a person's desk is handled before it gets there.

## How do the costs compare?

The two categories meter different things. Automation platforms price on task or operation volume, as [Zapier's pricing](https://zapier.com/pricing) shows. Construct meters depth and capacity: Lite at $9 with up to 50 steps per task, Starter at $59 with up to 150, and Pro at $299 with deep runs of up to 1,000 steps per task.

The useful comparison is cost per completed outcome. A job that takes a person an hour of cross-app judgment every week is where Construct comes out ahead, starting at $9 a month.

## When might Zapier, Make, or n8n still fit?

- High-volume record syncing or notifications with fixed templates.
- Webhook plumbing that needs sub-minute reaction time.
- A procedure that depends on drawn branches, delays, or approval steps today.

For research, inbox triage, recurring reports from several systems, and cross-app work whose steps change week to week, Construct is the better fit.
