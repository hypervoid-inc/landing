Construct gives a small business an always-on AI employee with its own workspace, files, schedules, and email address from $9 a month, and from $59 one plan covers a whole team workspace with no per-seat fee. The four big-lab agents cost a three-person business from $0 (Muse's free tier) to $375 a month (three ChatGPT Business Premium seats at $125), with Claude Team at $75 and Cursor Teams with Grok Bot at $120 in between, and each one requires an account in its maker's ecosystem: Meta, ChatGPT, Claude, or Cursor. For founders and small teams, Construct is the better choice: it works without any of those accounts, keeps every finished file in a workspace the team shares, lets you read and correct what the agent remembers, and its price does not rise with each person you add.

Already paying for one of the four? Keep it for personal work and give Construct the recurring jobs your team depends on. The comparison of all four, with sources, follows below.

## Why choose Construct instead of these four?

- **One plan for the whole team, not one seat per person.** A Construct team workspace, available on every plan, has Owner, Admin, and Member roles, no member limit, and everyone's work draws on the owner's plan. The same three people pay $75 to $375 a month on the per-seat plans below.
- **No ecosystem to join first.** You sign up for Construct directly. You do not need a Meta account, a ChatGPT Pro seat, a Cursor plan, or a paid Claude plan.
- **Work that outlives the chat.** Reports, spreadsheets, and scripts land in workspace files as the agent produces them, so a run that stops at step 30 still leaves steps 1 to 29 behind, and next week's run starts from last week's file.
- **Recurring jobs on a Calendar.** Save a job as a workflow and schedule it. Calendar runs happen on Construct's servers with your laptop closed: 3 scheduled tasks on Lite, 10 on Starter, 50 on Pro.
- **Memory you can read and fix.** Open what Construct has learned, correct a fact, or delete it before the next run relies on it.
- **It answers where your team already talks.** The web app, Slack, Telegram, Discord, and its own email inbox, with the same workspace behind every channel.

## ChatGPT Dots

A dot is an agent inside ChatGPT that runs on GPT-6 Astra with its own cloud computer and browser and connects to more than 4,000 apps through plugins. You reach it in ChatGPT on desktop, web, and mobile, or in Slack and Microsoft Teams, with texting listed as coming soon ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)). When you are away it does "proactive research" with read-only app access.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** the first dot is included with ChatGPT Pro or a Business Premium seat. [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2272332/openai-devday-2026-announcements/) reports dots are "only available on ChatGPT's Pro 200 and Business Premium plans," while [Layer3 Labs](https://www.layer3labs.io/guides/chatgpt-pricing) says Pro and Business Premium include one dot and lists Business Premium at $125 per seat monthly or $100 billed annually. Usage does not count against plan allowances for the first month, and the price of extra dots has not been disclosed ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-always-on-ai-agents-in-chatgpt-with-their-own-cloud-computers/)). {/* TODO: verify Pro tier eligibility and the Business Premium seat price on openai.com (403 on October 1). */}

**Watch for:** the highest entry price of the four, a single model, and usage terms after the first month that OpenAI has not published. OpenAI itself advises reviewing consequential work, according to The Next Web. Our [Construct vs ChatGPT Dots](/blog/construct-vs-openai-dots/) comparison goes deeper.

## Muse for Small Business

Muse runs on a dedicated "Muse Secure VM," with a separate Sentinel agent that controls connector actions and network traffic ([Meta](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse)), and Meta says it keeps working after people close the app ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)). The business version connects to Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Granola, HighLevel, QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Lovable, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe, and Zoom, plus Instagram, Facebook Pages, and Meta ad accounts ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/)).

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** free with a usage limit, with Power at $20 a month (500M Muse tokens per week) and Maximum at $100 (3B tokens per week) ([Meta Help Center](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)). DataCamp reports a payment card is required even on the free tier ([DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/muse-agent)).

**Watch for:** it is a personal agent on one Meta account, available in the US and Canada only. The pages we read mention no way to add staff. Our [Construct vs Muse](/blog/construct-vs-muse/) comparison covers the differences in detail.

## Grok Bot

Grok Bot gives you named Bots on "a persistent cloud computer with a browser, filesystem, and terminal," and work continues when you close the app or the laptop ([Grok Bot docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview)). A Bot can save a path you walk it through as a skill and rerun it on a schedule. It runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Cursor lists "Grok Bot access" on its Individual plan at $20 a month and on Teams at $40 per user ([Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)). Every self-serve Teams member gets access, and SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy, and X Premium+ accounts get linked usage ([Cursor help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)). Usage resets weekly. {/* TODO: verify SuperGrok monthly price; x.ai/bot returned 403 on October 1. */}

**Watch for:** all your Bots share one computer, and xAI says each Bot's screen is a separate work surface, "not security boundaries." Do not put a credential on it that another Bot should not use ([Grok Bot computer and apps](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/computer-and-apps)). Cursor manages model selection; there is no model picker. More in our [Grok Bot comparison](/blog/grokbot-alternative/).

## Claude Cowork

Cowork is Claude's mode for multi-step work. Since July 7, 2026, it has also run on the web and mobile ([Automaton Agency](https://automatonagency.com/insights/claude-cowork-mobile-and-web)). Tasks run in the cloud in beta, sessions keep going when you close the app, and from October 6, 2026, new Cowork tasks run only in the cloud ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)). On September 16, Anthropic merged Cowork and chat into one Claude, starting with Pro and Max ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude)). Scheduled tasks run hourly, daily, weekly, or on weekdays, even when your computer is asleep ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)).

[Claude for Small Business](https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-small-business-launches-new-workflows-integrations-and-training-programs) adds 27 integrations, including QuickBooks, Shopify, Square, Stripe, HubSpot, and Xero, and 43 ready-made workflows such as a Monday brief and month-end close, on every paid plan.

**Price, checked October 1, 2026:** Pro is $20 a month ($17 billed annually), Max starts at $100, Team Standard is $25 per seat monthly and Team Premium $125, with a two-seat minimum ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)).

**Watch for:** agentic work draws on the same usage limits as chat, work that needs local files still runs through the desktop app, and every teammate needs a seat. ## The small-business lens

### Which account do you need?

Each of the four sits on top of an account you may or may not have: Muse needs a Meta account, Dots needs ChatGPT Pro or Business Premium, Grok Bot needs Cursor or an eligible xAI subscription, and Cowork needs a paid Claude plan. If your business runs on none of them, picking one means choosing an ecosystem, not just an agent. Construct needs only its own sign-up, and Pro lets you bring keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI if you want a particular lab's models.

### Is it available where you are?

Muse for Small Business is US and Canada only. Dots on Pro excludes the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK, though Business Premium works in every supported ChatGPT region ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)). Anthropic lists the UK, EU countries, Switzerland, and Canada as supported for Claude ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries)). The Grok Bot documentation we read does not mention regions.

### What data can it reach, and who else sees it?

- **Construct** does not train on your data. Memory is visible and editable, and Activity records each action, what it touched, and why for the last 30 days.
- **Muse** keeps your data and credentials in your VM and says it does not share conversations or VM data with Meta's ad systems. You can opt out of training with a switch in settings.
- **Dots** connect through plugins; background research is read-only.
- **Grok Bot** shares files, browser logins, and app logins across all of your Bots.
- **Claude** uses your personal connectors in Cowork. On consumer plans, Anthropic uses your chats to improve its models only if you choose to allow it ([Anthropic privacy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training)). On Team plans, [Claude Tag](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/overview) works in Slack channels with service accounts an admin sets per channel.

### What needs your approval?

Muse says nothing publishes, sends, or spends without your approval ([Muse](https://muse.ai/business)). Dots let you write Custom Rules to allow, block, or require approval for specific actions. Grok Bot uses Auto Review rules that Enterprise admins can enforce ([Grok Bot settings](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/settings-and-notifications)). Cowork offers Manual, Auto, and Skip modes, and on Team plans owners decide whether members can let Google actions run without asking ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10166901-use-google-workspace-connectors)).

In Construct, set this up deliberately: its workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them.

## An illustrative example: a three-person design studio

This is an illustrative scenario, not a customer case study or a measured test.

A three-person studio runs on Google Workspace, keeps clients in HubSpot, and posts work on Instagram. It wants a Monday summary of open client emails, unpaid invoices, and last week's Instagram results.

- **Construct** on Starter at $59 connects Gmail and HubSpot from the integration catalog, writes the summary to a dated file in the team workspace, and runs every Monday from the Calendar. All three partners open the same file, and the bill is the same for three people as it would be for six.
- **Muse** covers the Instagram part and is available in Canada, but each partner would have a separate agent.
- **Dots** would cost $375 a month on Business Premium.
- **Grok Bot** costs $120 a month on Cursor Teams unless someone already pays for Cursor.
- **Claude Team** at $75 a month connects Gmail and HubSpot and schedules the summary in Cowork; putting Claude in the studio's Slack through Claude Tag adds usage-based charges for channel work.

## Already using one of these? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to cancel anything. Move the one recurring job your current agent handles least reliably, or the one your teammates need to see.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the instructions** you gave your current agent into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

There is no import from Dots, Muse, Grok Bot, or Claude: write your rules into the first instruction, and Construct saves them as memory you can read and correct.

## When might one of these still fit?

- You sell and advertise on Instagram and Facebook in the US or Canada and want an agent that reads your Meta ad accounts directly (Muse).
- Your company already pays for ChatGPT Business Premium and needs custom approval rules on every account action today (Dots).
- One person already pays for Claude Pro or Cursor and only wants a personal assistant (Cowork or Grok Bot).

For a team's recurring work that needs shared files, schedules, and one price for everyone, Construct is the better fit.
