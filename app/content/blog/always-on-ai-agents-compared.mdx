## ChatGPT Dots

Dots are OpenAI's always-on agents inside ChatGPT: the first dot comes with a ChatGPT Pro plan (from $100 a month) or a Business Premium seat, it runs only on GPT-6 Astra, and Pro subscribers in the UK, the EEA, and Switzerland do not get one.

Each dot has its own cloud computer and browser and connects to more than 4,000 apps through plugins. You reach it in ChatGPT on desktop, web, and mobile, or in Slack and Microsoft Teams, with texting listed as coming later ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)). When you are away it does "proactive research" with read-only app access, so it cannot send messages or change content in that time.

**Price, checked October 11, 2026:** The Next Web says dots are included in Pro and Business Premium plans. [Carly](https://www.usecarly.com/blog/chatgpt-dots/) and [eesel](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/openai-dots-pricing) list all three Pro tiers, at $100, $200, and $500 a month, while [BGR](https://www.bgr.com/2272332/openai-devday-2026-announcements/) reported at launch that dots "are only available on ChatGPT's Pro 200 and Business Premium plans." eesel, citing OpenAI's business pricing page, lists Business Premium at $125 per seat monthly or $100 billed annually. Talking to a dot does not count against plan limits, but tasks it starts in ChatGPT Work or Codex do, and from October 30, 2026, Pro 200's included Work and Codex usage halves to 10 times Plus at the same $200 ([Beam](https://beam.ai/agentic-insights/openai-dots-usage-limits-pro-200)). The price of extra dots has not been disclosed ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-always-on-ai-agents-in-chatgpt-with-their-own-cloud-computers/)).

**Watch for:** the highest team price of the four, a single model, and usage terms after the launch month that OpenAI has not published. OpenAI itself warns that "Dots can still make mistakes, so always review consequential work," according to The Next Web. Our [Construct vs ChatGPT Dots](/blog/construct-vs-openai-dots/) comparison goes deeper, and our [ChatGPT Dots alternatives](/blog/chatgpt-dots-alternatives/) list covers options that do not need ChatGPT Pro.

## Muse for Small Business

Muse is Meta's personal AI agent, free to start with Power at $20 and Max at $100 a month, and it is available only to adults in the US and Canada.

Muse runs on a dedicated "Muse Secure VM," where a separate service called Sentinel is "the sole permission authority for connector actions and network egress" ([Meta](https://research.meta.ai/blog/security-and-safety-for-ai-agents-our-approach-with-muse)), and Meta says it keeps working after people close the app ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-personal-ai-agent/)). The business version connects to Asana, Box, Canva, Dropbox, Figma, Granola, HighLevel, QuickBooks, Klaviyo, Lovable, Notion, Shopify, Slack, Stripe, and Zoom, plus Facebook and Instagram business accounts ([Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/introducing-muse-small-business/)). Since October 7, it also has an iPad app ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/07/meta-launches-muse-for-ipad-following-weeks-as-the-top-iphone-app/)).

### Is Muse free?

Yes, with a usage limit Meta does not publish in numbers. Power is $20 a month for 500M Muse tokens per week and Max is $100 for 3B tokens per week ([Meta Help Center](https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/)). DataCamp reports that "Muse requires a payment card to get started, even on the free tier" ([DataCamp](https://www.datacamp.com/blog/muse-agent)).

**Watch for:** it is a personal agent on one Meta account, available "to people in the US and Canada who are 18 and over" ([Muse](https://muse.ai/business)). The pages we read mention no way to add staff, roles, or shared access. Our [Construct vs Muse](/blog/construct-vs-muse/) comparison covers the differences in detail.

## Grok Bot

Grok Bot is xAI's always-on agent, launched in beta on August 11, 2026; it has no standalone price and comes with Cursor plans from $20 a month or with eligible xAI subscriptions.

Grok Bot gives you named Bots on "a persistent cloud computer with a browser, filesystem, and terminal," and closing the app, laptop, or phone does not stop a background turn or a routine ([Grok Bot docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/overview)). A Bot can save a path you walk it through as a skill and rerun it on a schedule. It runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, iPhone, iPad, and Android. On October 9, 2026, Bots gained their own email addresses on the @mail.grokbot.com domain, and 9to5Mac reports an "intelligence upgrade with the help of Anthropic's Claude" earlier that week ([9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/10/09/grok-bot-just-got-its-own-email-address-heres-how-to-claim-yours)).

### Is Grok Bot free, and how much does it cost?

No. Cursor's free Hobby plan does not list it. Cursor lists "Grok Bot access" on Individual at $20 a month and Teams at $40 per user ([Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)), and every self-serve Teams member gets access ([Cursor help](https://cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)). SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy, and X Premium+ give linked usage, while SuperGrok Lite does not include Grok Bot. Usage resets weekly. xAI's own pricing page refused our requests, so we do not list SuperGrok prices.

**Watch for:** all your Bots share one computer, and xAI says each Bot's screen is a separate work surface, "not separate security boundaries." Browser sessions and files are shared, so "Do not place a credential or file on it if another Bot on your account should not be able to use it" ([Grok Bot computer and apps](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/computer-and-apps)). "Cursor manages model selection, so there is no model picker" ([Grok Bot settings](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/settings-and-notifications)). More in our [Grok Bot comparison](/blog/grokbot-alternative/).

## Claude Cowork

Claude Cowork is Anthropic's mode for multi-step work, included with every paid Claude plan from Pro at $20 a month, with Team seats at $25 for small businesses.

Cowork went to the web and mobile on July 7, 2026 ([Automaton Agency](https://automatonagency.com/insights/claude-cowork-mobile-and-web)). Cloud sessions keep running when the desktop app is closed or your computer is asleep, though cloud tasks are still in beta on Team and Enterprise plans and work that needs local files runs through the desktop app ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-get-started-with-claude-cowork)). On October 6, 2026, new Cowork tasks on Pro and Max moved to the cloud ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15520349-use-claude-cowork-on-web-desktop-and-mobile)). On September 16, Anthropic merged Cowork and chat into one Claude, starting with Pro and Max ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude)). Scheduled tasks run hourly, daily, weekly, or on weekdays, even when your computer is asleep, unless they need local files or apps ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13854387-schedule-recurring-tasks-in-claude-cowork)).

### How much does Claude Cowork cost for a small business?

Pro is $20 a month ($17 billed annually), Max starts at $100, and Team is for 2 to 150 people, with Standard seats at $25 monthly ($20 annually) and Premium at $125 ($100 annually) ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). [Claude for Small Business](https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-small-business-launches-new-workflows-integrations-and-training-programs), on every paid plan, adds 43 workflows such as a Monday brief and month-end close, and 27 new integrations including Shopify, Xero, Gusto, Square, and Stripe. Anthropic recommends Team for businesses with more than one person.

**Watch for:** multi-step tasks use more of your usage than a quick question, every teammate needs a seat, and putting Claude in Slack through [Claude Tag](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/overview) on Team bills channel work by usage on top of seats.

## Grok Bot vs Claude Cowork vs Dots vs Muse: the small-business lens

The choice between the four comes down to which account your business already has, where you operate, who can see the agent's data, and what it does before it acts.

### Which account do you need?

Muse needs a Meta account, Dots need ChatGPT Pro or Business Premium, Grok Bot needs Cursor or an eligible xAI subscription, and Cowork needs a paid Claude plan. If your business runs on none of them, picking one means choosing an ecosystem, not just an agent. Construct needs only its own sign-up, and Pro lets you bring keys for OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Amazon Bedrock, or xAI if you want a particular lab's models.

### Which always-on agents work in the UK and EU?

Claude does, and so do Dots on Business Premium; Muse and Dots on Pro do not. Muse for Small Business is US and Canada only. Dots on Pro exclude the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK, though Business Premium works in every supported ChatGPT region ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/openai-dots-always-on-ai-agents-cloud-computers-devday)). Anthropic lists the UK, all EU countries, Switzerland, and Canada as supported ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/supported-countries)). The Grok Bot documentation lists no regions, beyond saying that tagging @bot on X "is not available in every region yet."

### What data can it reach, and who else sees it?

- **Construct** does not train on your data. Memory is visible and editable, and Activity records each action, what it touched, and why for the last 30 days.
- **Muse** keeps your data and credentials in your VM and says it "doesn't share a person's conversations or the data in their VM with Meta's ad systems." You can opt out of your interactions being used to train Meta's models.
- **Dots** connect through plugins; background research is read-only.
- **Grok Bot** shares files, browser logins, and app logins across all of your Bots.
- **Claude** uses your personal connectors in Cowork. On consumer plans, Anthropic trains on your chats if you choose to allow it, if a conversation is flagged for safety review, or if you otherwise opt in ([Anthropic privacy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training)). Anthropic says it does not train on business data on Team and Enterprise plans by default ([Claude blog](https://claude.com/blog/claude-for-small-business-launches-new-workflows-integrations-and-training-programs)).

### What needs your approval?

Muse says "nothing publishes, sends or spends without your approval" ([Muse](https://muse.ai/business)). Dots let you write Custom Rules to allow, block, or require approval for specific actions. Grok Bot uses Auto Review rules that an admin can enforce for the team ([Grok Bot settings](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/settings-and-notifications)). Claude for Small Business waits for approval "before anything sends, posts, or pays" by default, and on Team plans owners decide whether members can let Google actions such as sending Gmail run without asking ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/10166901-use-google-workspace-connectors)).

In Construct, set this up deliberately: its workflows do not pause for approval yet, so for anything customer-facing, ask for drafts and send them yourself once you have checked them. Our guide to [letting an AI agent use your Gmail safely](/blog/ai-agent-gmail-access-safely/) shows the setup.

## An illustrative example: a three-person design studio

This is an illustrative scenario, not a customer case study or a measured test.

A three-person studio runs on Google Workspace, keeps clients in HubSpot, and posts work on Instagram. It wants a Monday summary of open client emails, unpaid invoices, and last week's Instagram results.

- **Construct** on Starter at $59 connects Gmail and HubSpot from the integration catalog, writes the summary to a dated file in the team workspace, and runs every Monday from the Calendar. All three partners open the same file, and the bill is the same for three people as it would be for six.
- **Muse** covers the Instagram part and is available in Canada, but each partner would have a separate agent.
- **Dots** would cost $375 a month on Business Premium.
- **Grok Bot** costs $120 a month on Cursor Teams unless someone already pays for Cursor.
- **Claude Team** at $75 a month can schedule the summary in Cowork; putting Claude in the studio's Slack through Claude Tag adds usage-based charges for channel work.

### How to test this yourself

Run the same Monday summary on each agent you are considering, with the same test inbox and CRM, for two weeks. Record:

1. Minutes from sign-up to the first usable summary.
2. How many open client emails and unpaid invoices it caught, out of the ones you know about.
3. Every approval prompt, and anything it sent or changed that you did not ask for.
4. Where the summary was saved, and whether your teammates could open it without their own seat.
5. What the two weeks cost, including any usage beyond the plan.

## Already using one of these? Add Construct in 15 minutes

You do not have to cancel anything. Move the one recurring job your current agent handles least reliably, or the one your teammates need to see.

1. **Start on Lite for $9, or take the 7-day Pro trial** to test your own model keys and longer runs.
2. **Copy the instructions** you gave your current agent into a Construct chat, and move the source files into the workspace.
3. **Connect the apps** the job needs from the integration catalog.
4. **Run it once and check the result** in Files.
5. **Put it on the Calendar** so it runs every week whether or not anyone is online.

## When might one of these still fit?

- You sell and advertise on Instagram and Facebook in the US or Canada and want an agent that reads your Meta business accounts directly (Muse).
- Your company already pays for ChatGPT Business Premium and needs custom approval rules on every account action today (Dots).
- One person already pays for Claude Pro or Cursor and only wants a personal assistant (Cowork or Grok Bot).

For a team's recurring work that needs shared files, schedules, and one price for everyone, Construct is the better fit.
